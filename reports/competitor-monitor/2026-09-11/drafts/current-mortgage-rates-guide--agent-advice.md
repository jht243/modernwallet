## Look Beyond Preferred Lender Referrals

A preferred lender referral does not guarantee the lowest borrowing cost. The [Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB)](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/owning-a-home/process/explore/contact-multiple-lenders/) advises buyers to shop around for a better deal even when a real estate agent or homebuilder suggests a preferred lender. An agent or builder's familiarity with that lender does not mean it offers the best price available to you.

The CFPB recommends contacting at least three lenders and comparing across different lender types rather than consulting only one bank. Gather quotes from a local bank or credit union, other lenders serving the area, and lenders found online. You can also explore working with a mortgage broker instead of approaching a single lender directly.

Do this comparison before finding a house. The CFPB notes that once a seller accepts a purchase offer, you may have as little as a couple of days to line up financing. Shopping lenders early ensures you have time to evaluate your options rather than scrambling after your offer is accepted.
