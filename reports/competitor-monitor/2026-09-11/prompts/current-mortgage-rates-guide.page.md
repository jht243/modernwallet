# Current Mortgage Rates: How They're Set and How to Get the Best One

The Federal Reserve does not directly set mortgage rates. Instead, they track the 10-year Treasury yield, plus a spread lenders add for risk and profit. In recent years, that spread has been wider than its historical norm of 1.5 to 2 points. This guide explains what actually drives the rate you're quoted, why your credit score and loan type affect it more than timing ever will, and how to estimate your payment, shop, use points, and lock your rate, a concrete process that beats waiting for "the right time" to buy.

## What actually sets your mortgage rate
