## CLOSED FACT LIST — the ONLY facts these edits may state. Anything not on this list, you do not know.

1. The original Bilt Mastercard (the single no-annual-fee card this page currently describes) was discontinued; the last day cardholders could use it was February 6, 2026. [CNBC — Bilt Mastercard transition timeline](https://www.cnbc.com/select/bilt-mastercard-transition-timeline-what-to-know/)
2. Effective February 7, 2026, Bilt replaced it with three cards: the Bilt Blue Card ($0 annual fee), the Bilt Obsidian Card ($95 annual fee), and the Bilt Palladium Card ($495 annual fee). [Bilt — card lineup](https://www.bilt.com/card), [Bilt — Palladium card page](https://www.bilt.com/card/palladium)
3. All three Bilt 2.0 cards let a cardholder pay rent OR a mortgage with no added transaction fee and earn points on that payment; the original Bilt Mastercard covered rent only (mortgage earning came later, as an extension to that one card). [CNBC — Bilt card comparison](https://www.cnbc.com/select/bilt-credit-card-comparison/)
4. None of the three Bilt 2.0 cards charges a foreign transaction fee. [CNBC — Bilt card comparison](https://www.cnbc.com/select/bilt-credit-card-comparison/)
5. Bilt Card 2.0 removed the old rule that required a cardholder to make a minimum number of non-rent purchases each statement period just to earn points on rent at all. Under Card 2.0, how many points a cardholder earns on rent or a mortgage instead depends on which monthly spending tier they reach; falling short of every tier still earns a floor of 250 points rather than zero. [Bilt Rewards Support — Card 2.0 Program Overview](https://support.biltrewards.com/hc/en-us/articles/42897766682381-Bilt-Card-2-0-Program-Overview)
6. Bilt Card 2.0 also removed Card 1.0's 100,000-point annual cap on rent-earned points, so a cardholder's rent-earned points are no longer capped. [Bilt Rewards Support — Card 2.0 Program Overview](https://support.biltrewards.com/hc/en-us/articles/42897766682381-Bilt-Card-2-0-Program-Overview)
7. Every Bilt 2.0 card also earns "Bilt Cash," a second, separate rewards currency from Bilt Points, at 4% back on everyday spending. Bilt Cash expires at the end of each calendar year; up to $100 of an unused Bilt Cash balance automatically rolls over into the next year, and any amount over $100 does not. Bilt Points, by contrast, do not expire unless the account has 18 months of no activity. [Bilt Rewards Support — Bilt Cash](https://support.biltrewards.com/hc/en-us/articles/40132420650509-Bilt-Cash)
