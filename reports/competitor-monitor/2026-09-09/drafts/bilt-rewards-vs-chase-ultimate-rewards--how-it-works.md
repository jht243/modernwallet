## How Bilt Rewards Actually Works

Bilt Rewards lets you earn points on rent or mortgage payments with no added transaction fee across three credit cards. Bilt discontinued the original Bilt Mastercard, and the last day cardholders could use it was February 6, 2026. Effective February 7, 2026, Bilt replaced it with three cards: the Bilt Blue Card ($0 annual fee), the Bilt Obsidian Card ($95 annual fee), and the [Bilt Palladium Card](https://www.bilt.com/card/palladium) ($495 annual fee).

All three cards let you pay rent or a mortgage with no added transaction fee while earning points on the payment. The original Bilt Mastercard covered rent only, though mortgage earning was added later as an extension to that card. None of the three cards charges a foreign transaction fee.

Bilt also removed the old rule that required a minimum number of non-rent purchases each statement period to earn points on rent. Under Card 2.0, point earnings on rent or a mortgage depend on which monthly spending tier you reach, with a 250-point floor if you fall short of every tier. Confirm current per-tier earning rates and fees directly at [bilt.com](https://www.bilt.com/card).
