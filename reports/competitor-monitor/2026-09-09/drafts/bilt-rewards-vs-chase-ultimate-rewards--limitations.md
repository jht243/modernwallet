## Bilt Card 2.0 Limitations to Know

Bilt Card 2.0 determines your housing point earnings through monthly spending tiers and places calendar-year expiration limits on Bilt Cash. Under the [Bilt Card 2.0 Program Overview](https://support.biltrewards.com/hc/en-us/articles/42897766682381-Bilt-Card-2-0-Program-Overview), Bilt removed the old rule requiring a minimum number of non-rent purchases each statement period to earn points on rent. Rent and mortgage earnings now depend on which monthly spending tier you reach, though missing every tier still secures a baseline floor of 250 points.

Tier point-earning rates change over time. Confirm current spending thresholds on [Bilt's card lineup page](https://www.bilt.com/card) before submitting a payment. Card 2.0 lifted an earlier constraint by removing Card 1.0's 100,000-point annual cap on rent-earned points, so your housing rewards are no longer capped.

Every Bilt 2.0 card also earns [Bilt Cash](https://support.biltrewards.com/hc/en-us/articles/40132420650509-Bilt-Cash) at 4% back on everyday spending. This is a separate currency from Bilt Points and expires at the end of each calendar year. Up to $100 of an unused Bilt Cash balance automatically rolls over into the next year, but any amount over $100 does not.

Bilt Points do not expire unless an account has 18 months of no activity. Track your Bilt Cash balance before December 31 each year to avoid forfeiting amounts above the $100 rollover limit.
