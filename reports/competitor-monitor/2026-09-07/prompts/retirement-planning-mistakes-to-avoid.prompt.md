  Source: IRS newsroom, 401(k) limit increases to $24,500 for 2026.
- 2026 IRA contribution limit: $7,500 (all IRAs combined, traditional + Roth).
  Source: IRS newsroom (same release as above covers IRA limits).
- Required minimum distributions (RMDs) from traditional 401(k)/IRA accounts begin at age 73
  under current IRS rules. Source: IRS.
- The Department of Labor's general guideline: most retirees need roughly 70% to 90% of their
  pre-retirement income to maintain their standard of living. Source: U.S. Department of Labor,
  "What You Should Know About Your Retirement Plan."
- The 4% rule: withdrawing about 4% of an initial retirement balance in year one, then adjusting
  for inflation each year, has historically funded a 30-year retirement in most historical market
  periods (Trinity Study finding, already cited on this site's FIRE calculator and retirement

  long savings last, even when the long-run average return looks fine, because withdrawals sell
  shares at depressed prices with no paycheck to replace them. (Already established on this site's
