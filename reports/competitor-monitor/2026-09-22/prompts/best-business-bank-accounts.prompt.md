   - 4% cash back on qualifying purchases through Mastercard Easy Savings.

2. Mercury (https://mercury.com/business-banking)
   - "No monthly fee, no account opening fee, no account minimum, no overdraft fee."
   - "$0 to send money in USD" — free ACH, free domestic wires, free USD international wires.
   - "Up to $5 million" in FDIC insurance "through our partner banks and their sweep networks"
     (described on Mercury's site as "20x the usual coverage"). Mercury itself is a financial
     technology company, not a bank — banking services are provided through Choice Financial
     Group and Column N.A., both FDIC members.
   - Apply online in about 10 minutes.
   - Limitation: Mercury does not accept cash deposits and has no branch access.

3. NBKC (https://www.nbkc.com/business/checking)
   - "$0 required to open," "$0 minimum balance," no monthly account service fee.
   - $0 for returned items, overdrafts/NSFs, online banking, bill pay, mobile check deposits,
     eStatements, and stop payments. $0 for incoming domestic wires.
   - $5 per outgoing domestic wire (incoming is $0). $45 per outgoing or incoming international
     wire. 1% foreign transaction fee.
   - ATM: free at NBKC and MoneyPass network ATMs (90,000+ machines); up to $12 per month
     reimbursed for out-of-network ATM fees.
   - Optional Autobooks invoicing add-on available for $10/month.

4. Axos Bank Basic Business Checking
   (https://www.axosbank.com/business/business-checking-accounts/basic-business-checking)
   - "No monthly maintenance fees." No minimum balance or minimum opening deposit requirement.
   - Unlimited transactions with no transaction fees.
   - Free incoming domestic and international wires. Two reimbursed outgoing domestic wires per
     month, then $15 per domestic outgoing wire / $50 per international outgoing wire.
   - Unlimited domestic ATM fee reimbursement.
   - FDIC insurance up to the standard $250,000, expandable up to $265 million through the
     Insured Cash Sweep (ICS) program.
   - Integrates with QuickBooks.
