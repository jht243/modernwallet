GENERAL MECHANISM FACTS (for the "how same-day funding actually works" section):
   - Same-day funding requires the application to be complete and approved early in the business
     day (OnDeck's own cutoff is 10:30 a.m. ET) — applying in the afternoon commonly pushes
     funding to the next business day regardless of the lender's marketing claim.
   - Wire transfers move faster than ACH transfers but can carry a separate fee; ACH typically
     settles by the next business day even when a lender says "same day."
   - Approval speed and funding speed are two different promises: an instant approval does not
     guarantee same-day disbursement unless the lender funds from its own balance sheet rather
     than a third-party capital source.
   (These are general lending-mechanics facts, not tied to one lender; state them as such.)
