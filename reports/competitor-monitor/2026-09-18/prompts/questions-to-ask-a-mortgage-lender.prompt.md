## CLOSED FACT LIST

Anything not on this list, you do not know. Never invent a specific lender name, a specific
current-year average rate, or a specific dollar closing-cost figure beyond what is listed here.

1. The CFPB requires every mortgage lender to give an applicant a standardized 3-page Loan
   Estimate within 3 business days of a completed application, listing the interest rate, APR,
   estimated monthly payment, and estimated closing costs in the same format across lenders,
   which is what makes offers from different lenders directly comparable.
2. The CFPB recommends asking each lender what loan programs you qualify for (e.g. conventional,
   FHA, VA, USDA) because eligibility, required down payment, and mortgage insurance rules differ
   by program, and also asking whether the quoted rate is fixed or adjustable and whether discount
   points are included in the quoted rate.
3. The CFPB states closing costs typically run about 2% to 5% of the loan amount and lists what
   they include (lender/origination fees, appraisal, title services, recording fees, and more);
   ask each lender for the total estimated closing cost figure on the Loan Estimate, not just the
   headline rate.
4. The CFPB advises asking about the rate lock: how long the locked rate is guaranteed to hold,
   and what happens if closing runs past the lock's expiration (a borrower may have to pay a fee
   to extend the lock or accept a new, possibly higher, rate).
5. The CFPB confirms a real estate agent's preferred or affiliated lender is never a requirement.
   A buyer can ask a lender directly about any business relationship it has with their real estate
   agent or brokerage (a referral arrangement or joint venture), and is always free to compare that
   lender's offer against outside Loan Estimates before choosing.
6. The CFPB recommends requesting Loan Estimates from at least three different lenders on the same
   day, since mortgage rates can move daily, so requesting them close together is what keeps the
   comparison apples-to-apples.

## Section-by-section coverage (minimum)

1. Direct-answer intro: the questions that actually change which mortgage costs you less are
   about the loan program, the total closing costs, and the rate-lock terms, not just the headline
   rate quoted over the phone.
2. Ask what loan programs you qualify for and whether the quote is fixed or adjustable (fact 2).
3. Ask for the full Loan Estimate and compare the total closing costs, not just the rate (fact 1,
   3) — explain what the Loan Estimate is and the 3-business-day requirement.
4. Ask about the rate lock length and what happens if it expires before closing (fact 4).
5. Ask about the lender's relationship to your real estate agent (fact 5) — cover this honestly:
   an affiliated or preferred lender is not mandatory, and comparing outside offers is normal and
   legal.
6. Ask how quickly the lender can close, and get that timeline in writing against your rate lock.
7. Close: get Loan Estimates from at least three lenders on the same day and compare them
   side by side (fact 6), then a concrete next action.
