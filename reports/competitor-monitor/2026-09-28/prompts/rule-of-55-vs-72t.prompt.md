FACTS (closed list; anything not here you do not know):
- Rule of 55: IRS Topic 558 lists an exception to the 10% additional tax for distributions after separation from service in or after the calendar year the employee turns age 55 (age 50 for qualified public safety employees). It applies to the plan of the employer you left, not to IRAs or old employers' plans. Ordinary income tax still applies.
- 72(t) substantially equal periodic payments (SEPP): available from an IRA or a plan at any age. Payments must continue for five years or until age 59½, whichever is later. Changing the payment amount or method before then can trigger the 10% penalty retroactively on all distributions taken before 59½, plus interest. Three IRS-accepted methods: required minimum distribution method, fixed amortization method, fixed annuitization method.
- The 10% additional tax on early distributions is 10%; the age threshold is 59½.
