FACTS (closed list; anything not here you do not know):
- Vesting is your ownership of employer contributions. Your own salary deferrals are always 100% yours.
- Employer matching contributions follow the plan's vesting schedule: a cliff schedule reaches 100% after no more than three years of service; a graded schedule reaches 100% after no more than six years (for example 20% per year starting in year two). Plans may vest faster. Safe harbor 401(k) matches are generally fully vested immediately.
- Unvested money is forfeited when you leave; the plan uses forfeitures to reduce employer contributions or pay plan expenses. Plans generally must fully vest you at normal retirement age or if the plan is terminated.
- Your summary plan description or the plan's recordkeeper site shows your vested balance.
