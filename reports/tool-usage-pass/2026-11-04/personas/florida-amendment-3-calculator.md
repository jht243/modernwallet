## Friction
- "It passed yesterday. Why does the calculator still say 'if Amendment 3 passes'?" The sub-line under the big number contradicts the page's own FAQ. → tool fix in `src/components/FloridaAmendment3Calculator.tsx` (text only): change the bigSub to "{savings2027} in 2027 (approved by voters Nov 3, 2026)". Add one line to the disclaimer: "Approved Nov 3, 2026 with about 62% of the vote; final certified results are pending", linking the WUSF source the page already cites. Same outputs, no math change.
- "My mobile home is assessed at $60,000. You say I pay $96 now and save $96. My TRIM notice says about $280 for county and city." For homes assessed between $25,000 and $76,411 the engine subtracts the flat $51,411 instead of the banded exemption, so it understates today's bill and the savings. → math fix in `src/lib/florida-amendment-3.ts` `computeFlAmendment3`. Reason: under Fla. Const. Art. VII §6(a) and s. 196.031(1)(b), F.S., the first $25,000 applies to value from $0 to $25,000. The additional non-school exemption ($26,411 for 2026 after the CPI indexing from 2024's Amendment 5) applies only to assessed value from $50,000 up to $76,411. Replace `taxable(CURRENT_EXEMPTION)` for today's bill with `assessed − (25,000 + clamp(assessed − 50,000, 0, CURRENT_EXEMPTION − 25,000))`, floored at 0. Keep `CURRENT_EXEMPTION`. 2027 and 2028 are unchanged, because the new exemption is at least the home's value in this range. Worked example: $60,000 at 11.2 mills → exemption 25,000 + 10,000 = $35,000, taxable $25,000, bill today $280.00, 2027 $0, savings $280 (tool today: $96). At $45,000: taxable $20,000, bill $224.00, savings $224 (tool today: $0). Results for homes at or above $76,411 do not change. Sources: https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Constitution#A7S06, http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0100-0199/0196/Sections/0196.031.html, https://www.miamidadepa.gov/pa/exemption/homestead-second.page. Add a regression test with both cases.
- "I moved down from Ohio in March 2027. Do I get this?" The tool has no residency input, while the page text says newcomers wait five years. → tool fix (additive) in `FloridaAmendment3Calculator.tsx` plus an optional field in `src/lib/florida-amendment-3.ts`: a "Florida resident on Dec 31, 2026?" Yes/No toggle, default Yes, which leaves today's result unchanged. No → the 2027 and 2028 bills use the current exemption (no new rate), and a note says "the larger exemption starts in your fifth year of homestead exemption, per the amendment text; counties and cities may shorten this by a two-thirds vote". Source: https://constitutionalinitiatives.dos.fl.gov/Home/InitDetail?account=10&seqnum=110. Nothing new is computed; it reuses `CURRENT_EXEMPTION`.
- "I fat-fingered the millage and got $0 savings, but it still says $98,589 extra exempt. Is something broken?" → tool fix (additive) in `FloridaAmendment3Calculator.tsx`: when `mills === 0`, show a hint in the results ("Enter your non-school millage from your TRIM notice to see dollar savings; 10.5 is the statewide average"). The current outputs stay.

## Monetization
Verdict: no-fit

None has a credentialed author or an established publisher. The established-publisher Florida hits are real-estate licensing exam books (Dearborn 1078847584), which this homeowner would not buy. A general personal-finance book under a property-tax savings number would read as bolted on. Honest non-purchase option: the county property appraiser's homestead page and the TRIM notice. Revisit if Nolo or another established publisher releases a Florida homestead or property-tax title.
