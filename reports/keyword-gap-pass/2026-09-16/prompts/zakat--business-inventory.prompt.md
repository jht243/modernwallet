## CLOSED FACT LIST
Anything not on this list, you do not know.
1. Business trade goods (inventory held for resale) are valued at their current market/resale
   value as of the zakat due date, NOT at what the business originally paid for them (cost basis).
   This is the standard, widely-agreed valuation method for zakat on trade goods (`urud al-tijarah`)
   across the classical schools — restate as general, well-established zakat mechanics consistent
   with the page's existing "assets subject to zakat" FAQ, no new external citation needed for this
   general principle (it does not conflict with or add a number beyond what the page's own existing
   nisab/2.5%-rate mechanics already establish).
2. Only genuine trade inventory counts — goods actually held for resale in the ordinary course of
   business. Equipment, fixtures, and property the business uses to operate (not sell) are treated
   like the personal-use exclusion already on this page (a car you use personally, a home you live
   in), not as zakatable trade goods.
3. This site's calculator does not have a dedicated "business inventory" input field — restate as
   established internal fact: the reader enters their inventory's current resale value as part of
   their zakatable assets total (e.g., alongside cash and investments) the same way they would any
   other zakatable asset category.
