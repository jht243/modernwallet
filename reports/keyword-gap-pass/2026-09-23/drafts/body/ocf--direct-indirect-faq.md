This calculator uses the indirect method, which begins with net income, adds back non-cash charges like depreciation, and adjusts for balance sheet changes in working capital. The direct method instead lists actual cash collections from customers and subtracts cash paid directly to suppliers and employees line by line. Under Financial Accounting Standards Board (FASB) Accounting Standards Codification (ASC) 230, both methods must arrive at the identical total operating cash flow figure and differ only in how they present the underlying data.
