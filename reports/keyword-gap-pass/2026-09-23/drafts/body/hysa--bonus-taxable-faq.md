Bank sign-up bonuses are generally worth taking because they provide extra cash for meeting straightforward requirements like a minimum deposit or a qualifying direct deposit, but the payout is taxable. The Internal Revenue Service (IRS) treats cash account-opening bonuses the same way it treats interest income. Depending on the institution's reporting practices, the bank typically reports the bonus on Form 1099-INT or Form 1099-MISC, and the money is taxed as ordinary income in the year received.
