Run the calculator once for each child using their specific age, balance, and target, because differing timelines until college mean no single combined number works across different ages. To project a one-time lump sum without further additions, enter the lump sum as the current balance and set the monthly contribution to $0. Any non-qualified withdrawal triggers ordinary income tax plus a 10% penalty on the earnings portion only, whereas your original contributions return tax-free and penalty-free. Read our guide on [what counts as a qualified withdrawal](/guides/529-qualified-expenses/) to check which education expenses avoid those penalties.
