Yes, you can calculate the cash conversion cycle (CCC) in Excel, though this calculator page already does this calculation for free online. To build your own spreadsheet, use three formulas. DIO equals average inventory divided by COGS, times the period days. DSO equals average receivables divided by revenue, times the period days. DPO equals average payables divided by COGS, times the period days. CCC equals DIO plus DSO minus DPO. In our 365-day worked example, $90,000 average inventory on $600,000 cost of goods sold (COGS) produces 54.8 days DIO, and $110,000 average receivables on $1,000,000 revenue produces 40.2 days DSO. Subtracting 42.6 days DPO ($70,000 average payables on $600,000 COGS) from that subtotal yields the final CCC of 52.4 days.
