The main disadvantage of a high-yield savings account (HYSA) compared to a regular savings account is that most HYSAs operate through online-only banks without physical branch access for in-person service. The higher annual percentage yield (APY) is also variable, meaning the institution can lower your rate at any time. Otherwise, both account types share identical mechanics, including Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC) coverage and the same everyday liquidity rules, making the higher yield the only substantive difference.
