## CLOSED FACT LIST
- Operating cash flow ratio = operating cash flow ÷ current liabilities. It measures whether a
  company's cash from operations alone is sufficient to cover its short-term obligations, higher
  than 1.0 generally meaning operating cash flow alone covers current liabilities.
- Operating cash flow margin = operating cash flow ÷ revenue, expressed as a percentage. It
  measures what share of every revenue dollar converts into operating cash.
