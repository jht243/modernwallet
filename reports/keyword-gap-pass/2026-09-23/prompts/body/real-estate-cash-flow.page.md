# Current page: Rental Property Cash Flow Calculator (/real-estate/cash-flow-calculator/)

This calculator already computes cash flow, cap rate, cash-on-cash return, and DSCR for a
single-family (or any) rental deal from purchase price, financing, rent, and expenses. The page
already has a FAQ ("Is there a dedicated DSCR calculator, or does this tool cover it?") noting
that commercial and larger multifamily lenders lean on the same DSCR ratio, often requiring 1.20
to 1.25 or higher, and links to /guides/commercial-mortgage-calculator-explained/ for the
commercial DSCR threshold.

Worked example already on page: $240,000 purchase, 25% down, $180,000 loan at 6.75%/30yr =
$1,167/mo P&I, $2,300/mo rent, 5% vacancy -> $26,220 effective gross income, $8,516/yr operating
expenses -> $17,704 NOI, $308/mo cash flow, 5.5% cash-on-cash return, 7.38% cap rate, 1.26 DSCR.
