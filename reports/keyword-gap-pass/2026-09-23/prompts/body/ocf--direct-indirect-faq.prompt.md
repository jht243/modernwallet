## CLOSED FACT LIST
- FASB ASC 230 permits two presentation methods for the operating activities section of the cash
  flow statement: the indirect method (start with net income, add back non-cash charges, adjust
  for working capital changes) and the direct method (list actual cash inflows from customers and
  cash outflows to suppliers/employees directly). Both methods must produce the same total
  operating cash flow number; they differ only in how that number is presented/derived.
- This calculator implements the indirect method only (already stated on the page).
- The vast majority of public companies use the indirect method in practice because it is easier
  to prepare from existing accrual accounting records.
