- SCHD tracks the Dow Jones U.S. Dividend 100 Index. A stock must have paid dividends for at least 10 consecutive years to be eligible. Eligible names are then ranked on cash flow to total debt, return on equity, dividend yield, and five-year dividend growth.
- SCHD's index holds 100 stocks, excludes REITs, master limited partnerships, preferred stock, and convertibles. No single stock may exceed 4% of the index; no sector may exceed 25% at construction or rebalance.
- SCHD's total annual fund operating expenses are 0.06%, per its summary prospectus.
- VYM tracks the FTSE High Dividend Yield Index, which holds common stocks whose dividends are generally higher than average, with no quality overlay or dividend-growth-streak requirement. The index excludes REITs. The fund invests at least 80% of net assets in index stocks.
- VYM's total annual fund operating expenses are 0.04%, per Vanguard's summary prospectus (dated February 27, 2026) — tied for the lowest fee among widely held dividend ETFs.
- Both funds are market weighted within their respective screens; SCHD applies its quality/yield/growth screen before weighting, VYM does not apply a quality screen and is broader (several hundred holdings) versus SCHD's fixed 100-stock roster.
