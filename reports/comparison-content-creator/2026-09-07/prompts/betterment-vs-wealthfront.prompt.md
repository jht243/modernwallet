## THE CLOSED FACT LIST — every number/date/limit this page may state

(Sourced from this site's own already-published, current robo-advisor roundup — reuse these exactly.)

- Betterment charges $5 a month as its base/flat price, with no minimum balance required to open an account.
- Betterment switches you to a 0.25% annual fee once you either set up $200 or more in monthly recurring deposits, OR reach a $24,000 balance across your Betterment investing accounts.
- The crossover math: $5/month is $60/year, and 0.25% of $24,000 is also $60/year — below roughly $24,000 without recurring deposits, the flat $5/month fee is the more expensive of the two as an effective rate.
- Betterment Premium adds access to human financial advisors, charges 0.65% a year on the first $1,000,000, and requires $100,000 in eligible household assets to enroll.
- Betterment's tiered discount above $1,000,000: dollars between $1,000,000 and $2,000,000 are charged 0.15%, and dollars above $2,000,000 are charged 0.10%, while the first $1,000,000 stays at the standard rate.
- Betterment includes tax-loss harvesting at every balance level (no separate threshold to unlock it).
- Betterment charges a $75 flat fee per outbound account transfer to another firm.
- Wealthfront charges 0.25% a year on its Automated Investing Account, with a $500 minimum to open.
- Wealthfront includes tax-loss harvesting at every balance, with no separate dollar threshold required to unlock it.
- Wealthfront offers US Direct Indexing on any taxable Automated Investing Account holding at least $100,000, with no stated upper limit (accounts above $500,000 simply hold more individual stocks directly).
- Wealthfront's Smart Beta is a separate, no-additional-fee add-on available at a $500,000 balance, that enhances direct indexing rather than replacing it.
- Wealthfront also offers a separate S&P 500 Direct account, holding individual index stocks directly, charging a 0.09% advisory fee with a $5,000 minimum.
- Wealthfront's Automated Bond Ladder charges 0.15%; its Nasdaq-100 Direct account charges 0.12%.
- Wealthfront does not offer access to a human financial advisor or CFP at any account balance or tier.
- Wealthfront's Form ADV describes how the service manages wash-sale risk inside a client's own Wealthfront accounts, but it cannot see or coordinate with trades made in a client's outside brokerage accounts.

Anything not on this list, you do not know. Never invent a specific portfolio return, a specific number of ETFs each service holds, or a fee not listed above. Tell the reader to verify current fees at each company's own pricing page before opening an account, since fee schedules can change.
