## THE CLOSED FACT LIST — every number/date/limit this page may state

- Fidelity is a large, independently owned full-service brokerage offering commission-free online stock and ETF trades, retirement accounts (including IRAs and a custodial Roth IRA), and its own suite of mutual funds and index funds.
- Merrill Edge is the self-directed brokerage arm of Bank of America, offering commission-free online stock and ETF trades and integrating directly with Bank of America checking, savings, and credit card accounts inside one login.
- Merrill Edge does not offer a custodial Roth IRA for minors; it offers custodial UGMA/UTMA accounts instead, which are general taxable custodial accounts without a Roth IRA's tax-free growth structure. Fidelity does offer a custodial Roth IRA for minors with documented earned income, with no stated account minimum.
- Merrill Edge participates in Bank of America's Preferred Rewards program, which ties banking and investing balances together to unlock benefits such as credit card rewards boosts and reduced banking fees; do not state specific reward percentages or balance tiers — direct the reader to Bank of America's own Preferred Rewards page.
- Merrill Edge offers a robo-advisor option called Merrill Guided Investing, which requires a $1,000 minimum and charges a 0.45% annual advisory fee; Fidelity offers its own robo-advisor, Fidelity Go, as a separate product from self-directed trading. Do not state Fidelity Go's specific fee or minimum — direct the reader to Fidelity's own page for current terms.
