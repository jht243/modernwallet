## WHAT'S MISSING (the enrichment gap)
The page explains the qualified-vs-non-qualified TAX distinction in passing (one table cell + a few sentences + one FAQ), but never treats "qualified annuity vs non-qualified annuity" as its own head-to-head. A reader searching that exact term needs the FULL distinction: what funds each type (retirement-plan money vs after-tax money), contribution/rollover rules, whether IRS required minimum distribution (RMD) rules apply, early-withdrawal penalty treatment, and a real verdict on who ends up with each type. This is a materially bigger gap than the tax-treatment sentence currently covers.
