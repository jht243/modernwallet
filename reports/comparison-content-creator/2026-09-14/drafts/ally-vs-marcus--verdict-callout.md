If your choice is down to these two, pick [Ally Bank](https://www.ally.com/bank/online-savings-account/) if you want no minimum deposit, no monthly fee, and a standard rate that never requires promotional management. Pick [Marcus by Goldman Sachs](https://www.marcus.com/us/en/savings/high-yield-savings) if you can line up a referral to capture its temporary Referred Rate Boost. That rate increase lasts for a limited multi-month window before your balance reverts to the standard rate. Choose Marcus only if you will actively track that reversion date instead of assuming the promotional boost is permanent.
