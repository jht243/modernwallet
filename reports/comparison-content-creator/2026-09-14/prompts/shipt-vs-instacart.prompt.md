## THE CLOSED FACT LIST — every number/date/limit this page may state

- Shipt is a grocery-shopping and delivery service owned by Target; its store selection is centered on Target and Shipt's other named retail partners.
- Instacart (NASDAQ: CART) is an independent, publicly traded grocery-delivery platform that partners with a broad range of grocery chains rather than being owned by any single retailer.
- Both companies use a similar operating model: a shopper picks items in-store (with in-app substitution/communication tools) and delivers them to the customer.
- Both classify their shoppers as independent contractors (1099, no tax withholding), not employees.
- Both offer a paid membership/subscription for reduced or waived delivery fees (Instacart's is Instacart+; Shipt operates on an annual or monthly membership model for free delivery on qualifying orders).
- Do NOT state specific current membership prices, specific shopper pay rates, or specific delivery fees — these change and vary by market.
