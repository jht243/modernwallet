## THE CLOSED FACT LIST — every number/date/limit this page may state

- H&R Block offers both DIY online tax software and in-person tax preparation at physical retail offices located across the United States — it is the only one of the two with a real in-person filing option.
- TaxAct is an online/desktop DIY tax-preparation software company with no retail office network; all filing is done by the taxpayer online or via downloaded software, generally at a lower price point than H&R Block's comparable online tiers.
- Both companies offer tiered pricing (a free tier for simple returns, moving up through paid tiers for more complex situations like self-employment income or itemized deductions); this site already has separate comparisons covering TurboTax against each of these two, so this page should NOT re-explain TurboTax's own pricing tiers in depth — it should focus on the H&R Block/TaxAct comparison itself and link out to the existing TurboTax comparisons for that context.
- TaxAct offers a "Price Lock Guarantee" on its online products, meaning the price quoted when a filer starts their return is locked and won't increase later in the process for the same tax situation.
- H&R Block offers an "audit support" add-on and, because of its in-person office network, a filer can hand off a return in-progress to a tax professional at a local office if their situation becomes too complex for the DIY software — TaxAct has no equivalent in-person handoff option.
- Both companies allow e-filing directly with the IRS and both support importing a prior year's return from major competitors.
- Do NOT state specific current-year dollar prices for either product's tiers — pricing changes every tax season.
