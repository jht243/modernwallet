## What to cover, section by section

2. **How the two scheduling models actually work** — Flex's block-reservation system (drivers claim available blocks, sometimes released at set times, requiring the app to be checked to grab good blocks) vs DoorDash's open-ended dash-anytime model (start/stop whenever, accept or decline individual offers).
3. **How pay is structured and what that means day to day** — Flex's block-total estimate vs DoorDash's per-delivery base-plus-tip model visible before acceptance; note that DoorDash gives more real-time control over which specific deliveries to take, while Flex trades that control for a scheduled, plannable time commitment.

6. **Verdict** — recommend Flex for workers who want schedule predictability and don't mind competing for blocks in advance; recommend DoorDash for workers who want maximum flexibility to start and stop on a whim and to see exact pay before accepting each job.
