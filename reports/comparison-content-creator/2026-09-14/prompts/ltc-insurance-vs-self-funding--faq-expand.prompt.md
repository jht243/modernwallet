## THE CLOSED FACT LIST

- LTC insurance trades a known, recurring premium cost for a capped daily/monthly benefit if
  care is ever needed; the insurer bears the risk of the reader needing care longer or more
  expensively than the policy anticipated.
- Self-funding trades that premium cost for full liquidity and control of the reader's own
  assets, but the reader personally bears the full risk of an unexpectedly long or expensive
  care need, with no cap on their own exposure.
- LTC insurance premiums are not guaranteed level for the life of the policy on most products —
  insurers have a history of applying premium rate increases to in-force policyholders, subject
  to state regulatory approval, which is why obtaining current quotes is necessary rather than
  assuming a quoted premium stays flat for decades.
- Do NOT state a specific premium dollar amount, a specific rate-increase percentage, or a
  specific benefit cap dollar amount.

Anything not on this list, you do not know. Never invent a premium amount or benefit cap.
