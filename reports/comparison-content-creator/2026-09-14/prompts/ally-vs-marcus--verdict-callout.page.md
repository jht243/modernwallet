Existing verdict (best-high-yield-savings-accounts roundup):

For the simplest fee-free, no-minimum option with a long track record, Ally, American Express, Synchrony, and Capital One all offer essentially the same structure — no fees, no minimum balance, and a rate that moves with the broader market. Marcus and Wealthfront are worth a closer look if you can take advantage of a referral or new-client rate boost, since those temporary boosts can meaningfully outrun the pack for a few months at a time.
