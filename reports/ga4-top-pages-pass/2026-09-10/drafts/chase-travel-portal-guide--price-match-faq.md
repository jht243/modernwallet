According to [Forbes Advisor](https://www.forbes.com/advisor/credit-cards/chase-travel-price-match-guarantee-lmandp5/), Chase is piloting a Price Match Guarantee for prepaid hotel bookings, limited to select Chase Sapphire Reserve, Chase Sapphire Reserve for Business, and J.P. Morgan Reserve cardholders. Launched in beta on March 18, 2026, the feature excludes flights, rental cars, and cruises, with no timeline to expand to other Chase cards. Claims must be submitted within 24 hours of booking for an exact match on room, bed type, cancellation policy, and guests, with a public USD difference over $5 per night. Loyalty, promotional, corporate, or coupon rates do not qualify, and approved claims refund the difference to the original payment method within 1 to 2 billing cycles.
