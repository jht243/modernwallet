## THE CLOSED FACT LIST

Anything not on this list, you do not know. Never invent a price, limit, benchmark, or URL;
say it is unpublished and tell the reader to verify at the source's own page. Where a fact
varies by cardholder cohort, say so explicitly rather than picking one number.

1. Every Ultimate Rewards-earning card (Chase Freedom, Sapphire Preferred, Sapphire Reserve,
   the Ink Business line, etc.) can book through the Chase Travel portal at a **baseline value
   of 1 cent per point** for standard (non-Points Boost) redemptions.
   Source: Chase — "How to use the Chase Travel℠ portal".
2. **Points Boost** (a rotating set of tagged hotel/flight listings) redeems at an elevated
   rate for a smaller set of premium cards: up to **2 cents per point** on Sapphire Reserve,
   Sapphire Reserve for Business, and J.P. Morgan Reserve; up to **1.5 cents per point** on
   Sapphire Preferred and Ink Business Preferred / Ink Business Plus.
   Source: Chase — "How Points Boost Works".
3. A cardholder who held an eligible card **before the Points Boost overhaul (applied before
   June 23, 2025)** may still redeem at their card's prior fixed rate for a transition window —
   e.g., a legacy Sapphire Reserve cardholder at 1.5 cents per point baseline (not 1 cent) on
   all Chase Travel purchases, separate from Points Boost. Source: The Points Guy —
   "Chase Ultimate Rewards Points Value" (valuation dated June 2026).
4. Ultimate Rewards points can be **transferred to airline and hotel partners**, generally at a
   1:1 ratio. Source: Chase — "How to use the Chase Travel℠ portal" (existing guide, already
   sourced).
5. **The Points Guy's June 2026 valuation values Chase Ultimate Rewards points at 2.05 cents
   each** as a standard target for well-executed transfer-partner redemptions — meaningfully
   above the portal's 1-cent baseline and above even most Points Boost rates. Source: The
   Points Guy — "Chase Ultimate Rewards Points Value" (dated June 2026; state the date, this
   number moves over time and must not be presented as fixed/permanent).
6. Chase is piloting a **Price Match Guarantee for prepaid hotel bookings** made through Chase
   Travel, in **beta since March 18, 2026**, limited to select **Chase Sapphire Reserve, Chase
   Sapphire Reserve for Business, and J.P. Morgan Reserve** cardholders (Chase has said it
   plans to extend this to more Sapphire Reserve cardholders but has not given a timeline, and
   has not said whether it will extend beyond these three cards). It does NOT cover flights,
   rental cars, or other bookings — hotels only. To claim: submit within 24 hours of booking,
   the competing publicly-available rate must match room type, bed type, cancellation policy,
   number of guests, and be in USD; the nightly rate difference must be more than $5; loyalty-
   member-only, promotional, corporate, or coupon-based rates do not qualify. An approved claim
   refunds the difference to the original payment method within 1 to 2 billing cycles. Source:
   Forbes Advisor — "Chase Travel Tests Price Match Guarantee On Select Hotel Bookings" (state
   this is Forbes Advisor's reporting; Chase has not published its own public policy page for
   this feature as of this writing, so do not attribute it to Chase directly — attribute it to
   Forbes Advisor's reporting on Chase's rollout).
7. The portal covers flights, hotels, rental cars, and cruises. Source: existing sibling guide
   (already published, same site).
