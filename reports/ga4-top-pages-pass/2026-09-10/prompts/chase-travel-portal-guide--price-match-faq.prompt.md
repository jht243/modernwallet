## THE CLOSED FACT LIST

Anything not on this list, you do not know. Never invent a number.

1. Chase is piloting a **Price Match Guarantee for prepaid hotel bookings** made through Chase
   Travel, in **beta since March 18, 2026**, limited to select **Chase Sapphire Reserve, Chase
   Sapphire Reserve for Business, and J.P. Morgan Reserve** cardholders. Chase has said it plans
   to extend this to more Sapphire Reserve cardholders but has given no timeline, and has not
   said whether it will extend to other cards (do not imply it is available to all Ultimate
   Rewards cardholders — it is not).
2. It covers **prepaid hotel bookings only** — it does NOT cover flights, rental cars, or
   cruises.
3. To claim: submit within **24 hours of booking**. The competing rate must be publicly
   available, in USD, and match the original reservation exactly on room type, bed type,
   cancellation policy, and number of guests. The nightly rate difference must be **more than
   $5**. Loyalty-member-only, promotional, corporate, or coupon-based rates do NOT qualify.
4. An approved claim refunds the difference to the **original payment method within 1 to 2
   billing cycles**.
5. Source for all of the above: Forbes Advisor's reporting on Chase's rollout (Chase itself has
   not published its own public policy page for this feature as of this writing — attribute the
   claim to Forbes Advisor's reporting, not to Chase directly).
