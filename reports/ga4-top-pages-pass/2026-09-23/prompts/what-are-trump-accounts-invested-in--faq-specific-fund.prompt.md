## THE CLOSED FACT LIST — the only facts this answer may state

- The law (IRC 530A) requires the account to hold a low-cost mutual fund or ETF that tracks the
  S&P 500 or a similar broad U.S.-equity index. It does not name one specific fund.
- This site's own sibling guide (/guides/trump-accounts/) already establishes, and you may reuse
  verbatim, that: the Bank of New York Mellon (BNY) is the Treasury's financial agent; Robinhood
  is the initial trustee; Fidelity, Schwab, Vanguard, and Bank of America are among the other
  approved trustees a family can choose.
- Because the law names an INDEX, not a specific FUND, each trustee selects its own qualifying
  low-cost fund that meets the S&P-500-or-similar requirement — so the exact fund (and its
  ticker and expense ratio) differs by which trustee/provider a family picks.
