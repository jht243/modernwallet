CLOSED FACT LIST (anything not here, you do not know; never invent a number, date, return rate, or URL):
1. A Trump Account's money is locked until January 1 of the calendar year the child turns 18; it cannot be used earlier. (this page, FAQ "What's the catch")
2. Growth is tax-deferred, and withdrawals are taxed as ordinary income under traditional-IRA rules, unlike a Roth IRA or a 529 used for school. (this page)
3. Contributions are capped at $5,000 per year, made with after-tax dollars, not deductible, and must be invested in a U.S.-equity index fund such as an S&P 500 fund. (this page)
4. The $1,000 federal seed goes to U.S.-citizen children born January 1, 2025 through December 31, 2028 only; a child born earlier gets no seed. (this page)
5. Children born 2016 through 2024 in ZIP codes with median family income about $118,000 or less may receive a separate $250 deposit funded by Michael and Susan Dell; no application is needed. (this site's Dell guide)
6. A custodial Roth IRA offers tax-free growth but requires the child to have earned income. (this page)
7. A 529 plan is tax-free for education; a custodial brokerage account has no lock-up and full investment choice. (this page)
8. Almost any child under 18 can have an account opened. (this page, eligibility)
