- Claiming Social Security at 62 instead of 67 cuts the monthly check by 30%. On a $2,000 monthly benefit at 67, claiming at 62 pays $1,400 a month for life.
- Waiting from 62 to 67 adds $600 a month on that example; the extra pays back the skipped payments by about age 79. Break-even ages are estimates, not guarantees.
