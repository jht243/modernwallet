Retire at 67 if you want a larger guaranteed monthly check and Medicare already in place, but retire at 62 only if your savings can bridge the three-year health coverage gap and fund extra years of living costs. Stopping work and claiming Social Security are separate choices, so you can leave your job at 62 while delaying your claim to protect your benefit amount. For anyone born in 1960 or later, waiting until [full retirement age](https://www.ssa.gov/retirement/full-retirement-age) at 67 avoids the permanent 30% benefit reduction and ensures your savings have to cover fewer years.
