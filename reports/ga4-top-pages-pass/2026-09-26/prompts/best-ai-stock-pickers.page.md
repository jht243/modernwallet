# Best AI Stock Picker roundup (current page text, condensed)

## Intro
The best AI stock picker for most retail investors is Danelfin, which uses explainable machine
learning to assign equities a transparent 1 to 10 probability rating based on technical,
fundamental, and sentiment factors. This review evaluates standalone AI equity screening and
ranking platforms — not day-trading scanners (see the best AI trading bots roundup) or general
chatbots (see the AI financial advice chatbots guide).

## The 5 tools reviewed, with bestFor + free-tier + pricing facts already published on this page

1. **Danelfin** — bestFor: "Swing traders and medium-term equity investors seeking transparent
   factor ratings." Free tier: yes, limited stock reports. Paid: Plus ~$22/mo, Pro ~$59/mo.
   Strength noted: "Free tier lets you evaluate daily ratings on major index components."
2. **Kavout** — bestFor: "Quantitative equity screeners who want credit-based machine learning
   research." Free tier: yes, $0 with 10 research credits/month. Paid: Pro $16/mo (billed
   annually), Premium $39/mo (billed annually). Limitation noted: "Interface caters to
   intermediate quantitative investors rather than casual beginners."
3. **Trade Ideas** — bestFor: "Active day traders requiring real-time intraday algorithmic alert
   streams." No free tier. Paid: Standard $127/mo, Premium $254/mo. Limitation noted: "Steep
   learning curve with a complex interface that overwhelms casual investors" and "Most expensive
   tool on this list."
4. **Prospero.ai** — bestFor: "Tracking institutional dark pool flow and options market sentiment
   on mobile." Free tier: yes, "generous free tier provides daily actionable market sentiment
   signals." Paid: unpublished in-app pricing.
5. **Magnifi** — bestFor: "Conversational natural-language screening across stocks and mutual
   funds." Free tier: yes, basic exploratory features. Paid: Magnifi Premium ~$14/mo.
