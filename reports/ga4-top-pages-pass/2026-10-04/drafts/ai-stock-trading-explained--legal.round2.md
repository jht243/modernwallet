Nothing in recent enforcement actions makes using an artificial intelligence (AI) tool itself unlawful. Regulators have acted against deceptive marketing and fraud that uses AI claims as bait, rather than the underlying software. The Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) penalized firms for making false statements about their technology, while a joint alert from the Financial Industry Regulatory Authority (FINRA), the SEC, and state regulators warned investors to check credentials on FINRA BrokerCheck before funding an account. The legality question turns on who operates the platform, whether they are registered, and whether they make unrealistic return promises.
