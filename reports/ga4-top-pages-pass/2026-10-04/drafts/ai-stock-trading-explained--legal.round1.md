Yes, using artificial intelligence for stock trading is legal in the United States, but firms offering these tools must follow existing securities regulations. Federal enforcement actions focus on deceptive marketing and fraud rather than the underlying software. For example, recent regulatory settlements targeted false claims about algorithmic capabilities rather than the technology itself. Before depositing funds with any service, check its registration through official broker databases. Watch out for red flags like demands for upfront fees, requests for login credentials, or promises of guaranteed returns.
