# CLOSED FACT LIST (anything not here, you do not know; never invent a number, weekday, time, limit, or URL)
- A non-competitive bid guarantees you receive the requested amount at the discount rate set by competitive institutional bidders in the auction. You do not know the exact price when you bid.
- The TreasuryDirect auction schedule lists upcoming offerings every Friday by 10:45 AM ET, with the auction date, issue date, and maturity date for each.
- You must submit the bid before the auction closing deadline.
- With TreasuryDirect, money leaves your bank account by ACH on the issue date, not on auction day.
- After 5:00 PM ET on auction day you can see your final discount price on TreasuryDirect.
- The issue date can be a few days or weeks after the auction date.
- Match the bill term to the date you need the cash back, rather than guessing interest rate moves.
- Brokers let you place non-competitive bids too, and also sell existing bills on the secondary market at limit prices.
