# CLOSED FACT LIST (anything not here, you do not know; never invent a date, limit, or URL)
- IRS Form 4547 is titled "Trump Account Election(s)". Taxpayers use it to elect to open an initial Trump account, and it can also be used to elect to request the one-time $1,000 pilot program contribution.
- Who files for the account: the authorized individual is a legal guardian, parent, adult sibling, or grandparent, in that order of priority. For the pilot contribution, the authorized individual is someone who anticipates the child will be their qualifying child for the tax year of the election.
- Account eligibility: the child is under age 18 at the end of the election year (for a 2026 election, born after December 31, 2008), has a valid Social Security number issued before the election is made, and has no Trump account election already filed.
- Pilot contribution eligibility: the child is born after December 31, 2024 and before January 1, 2029, has not had a prior pilot program election processed, is a U.S. citizen, and has a valid SSN.
- When and how: Form 4547 can be filed at any time, including with the income tax return. The IRS instructions describe e-filing with the current-year return as the fastest method. Elections after filing, or online options, are handled at trumpaccounts.gov; the instructions say online elections may be available starting in the middle of 2026. On paper, mail it to the address at IRS.gov/PaperReturns for that year return. Do not attach Form 4547 to Form 1040-X or amend a Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR to attach it.
- What the form asks: Part I the authorized individual name, address, SSN; Part II the child name, SSN, relationship to the filer, address, and a check box confirming authority to open the account; Part III line 7 for the pilot program election; Part IV consent to disclosure, signature and date. Paper forms need a handwritten signature.
- If the child is not eligible for the pilot, the account election is still made on Form 4547 and line 7 is left unchecked.
- The pilot contribution is made as soon as practicable after the election and after Treasury confirms the account is opened, but not before July 4, 2026.
- The IRS is still finalizing detailed regulations under Notice 2025-68 (from the existing page).
