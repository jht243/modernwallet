Leasing and financing do not evaluate your credit differently. Both lenders and leasing companies review your credit history to determine account approval, interest rates, or lease terms. Because terms vary by creditor, vehicle, and credit profile, the [Federal Trade Commission (FTC)](https://consumer.ftc.gov/financing-or-leasing-car) advises checking your free credit report at [AnnualCreditReport.com](https://www.annualcreditreport.com) and comparing multiple offers.
