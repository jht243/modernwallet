- A house purchase or down payment is not a qualified 529 expense.
- A non-qualified withdrawal: only the earnings are taxed as ordinary income plus a 10% federal penalty; original after-tax contributions come out tax-free and penalty-free.
- Each withdrawal is split pro-rata between contributions and earnings.
- The 10% penalty is waived up to the amount of a scholarship the beneficiary receives; ordinary income tax on earnings still applies.
- Up to $35,000 lifetime of leftover 529 money can be rolled into the beneficiary's Roth IRA; the 529 must be at least 15 years old, each year's rollover is capped at the annual Roth IRA contribution limit, and the beneficiary needs earned income.
