# Podcast Pain Brief — week of 2026-08-25

## Interpreted brief

This week's episodes (Ready For Retirement, How to Money, Suze Orman's Women & Money,
The Better Budgeting Podcast, Catching Up to FI, Rental Income Podcast) skew heavily
toward retirement-readiness anxiety: listeners want to know if they're "behind" on
savings benchmarks, how to convert a portfolio into retirement income, and how Social
Security spousal/early-collection rules actually work. A second cluster is everyday
budgeting — arbitrary age-based savings benchmarks, breaking the paycheck-to-paycheck
cycle, and money-tendency/behavior patterns. A smaller but real cluster covers investing
mechanics (asset allocation, dividend yield claims, HSA usage) and small-scale rental
investing (deal evaluation, mortgage vs. rent math).
