# Podcast Pain Brief — run 2026-09-22

## Interpreted brief
This week's 36 new episodes across 6 transcript-ready personal-finance shows (Ready For
Retirement, How to Money, Suze Orman's Women & Money, The Better Budgeting Podcast, Catching
Up to FI, Rental Income Podcast With Dan Lane) surface listeners wrestling with: how much they
can actually SPEND once they've saved enough (retirement spend-down anxiety, not just
accumulation), whether their portfolio and pension choices are set up right, how to talk money
with aging parents, sinking funds and utility-bill control as budgeting tactics, Roth conversion
timing/penalties, and how everyday landlords are finding and pricing rental deals in a tight
market. The through-line: people who already have a plan are second-guessing whether it's the
*right* plan, and want a spend-down/decision framework more than another "how to save" primer.
