# Podcast Pain-Mining Brief — 2026-09-15

## Interpreted brief

This week's 36 new episodes across 6 transcript-ready personal-finance shows cluster
around readers who are close to (or newly in) retirement and don't trust their own
numbers: how much they can safely spend once the paycheck stops, when to file for
Social Security, whether to do a Roth conversion before RMDs hit, and how a 72(t) plan
or a rental property actually pencils out. A second strand is everyday budgeting and
debt-payoff readers who keep "messing up their budget" and are looking for a concrete
system rather than generic advice. A third, smaller strand is tax/estate readers asking
what a "Trump account" (the new government savings account for kids) actually is and
how it compares to a 529.
