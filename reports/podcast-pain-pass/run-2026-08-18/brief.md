# Podcast Pain Brief — 2026-08-18

## Interpreted brief
This week's 38 episodes across 7 personal-finance shows repeatedly surface the same struggle: people don't know how to translate their specific numbers (a pension, a $2.5M portfolio, an inherited IRA, a rental's cash flow) into a decision they can act on. Listeners are asking "how much can I actually spend," "am I doing this Social Security/mortgage/investing thing right," and "how do these two accounts/strategies compare for MY situation" — not abstract definitions. Real estate investors specifically want cash-flow math help; near-retirees want spend-down and Social Security timing guidance; several shows raised the new 2025 "Trump Accounts" for kids, which listeners don't yet understand.
