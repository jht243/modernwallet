### Betterment (subject) - betterment.com/pricing
- Investing (automated) pricing: $5 per month for balances $0 to $24K; automatically switches to 0.25% annually when you set up recurring deposits totaling $200 or more a month, or reach a $24,000 balance across Betterment investing accounts (including Cash Reserve). No minimum balance for the $5 per month base price.
- Fees are taken from the Betterment investing account at the end of each month; calculated on daily invested balance and assessed monthly; no fee if no balance.
- High-balance discounts (same for Digital and Premium): $1M to $2M portion 0.15% annual; over $2M portion 0.10%. Balances in HSA, 401(k), Cash Reserve, checking and self-directed investing accounts are excluded from the discount balance.
- Premium: 0.65% annual advisory fee on the first $1M (0.25% Digital base plus 0.40% Premium fee); requires $100,000 minimum eligible investment balance per household; access to an advisor and Premium benefits. Over $1M: 0.15% ($1M-$2M) and 0.10% ($2M+), Premium benefits still apply.
- ETFs in portfolios carry embedded fund fees, in addition to Betterment's management fee; Betterment says it does not receive any part of the fund fees. Outbound transfer to another company: flat $75 per investing account. No fee to withdraw to linked checking; no additional trading or deposit fees.
- Customer Support at no additional cost. Free transfer specialist team; complimentary Licensed Concierge team for those moving $20K or more.
- Self-directed investing: trade stocks and ETFs with no trading commissions; Betterment's management fee does not apply to self-directed accounts.
- Cash Reserve: no monthly management fees; variable APY 3.50% as of 9/21/2026, plus a 0.75% boost on balances up to $1M for new clients with a qualifying deposit (4.25% total with the boost, through 4/16/2027 per the offers menu); $10 minimum deposit for base APY. Betterment is not a bank; FDIC insurance through Program Banks, up to $250K per depositor per bank, potential aggregate $4M ($8M joint). Requires a Betterment Securities brokerage account.
- Checking: no overdraft fees; ATM fees reimbursed; Visa 1% foreign transaction fee reimbursed; checking accounts do not earn APY; accounts and debit card provided by nbkc bank, Member FDIC; insured up to $250,000 per depositor.
- Account types named on the site: automated investing, self-directed investing, high-yield cash, IRAs (traditional, Roth, SEP), crypto investing, checking, trusts, Solo 401(k), 401(k) plans for businesses, custom portfolios, tax-saving technology, securities lending.
- Advisory services by Betterment LLC, an SEC-registered investment adviser; brokerage by Betterment Securities, SEC-registered broker-dealer, member FINRA/SIPC. Investments are not FDIC insured and may lose value.
- Betterment's pricing page comparison table lists Wealthfront 0.25%, Fidelity Go "no advisory fee under $25,000, 0.35% per year $25,000+", E*TRADE Core Portfolios 0.30% (competitor claims from Betterment; do not restate as verified; use each vendor's own page below).

### Wealthfront - wealthfront.com/pricing
- Advisory fee for The Wealthfront Portfolio: 0.25% annually. Example on its page: $15,000 balance equals $3.18 per month (as shown there).
- Features listed: automatic trades, expert-built portfolios, fully customizable allocations with hundreds of funds, Tax-Loss Harvesting, automatic rebalancing, app that tracks net worth and links accounts.
- Brokerage Account: no advisory or management fees on stocks and ETFs; $1 to get started; Cash Account earns a high APY (rate not stated in the text read).

### Schwab Intelligent Portfolios - schwab.com/intelligent-portfolios
- No advisory fee and no commissions. Minimum to open: $5,000. Portfolio of low-cost ETFs; ETF operating expenses are paid by the investor, including Schwab ETFs. A portion of the portfolio is held as an FDIC-insured deposit at Charles Schwab Bank (cash allocation based on risk profile); Schwab says some cash alternatives outside the program pay a higher yield. Schwab earns revenue from other sources than an advisory fee.
- Tax-loss harvesting available for taxable portfolios with $50,000 or more, no additional fee, must be enrolled. 24/7 live support from U.S.-based service professionals.

### Vanguard Digital Advisor - investor.vanguard.com/advice/digital-advisor
- $0 advisory fees for the first 90 days (investment costs still apply). $100 in assets required to enroll. About $15 to $16 per year per $10K in an all-index portfolio (Vanguard's approximation). An option with active funds (may include 3 exclusive Advice Select funds) carries a higher advisory fee: no more than $20 per $10,000 annually.
- Features: personalized risk assessment, multiple goals (retirement, house, education), goal optimizer, debt payoff calculator, rainy day emergency fund tool, tax-loss harvesting, option to keep certain holdings on enrollment.

### Fidelity Go - fidelity.com/managed-accounts/fidelity-go/overview
- No minimum to open an account; account balance must be at least $10 for Fidelity to start investing. $0 advisory fees under $25,000; 0.35% per year for balances of $25,000 and above. No trading fees, transaction fees or rebalancing fees.
- At $25,000 or more: unlimited 1-on-1 financial coaching sessions with advisors; for taxable accounts, tax-loss harvesting opportunities in Flex Funds that hold stocks. Clients with $500,000 or more at Fidelity may be eligible for a dedicated representative.

### Acorns - acorns.com/pricing
- Subscription plans, no transaction fees: Bronze $4 per month; Silver $8 per month; Gold $12 per month.
- Bronze: investment account with an expert-built diversified portfolio, Round-Ups (spare change investing), Acorns Later retirement account, Acorns Earn bonus investments, learning courses.
- Silver adds: Real-Time Round-Ups, paycheck split, bonus investments matched up to 25%, live Q&As, 1% IRA match on new contributions in the first year, 3.59% APY on Emergency Savings (variable, as of September 22, 2026).
- Gold adds: investment account for kids with a 1% match, bonus investments matched up to 50%, 3% IRA match on new contributions in the first year, family tools. Acorns checking (Mighty Oak) APY 2.33% as of September 22, 2026; no overdraft fees; 55,000+ fee-free ATMs in the AllPoint network. Acorns is not a bank; banking by nbkc bank or Lincoln Savings Bank, members FDIC. Plans page also lists a Premium subscription with a $10,000 life insurance policy and a Will for eligible customers (price not stated in the text read).

### Ally Invest Robo Portfolios - ally.com/invest/managed-portfolios/
- Minimum $100 for Robo Portfolios. Two options: cash-enhanced portfolio with 30% held as an interest-earning cash buffer, no advisory fee, 3.20% annual interest on the cash buffer credited monthly (cash buffer is not accessible like savings and not FDIC insured); or a portfolio investing about 98% in the market with a 0.30% annual advisory fee ($3 per $1,000), about 2% cash earning 3.20%. Fee charged within the first 10 days of the month on daily portfolio balance. ETF expense ratios are separate. Personal Advice requires $100,000 minimum. SIPC coverage up to $500,000 including $250,000 cash.

### M1 - m1.com/pricing
- Self-directed investing with automation: no commission, trading or management fees for self-directed brokerage accounts; $3 per month platform fee, waived for clients with more than $10,000 on the platform. High-Yield Cash Account 3.35% APY (variable; minimum initial deposit $100). Margin loans at 5.90% on accounts with at least $2,000 in eligible securities. Cash swept to partner banks eligible for FDIC insurance up to an aggregate of $4.75 million. SIPC protection up to $500,000. $14B+ in client assets as of Sept. 2026 (M1 claim).
