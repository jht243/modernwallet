Stash (stash.com/pricing):
- One plan, "The Stash Plan": $12 per month, or $108 billed annually (listed as saving 25%).
- Includes 24/7 financial guidance, a personalized investment plan, a 3% retirement match (worth up to $225 per year), a $10,000 life insurance policy, and Stock-Back rewards (worth up to $120 per year). 7-day trial offered.
- Page says no banking overdraft charges and no add-on transaction fees; flat monthly subscription.
- Also charges an assets-under-management (AUM) advisory fee of 0.25% annually on Smart Portfolios with balances of $1,000 or more and on Managed Retirement Accounts.
- Accounts: personal brokerage, retirement, custodial accounts for kids, Smart Portfolio. Fractional shares from $5 or less; thousands of stocks and ETFs. Stock-Back Card earns stock when you spend. Direct deposit up to 2 days early. Budgeting tools.
- Expert money coaching from a registered investment advisor, 24/7 instant answers. Stash is an SEC-registered investment advisor. Banking through Stride Bank N.A., member FDIC. Cancel any time. Built for long-term investing, not day trading. Over 1M investors.
- Derived arithmetic (allowed): $12 x 12 = $144 per year billed monthly; $108 billed annually; 0.25% of $10,000 = $25.

Acorns (acorns.com/pricing):
- Bronze $4 per month; Silver $8 per month; Gold $12 per month.
- All tiers: investment account with expert-built diversified portfolio, Round-Ups, Acorns Later retirement account, Acorns Checking (no overdraft fees, 55,000+ fee-free ATMs), Earn bonus investments, Learn courses.
- Silver adds a 1% IRA match on new contributions in the first year; Gold has a 3% IRA match on new contributions in the first year (up to $225 on a maxed annual contribution, per Acorns).
- Gold adds Early Invest (kids' account), Custom Portfolios (individual stocks and ETFs), a $10,000 life insurance policy for eligible customers, a no-cost Will (valued at $299), tax filing through third-party providers, allowance and chore tools.
- Emergency Savings APY 3.59% as of September 22, 2026, variable.
- Derived: Gold $12 x 12 = $144 per year.

Betterment (betterment.com/pricing):
- Investing accounts: base price $5 per month; switches to 0.25% annually on the balance with recurring deposits totaling $200 or more per month or $24,000 or more across Betterment investing accounts. No minimum balance.
- Premium: 0.65% annual fee, needs $100,000 minimum eligible investments, access to advisors. Balances over $1 million get discounts (0.15% for $1 to $2 million; 0.10% above $2 million).
- Cash Reserve: 3.50% variable APY as of 9/21/2026, plus 0.75% boost on balances up to $1M for new clients with a qualifying deposit; no fees. Checking with no overdraft fees and ATM fees reimbursed.
- Fund expenses are in addition to Betterment's fees.
- Derived: $5 x 12 = $60 per year; 0.25% of $10,000 = $25 per year.

Wealthfront (wealthfront.com/pricing):
- Wealthfront Portfolio advisory fee 0.25% annually. Tax-loss harvesting is described as covering the fee for many Classic portfolio clients (no figure to be stated).
- Brokerage Account: "No fees. $1 to get started." for buying ETFs and stocks without advisory or management fees. Cash Account earns APY (no rate to be stated).
- Derived: 0.25% of $10,000 = $25 per year.

Robinhood (robinhood.com/us/en/gold/):
- Robinhood Gold $5 per month; first 30 days free.
- Gold: 3.6% APY on eligible brokerage cash, no cap and no minimum; 3% match on IRA contributions (up to $225 based on the 2026 maximum; must stay subscribed to Gold for one year and keep assets in the IRA five years for the full match); deposits up to 3x portfolio value; Robinhood Strategies managed portfolios with no management fees above $100K for Gold members; $0.50 per futures contract and $0.35 per index options contract; instant withdrawal costs a 1.5% fee.
- Derived: $5 x 12 = $60 per year.

M1 (m1.com/pricing):
- No commission, trading or management fees for self-directed brokerage accounts. Platform fee $3 per month, waived for clients with more than $10,000 on the platform. M1 Advisor is a free advisory service for M1 clients. Other fees may apply (regulatory, account closure, ADR).
- High-Yield Cash Account listed at 3.35% APY on uninvested cash. Accounts: individual, joint, custodial, Traditional, Roth and SEP IRA, trust. Margin on accounts with at least $2,000 in eligible securities.
- Derived: $3 x 12 = $36 per year below $10,000.

Fidelity Go (fidelity.com Fidelity Go overview):
- $0 advisory fee for balances under $25,000; 0.35% per year at $25,000 and above. No minimum to open; invest with as little as $10 (balance must be at least $10 to invest).
- From $25,000: unlimited 1-on-1 financial coaching calls; tax-loss harvesting on taxable accounts in Flex Funds holding stocks. IRA, HSA or taxable account choice. Mutual funds mix.
- Derived: 0.35% of $30,000 = $105 per year.
