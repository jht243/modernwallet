### Subject: Venmo (`https://venmo.com/` and `https://venmo.com/about/fees/`, checked November 15, 2026)
- Operated as a service of PayPal, Inc. (licensed money transmitter, NMLS ID 910457).
- **Account and personal domestic sending fees:** **$0.00** to set up an account; **$0.00** monthly fee; **$0.00** for online purchases; **$0.00** to send money to a Venmo account or US PayPal account using your Venmo balance, debit card, or linked bank account; **3.00% fee** to send money to a Venmo account or US PayPal account using a **credit card**; **$0.00** to receive personal payments (unless the sender marks the payment as for goods and services).
- **Cashing out to a bank or debit card:**
  - **Standard transfer:** **$0.00** (typically available in **1 to 3 business days** when sent to a linked bank account, and approximately **48 hours** when sent to an eligible linked debit card).
  - **Instant Transfer** (typically available in minutes to an eligible linked debit card or bank account): **1.75%** fee, with a **$0.25 minimum** and **$25.00 maximum** fee.
- **Seller, business, and charity fees:**
  - Personal profile payment identified by the sender as **goods and services**: **2.99%** seller transaction fee.
  - **Venmo Business Profile:** **1.9% + $0.10** per payment received (except Tap to Pay), and **2.29% + $0.09** per payment received via **Tap to Pay**.
  - **Charity profile:** **1.9% + $0.10** transaction fee.
- **International sending (limited to eligible non-US PayPal accounts):** **5.00%** fee (**$0.99 minimum**, **$4.99 maximum**) when funded by Venmo balance, debit card, or bank account; plus an additional **3.00%** fee when funded by credit card (**4.00%** currency conversion spread, or lower as disclosed during the transaction).
- **Check cashing, Debit Card, Credit Card & Crypto:**
  - In-app check cashing in minutes: **2.00%** (**$6.00 minimum**) for payroll and government checks with pre-printed signature; **5.00%** (**$6.00 minimum**) for other checks ($0 if rejected).
  - **Venmo Debit Card:** **$0.00** card acquisition or replacement fee; **$0.00** at US MoneyPass Automated Teller Machines (ATMs); **$2.50** at out-of-network US ATMs or non-US ATMs; **2.50%** foreign transaction fee outside the US; **$3.00** over-the-counter bank signature withdrawal; up to **5% cash back** with Venmo Stash rewards at select brands (capped at **$100/month**; 5% tier requires **$1,500** in qualifying monthly spend).
  - **Venmo Visa Credit Card** (issued by Synchrony Bank): **$0 annual fee**, up to **4% cash back**, variable purchase Annual Percentage Rate (APR) of Prime + 11.99%, 20.99%, or 23.99%; cash advance fee of **$10.00 or 5%** (whichever is greater).
  - **Cryptocurrency:** Tiered buy/sell fees from **$1** starting size — **2.20%** ($1 to $74.99), **2.00%** ($75 to $200), **1.80%** ($200.01 to $1,000), **1.50%** above $1,000 (fees waived for buying/selling PYUSD; blockchain network fee applies on external transfers).

### Alternative 1: Zelle (`https://www.zellepay.com/` and `https://www.zellepay.com/faq/are-there-any-fees-send-money-using-zelle`, checked November 15, 2026)
- Operated by Early Warning Services, LLC and embedded directly inside over **2,400** US bank and credit union mobile apps and online banking portals.
- **Published fees:** Zelle itself charges **$0** consumer fees to send or receive money, and according to a **Q1 2026 survey** published on Zelle's site, **99.40%** of consumer checking and savings accounts linked to Zelle charge **$0** to send, receive, or request money (Zelle advises confirming with your specific bank or credit union).
- **How settlement works (no wallet hold or instant cash-out fee):** Both sender and recipient must hold eligible US checking or savings accounts. Money moves **directly from bank account to bank account**, typically arriving **within minutes** for enrolled users — meaning there is no intermediate app balance to cash out and **no 1.75% instant transfer fee**.
- **Limits & constraints vs Venmo:** **US bank accounts only** (cannot send internationally); **cannot fund payments with a credit card**; payments **cannot be reversed or canceled** once the recipient is enrolled (cancellable only if the recipient has not yet enrolled); per-transaction and daily dollar sending limits are set by each participating bank rather than published as a single network figure on Zelle's consumer FAQ page.
- **Worked arithmetic:** If a roommate sends you **$1,000** for rent and you need the money in your checking account the same day, cashing out **$1,000** via Venmo Instant Transfer at **1.75%** costs **$17.50** (or **$210/year** across 12 monthly rent transfers), whereas receiving **$1,000** through Zelle at one of the 99.40% fee-free participating banks lands directly in your bank account in minutes for **$0.00**.

### Alternative 2: PayPal (`https://www.paypal.com/us/digital-wallet/paypal-consumer-fees`, checked November 15, 2026)
- Venmo's parent company (PayPal, Inc.), offering a standalone digital wallet that connects directly with both PayPal and Venmo accounts plus cross-border personal transfers.
- **Published consumer personal transaction fees ("Friends and Family"):**
  - **Domestic personal send** funded by PayPal balance, PayPal Debit Card, linked bank account, or Amex Send Account: **$0.00**.
  - **Domestic personal send funded by credit or debit card:** **2.90% + $0.30 USD** fixed fee (slightly below Venmo's flat **3.00%** credit card fee above $30, though PayPal applies the card rate to card-funded Friends and Family sends whereas Venmo charges $0 on debit cards).
  - **Receiving personal transactions:** **$0.00** when no currency conversion is involved.
  - **International personal send:** **5.00% international fee** (**$0.99 USD minimum**, **$4.99 USD maximum**), plus the domestic funding fee (**$0** from balance/bank, or **2.90% + $0.30 USD** from cards), plus a **4.00%** currency conversion spread when the recipient receives a different currency (**3.00%** conversion spread on other conversions).
- **Withdrawing from a personal PayPal account:**
  - **Standard transfer** to a linked bank account or eligible debit card: **$0.00**.
  - **Instant Transfer** to a linked bank account or eligible debit card: **1.75%** of the withdrawal amount (**$0.25 USD minimum**, **$25.00 USD maximum** — identical to Venmo's 1.75% instant transfer schedule). Paper check withdrawal (for accounts without a PayPal Balance account): **$1.50 USD**.
- **Donations & crypto:** Receiving domestic donations costs **2.89% + $0.49 USD** fixed fee (+ **1.50%** additional fee for international donations); crypto buy/sell tiers run **2.20%**, **2.00%**, **1.80%**, and **1.50%**, plus **1%** on non-PYUSD one-step external wallet transfers (**1.5%** for PYUSD).
- **Worked arithmetic:** Sending **$500** domestically to a friend using a **credit card** costs **$15.00** on Venmo (3.00% of $500) versus **$14.80** on PayPal (**2.90% of $500 = $14.50 + $0.30 = $14.80**), a $0.20 difference. However, sending **$500** using a **debit card** costs **$0.00** on Venmo (debit cards are free on Venmo personal sends) versus **$0.00** only when using a linked bank account or balance on PayPal.

### Alternative 3: Apple Cash (`https://www.apple.com/apple-cash/` and `https://applecash.greendot.com/termsconditions/`, checked November 15, 2026)
- Built into the Wallet and Messages apps on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, and Apple Vision Pro; services provided by **Green Dot Bank** (Member Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC), insured up to **$250,000** once the card is registered) and Apple Payments Inc.
- **Published fee schedule (from Apple Cash Terms and Conditions):**
  - **Monthly fee:** **$0**; **per-purchase fee** (via Apple Pay): **$0**; **customer service fee:** **$0**; **inactivity fee:** **$0**; **overdraft/credit feature:** none.
  - **Sending and receiving personal payments** funded by Apple Cash balance or a debit card in Wallet: **$0**.
  - **Standard Automated Clearing House (ACH) bank transfer:** **$0** (typically **1 to 3 business days**).
  - **Instant transfer to an eligible debit card:** **1.7%** of the transfer amount, rounded to the nearest cent, with a **$0.25 minimum** and **$25.00 maximum** (the **only** fee listed on Apple Cash's fee disclosure table).
- **Where it falls short vs Venmo:** Works **only on Apple devices** (an Android user or Windows desktop browser user cannot send or receive Apple Cash), **does not allow credit card funding** for person-to-person sends (requires an Apple Cash balance or debit card in Wallet), and has no business profile or Tap to Pay merchant profile built into consumer Apple Cash.
- **Worked arithmetic:** An instant debit-card cash-out of **$800** costs **$13.60** on Apple Cash (**1.7% of $800**) versus **$14.00** on Venmo (**1.75% of $800**), a **$0.40** saving; both hit their **$25.00** fee cap above **$1,470.59** on Apple Cash and **$1,428.58** on Venmo.

### Alternative 4: Wise (`https://wise.com/us/pricing/`, checked November 15, 2026)
- International multi-currency account and money-transfer service built around the **mid-market exchange rate** with no monthly subscription fees.
- **Published personal pricing:**
  - **Account registration & holding money:** **Free ($0)**; sending and receiving the **same currency between Wise contacts** is **free ($0)**.
  - **Sending or converting currencies:** **From 0.23%** (varies by currency pair), using the mid-market rate rather than embedding a 3% to 4% exchange-rate spread.
  - **Receiving money:** Free local account details to receive in **22 currencies**; free non-wire domestic payments in **9 currencies** (AUD, CAD, EUR, GBP, HUF, NZD, PHP, SGD, USD); receiving a **USD wire or Society for Worldwide Interbank Financial Telecommunication (SWIFT) payment** costs a fixed **$6.11 USD** (2.16 GBP for GBP SWIFT, 2.39 EUR for EUR SWIFT).
  - **Wise Multi-Currency Card:** **$9 USD** one-time card fee ($0 monthly subscription); **$0** to spend held currencies in-store or online; **ATM withdrawals free up to $250 USD per calendar month**, then **$1.95 USD + 1.95%** per withdrawal; **2%** fee when topping up certain external e-wallets.
- **Where it falls short vs Venmo:** Less suited to casual US group bill-splitting among friends who do not hold Wise accounts, charges **$9 USD** upfront for its physical debit card (vs **$0** for the Venmo Debit Card), and caps fee-free ATM withdrawals at **$250/month** (whereas Venmo Debit Card charges $0 across all US MoneyPass ATMs).
- **Worked arithmetic:** Sending **$1,000** abroad where a currency conversion applies: on Venmo (to an eligible non-US PayPal account), you pay the **$4.99** capped international send fee plus up to a **4.00%** currency conversion spread (**$40.00**), totaling **$44.99**. On Wise at its starting **0.23%** conversion/sending rate with the mid-market rate, **$1,000** costs **$2.30** (higher on pricier currency corridors, shown upfront before sending).

### Alternative 5: Square (`https://squareup.com/us/en/pricing`, checked November 15, 2026)
- Point of Sale (POS), invoicing, and business banking platform from Block, Inc. (banking via Square Financial Services, Inc. or Sutton Bank, Members FDIC) for freelancers, sole proprietors, and shops comparing against **Venmo Business Profiles**.
- **Published plans & payment fees:**
  - **Monthly software tiers:** **Square Free** at **$0/month** per location; **Square Plus** at **$49/month** per location (30-day free trial); **Square Premium** at **$149/month** per location (30-day free trial; custom pricing available above $250,000/year in card volume).
  - **In-person card processing (tap, dip, or swipe):** **2.6% + $0.15** on Square Free, **2.5% + $0.15** on Square Plus, **2.4% + $0.15** on Square Premium.
  - **Online / invoice card payments:** **3.3% + $0.30** on Square Free, **2.9% + $0.30** on Square Plus and Square Premium.
  - **ACH bank transfer via invoice:** **1%** (**$1 minimum**; uncapped on Square Free, **$10 maximum fee cap** on Square Plus and Square Premium; **$5 cap** via API).
  - **Payouts & banking:** Next-business-day external bank transfer is **free ($0)**; **Square Checking** provides instant access to sales on a free business debit card with $0 monthly fees, and **Square Savings** pays **1.00% APY** with $0 fees or minimums (instant transfer to an external bank carries a fee, dollar percentage not itemized on `/pricing`). International card surcharge is **1.5%**; optional payroll starts at **$35/month + $6 per person paid**; POS hardware starts at **$59**.
- **Where it falls short vs Venmo:** Not a consumer friend-to-friend bill-splitting app, and in-person card acceptance on Square Free (**2.6% + $0.15**) or online invoice card acceptance (**3.3% + $0.30**) costs more per transaction than a standard QR/in-app **Venmo Business Profile** payment (**1.9% + $0.10**) or Venmo Tap to Pay (**2.29% + $0.09**).
- **Worked arithmetic:** A vendor collecting **100 in-person $50 customer payments** ($5,000/month) pays **1.9% + $0.10** on a Venmo Business Profile = **$95 + $10 = $105.00/month** (or **$114.50 + $9.00 = $123.50/month** via Venmo Tap to Pay at 2.29% + $0.09), versus **$130 + $15 = $145.00/month** on Square Free tap/dip/swipe (2.6% + $0.15) — though Square lets customers pay with any Visa, Mastercard, American Express, or Discover card without needing a Venmo account, and a single **$2,000** client invoice paid by ACH on Square Free costs **1% = $20.00** versus **$38.10** on Venmo Business (1.9% + $0.10).

### Alternative 6: Xoom (`https://www.xoom.com/`, checked November 15, 2026)
- PayPal's dedicated international money-transfer service (operated by PayPal, Inc., NMLS ID 910457, serving customers since 2001).
- **What it supports:** Cross-border transfers to **foreign bank accounts and debit cards**, **mobile wallets** (including India's Unified Payments Interface (UPI) and Brazil's PIX instant payment system), **cash pickup and home cash delivery**, international bill pay, and mobile phone reloads.
- **How fees are published:** Xoom publishes **no fixed fee table or transfer limits on its homepage**; instead, Xoom states `"We'll show you the exact fee and the amount they'll receive before you hit send"` and discloses that fees vary by destination country, feature selected, and funding method, and that **Xoom also makes money when converting your send currency into a different currency**. Because no fee percentages or flat dollar amounts appear on the homepage checked, no worked cost example is calculated for Xoom.
- **Where it falls short vs Venmo:** Built for cross-border remittances rather than free domestic US friend-to-friend splitting, and unlike Venmo's itemized fee table (`venmo.com/about/fees/`), Xoom requires entering a destination country and transfer amount in its calculator to see a price.
