### Subject: TurboTax (`https://turbotax.intuit.com/personal-taxes/online/` and `/personal-taxes/cd-download/`, checked November 15, 2026)
- Sold by Intuit Inc. Online personal tax filing is divided into three service levels based on return complexity and expert involvement:
  1. **TurboTax Do It Yourself** (step-by-step online tools): **$0 to $139** for federal filing; state returns cost extra on paid products. Includes **TurboTax Free Edition** (**$0 federal + $0 state + $0 to file**) for simple Form 1040 returns only; TurboTax states roughly **37%** of taxpayers are eligible.
     - Simple Form 1040 returns cover W-2 income, interest or dividends (Forms 1099-INT, 1099-DIV, 1099-OID) that do not require Schedule B, Earned Income Tax Credit (EITC), Child Tax Credit, student loan interest, taxable qualified retirement plan distributions, and Schedule 1-A deductions (qualified tips, overtime pay, car loan interest, and seniors age 65 and older).
     - Simple Form 1040 returns do NOT cover itemized deductions on Schedule A (mortgage interest, charitable contributions, medical expenses, state and local taxes), investment or stock sales, rental property, or Schedule C self-employment income (covered in Do It Yourself Deluxe and Do It Yourself Premium, formerly Self-Employed). If you start in Free Edition and add an uncovered form such as investment sales, TurboTax requires upgrading to a paid tier.
  2. **TurboTax Expert Assist** (file yourself with unlimited on-demand tax expert advice via phone, chat, or one-way screen-share video from Intuit experts averaging 12 years of experience, plus a final expert review before filing; includes a check of 2024 taxes for missed breaks at no extra cost): **$59 to $209** for federal filing; state returns are additional. Tiers named on page: Expert Assist Basic, Expert Assist Deluxe, Expert Assist Premium (formerly Self-Employed).
  3. **TurboTax Expert Full Service** (matched dedicated tax expert prepares, signs, and files the return from start to finish online or in person where available): **starting at $129** for federal filing (starting price includes W-2 and Form 1040; final price depends on actual tax situation and forms used, confirmed before the expert starts); state returns are additional.
- **State return prices:** TurboTax's online overview page states `"State additional"` on paid Do It Yourself, Expert Assist, and Expert Full Service tiers without listing a single fixed dollar figure per state on that overview page (quote-based by tier/return at print or e-file; Free Edition state is $0 for qualifying simple returns).
- **Payment and refund-deduction fee:** Pay only when you print or e-file. Paying TurboTax fees out of a federal refund carries an additional **$40** Refund Processing Service fee for individual returns.
- **TurboTax Desktop (download software):** Separate downloadable software line that includes up to **5 federal e-files**; Deluxe and Premier include **1 state download** (state e-file fee sold separately), while Desktop Business (for partnerships, S corporations, C corporations, multi-member Limited Liability Companies (LLCs), and trusts or estates) sells state downloads separately.
- **Guarantees & audit support:** Free one-on-one audit guidance year-round from tax staff; Maximum Refund / 100% Accurate Calculations guarantee (Free Edition customers receive $30 if another method yields a larger refund or smaller tax due on an amended return; seven-year return lifetime). Access to up to 7 years of prior TurboTax returns on file through December 31, 2026.

### Alternative 1: TaxAct (`https://www.taxact.com/taxes-online`, checked November 15, 2026)
- Online consumer Form 1040 filing structured into three main service paths:
  1. **Do It Yourself:** **Starting at $0.00** federal (+ state additional). **TaxAct Free Edition** ($0 federal, state additional) covers simple Form 1040 returns without attached forms or schedules; TaxAct states about **40%** of users qualify. Paid DIY tiers named on the page are **Deluxe**, **Premier**, and **Self-Employed** (exact upper DIY tier dollar prices and per-state dollar fees are not itemized on `/taxes-online`; paid prices are charged only when printing or e-filing).
  2. **DIY with Expert Help (Xpert Assist):** **Starting at $45.00** (also shown as starting at **$54.00** in the comparison table on the same page, + state additional), providing unlimited live chat and phone help from credentialed tax experts plus a quick expert review before filing. TaxAct's FAQ on the same page also states that standalone **Xpert Assist** can be added to consumer 1040 products (Free, Deluxe, Premier, Self-Employed) for **$60**.
  3. **Xpert Full Service** ("Expert handles everything"): **Starting at $99.00** federal (+ state additional), matching the filer with a dedicated credentialed tax expert via video/phone consultations to prepare and file the return.
- **Guarantees & add-ons:** **$100k Accuracy Guarantee** (reimburses software costs plus up to **$100,000** in refund difference or tax liability difference caused by a software calculation error); Refund Transfer option to pay fees from a federal refund; E-file Concierge phone status notifications; optional 3-year Audit Defense add-on.
- **Worked arithmetic:** For a filer who wants full-service preparation on a straightforward W-2 and Form 1040 return before state fees, TaxAct Xpert Full Service starts at **$99** federal versus **$129** federal on TurboTax Expert Full Service, a **$30** lower federal starting price (state fees are extra on both). For live expert review on DIY, TaxAct starts at **$45 to $54** federal (or a **$60** add-on on Free Edition) compared with **$59** federal at the bottom of TurboTax Expert Assist.

### Alternative 2: 1040.com (`https://www.1040.com/`, checked November 15, 2026)
- Operated by 1040.com, A Taxwell Company; Internal Revenue Service (IRS)-authorized e-file provider.
- **Published price:** **$34.99 flat rate** total for **federal AND state** tax returns combined (pay only when you file). Note: TaxAct's sibling page displays a cross-promotion banner mentioning `"$55 flat"` for new customers filing by May 1, whereas 1040.com's own homepage publishes **$34.99** flat for federal and state returns across the page. State both facts transparently.
- **What the $34.99 flat rate includes:** All supported forms and schedules from simple W-2 returns to complex returns at one price — including **multiple state returns** at no extra charge (if you moved during the year), **Schedule C** self-employment / contractor / small-business income, **Health Savings Account (HSA)** forms, charitable donations, home ownership or home sale, student credits, Social Security and retirement income, unemployment, investments, **stock and cryptocurrency sales**, rental properties, and filing an April 15 tax extension (to submit by October 15).
- **Where it falls short vs TurboTax:** No free $0 tier even for a bare Form 1040 (every filer pays the flat rate), and 1040.com's homepage lists self-guided step-by-step online filing and knowledge-base support rather than dedicated full-service CPA/enrolled-agent handoff or live screen-sharing tiers like TurboTax Expert Assist and Expert Full Service.
- **Worked arithmetic:** A freelancer with 1099-NEC Schedule C income, stock sales, and **2 state returns** (after moving mid-year) pays **$34.99 total** on 1040.com's published homepage rate ($34.99 x 1 = $34.99, with $0 extra for Schedule C and $0 extra for both states). On TurboTax Online, Schedule C requires upgrading out of Free Edition into paid Do It Yourself (up to **$139** federal) plus two separate paid state returns on top.

### Alternative 3: OLT — Online Taxes (`https://www.olt.com/main/home/pricing.php`, checked November 15, 2026)
- Full name on first mention: **Online Taxes (OLT)**.
- **Published plans (two tiers, all forms included in both):**
  1. **Federal Free Edition:** **$0** for a federal return + **$9.95** per state return. Unlike TurboTax Free Edition (which restricts $0 filing to simple Form 1040 returns), OLT's $0 Federal Free Edition explicitly includes **simple and complex returns**, home ownership deductions (mortgage interest and property taxes), **cryptocurrency and stock sales** (dividends and capital gains), **rental property income** and depreciation, **Schedule C self-employed / freelance** and small-business forms, retirement income (Social Security, pensions, 401(k) distributions, Individual Retirement Account (IRA) withdrawals), prior-year data import, Artificial Intelligence (AI) W-2 and 1099 import, and email customer support.
  2. **Premium Edition:** **$7.95** for a federal return + **$7.95** per state return (discounted by **$2** from the $9.95 regular state price). Includes every form in Free plus phone and live chat customer support, audit support, year-over-year return comparison, and priority processing.
- **Where it falls short vs TurboTax:** No full-service tax preparer option where a dedicated tax professional prepares and signs your return for you, and phone or live chat support requires the $7.95 Premium Edition (the $0 tier provides email support only).
- **Worked arithmetic:** A self-employed filer or investor with stock sales and **1 state return** pays **$0 + $9.95 = $9.95 total** on OLT Federal Free Edition, or **$7.95 + $7.95 = $15.90 total** on OLT Premium Edition (only **$5.95** more than Free Edition once the $2 state discount is counted). A filer in a state with no state income tax return pays **$0.00** on OLT Federal Free Edition even with Schedule C or rental property.

### Alternative 4: Jackson Hewitt Online (`https://www.jacksonhewitt.com/file-taxes-online/`, checked November 15, 2026)
- Online Do It Yourself tax-filing product from Jackson Hewitt.
- **Published price:** **$25 flat fee** total to file **federal AND state** tax returns online (backed by its Transparent Pricing Guarantee stating you pay no more than **$25** in online tax prep fees, or Jackson Hewitt refunds the difference). Pay $25 only when you file.
- **What is included:** Simple or more complex DIY returns on desktop and mobile, W-2 and unemployment income, **multi-state filers** included in the same $25 flat fee, federal and state extension filing, **free live chat support**, Maximum Refund Guarantee, and Worry-Free Tax Protection (up to **$1,500**).
- **Where it falls short vs TurboTax:** No $0 free tier for simple W-2 filers (a simple Form 1040 filer who qualifies for TurboTax Free Edition pays $0 on TurboTax vs $25 on Jackson Hewitt Online), and the $25 online flat rate covers self-prepared DIY returns rather than TurboTax's online screen-share Expert Assist ($59 to $209) or online Full Service ($129+); filers wanting a Jackson Hewitt Tax Pro must use its separate assisted/in-office services rather than the $25 DIY package.
- **Worked arithmetic:** A household with itemized deductions or two state returns pays **$25 total** ($25 federal + $0 extra for state returns) on Jackson Hewitt Online, saving **$9.99** compared with 1040.com's **$34.99** flat rate and up to **$114** before state fees compared with the **$139** top end of TurboTax Do It Yourself federal pricing. Conversely, a single W-2 filer with a simple Form 1040 pays **$25 more** on Jackson Hewitt Online than on TurboTax Free Edition ($0).

### Alternative 5: ezTaxReturn (`https://www.eztaxreturn.com/`, checked November 15, 2026)
- IRS-authorized online tax e-file provider operating since 2000 (over 25 years), available in English and Spanish with free 7-day US-based callback and email customer support.
- **Published prices:**
  - **Free Federal Tax Filing:** **$0** federal return for qualifying simple returns (state return additional at **$45.99**).
  - **Paid Federal Tax Return** (when you do not qualify for a simple return): **$44.99** federal.
  - **State Tax Return:** **$45.99** state prep and e-file.
  - **Fed & State Tax Returns combined:** **$90.98** ($44.99 + $45.99).
  - **Optional paid add-ons:** Audit defense protection **$42.99**; Amended tax return insurance **$12.99**; Refund transfer (deducting filing fees from your IRS refund via TPG bank) **$39.95** on top of DIY filing fees.
- **Where it falls short vs TurboTax:** Adding a state return ($45.99) plus paid federal ($44.99) brings the DIY base total to **$90.98** — more than triple Jackson Hewitt Online ($25) and 2.6x 1040.com ($34.99) — and ezTaxReturn offers callback/email customer support rather than a dedicated preparer signing your return like TurboTax Expert Full Service. Even on a $0 simple federal return, adding a state return on ezTaxReturn costs **$45.99**, whereas TurboTax Free Edition includes $0 state for qualifying simple returns.
- **Worked arithmetic:** A non-simple filer submitting 1 federal return ($44.99) and 1 state return ($45.99) pays **$90.98**. Adding Audit defense protection ($42.99) and Refund transfer ($39.95) raises the total to **$173.92** ($90.98 + $42.99 + $39.95), illustrating how optional add-ons nearly double the headline software cost.

### Alternative 6: Keeper (`https://www.keepertax.com/pricing`, checked November 15, 2026)
- Year-round bookkeeping, expense deduction tracking, quarterly estimated tax scheduling, and tax-filing subscription app tailored to 1099 freelancers, contractors, and business owners. Every plan starts with a **14-day free trial** and can be canceled anytime.
- **Published plans (four tiers):**
  1. **Bookkeeping Only:** **$20/month** (**$240/year** over 12 months) — tracks expenses and finds deductions year-round, **does NOT include tax filing**.
  2. **Standard:** **$199/year** (equivalent to **$16.58/month** billed yearly) — includes year-round bookkeeping, quarterly tax estimates, expert guidance, and annual tax filing for **federal plus up to 2 state returns** ($41/year cheaper than paying $20/month for 12 months of Bookkeeping Only without filing).
  3. **Premium:** **$399/year** (equivalent to **$33.25/month** billed yearly) — adds **multi-state returns (3 or more states)** and expanded investment/deduction coverage alongside federal and state filing.
  4. **Business:** **$1,199/year** (equivalent to **$99.92/month** billed yearly) — covers S corporations, partnerships, and multi-state business/personal filing (3+ states).
- **Where it falls short vs TurboTax:** Much higher upfront annual cost ($199 to $1,199 billed yearly) than DIY tax software if you only have W-2 wages and do not need year-round 1099 expense tracking, and there is no $0 free filing plan beyond the 14-day trial.
- **Worked arithmetic:** Over 12 months, Keeper Bookkeeping Only at **$20/month** costs **$240/year** with zero tax returns included, whereas Keeper Standard costs **$199/year** ($41 less) and includes federal plus up to **2 state returns**. Compared with TurboTax Do It Yourself at up to **$139** federal plus two extra paid state returns (and no year-round bookkeeping included), Keeper Standard bundles both bookkeeping and two-state filing at a known **$199** annual figure.
