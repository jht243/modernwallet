### Subject: Robinhood (`https://robinhood.com/us/en/`, `https://robinhood.com/us/en/gold/`, `https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/trading-fees-on-robinhood/`, `https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/margin-rates/`, checked November 15, 2026)
- **Standard brokerage account ($0/month):** **$0** commission on online US stocks, Exchange-Traded Funds (ETFs), and stock/ETF options; **$0** fee to open or maintain an account; **$0** account minimum; fractional shares and cryptocurrency purchases starting at **$1**.
- **Options and regulatory passthrough fees:** Effective January 10, 2025, Robinhood collects a combined **$0.04** fee per options contract traded to cover Options Clearing Corporation (OCC) clearing and Options Regulatory Fee (ORF) costs. Consolidated Audit Trail (CAT) fee is **$0.0003** per options contract, **$0.000003** per share of listed equities, and **$0.00000003** per share of Over-The-Counter (OTC) equities (waived if under $0.01). Financial Industry Regulatory Authority (FINRA) Trading Activity Fee (TAF) of $0.000195 per share (waived for sales of 50 shares or less) and $0.00329 per options contract sell (capped at $9.79 per trade) is temporarily paused from October 1, 2026 through December 31, 2026 per FINRA. American Depositary Receipts (ADRs) carry bank custodial fees typically ranging from **$0.01 to $0.03** per share.
- **Index options & futures fees:** Without Gold, index options cost **$0.50** per contract (plus exchange fees and the $0.04 regulatory/clearing fee). With Gold, index options cost **$0.35** per contract, and futures cost **$0.50** per contract. Robinhood's own published example for 12 SPXW index options contracts (premium under $1, $0.50 exchange fee): **$10.68** total with Gold ($4.20 contract fee + $6.00 exchange fee + $0.48 clearing/regulatory) vs **$12.48** total without Gold ($6.00 contract fee + $6.00 exchange fee + $0.48 clearing/regulatory).
- **Robinhood Gold subscription:** **$5/month** (**$60/year**) after a **30-day free trial**, offered by Robinhood Gold, LLC:
  - **3.6% Annual Percentage Yield (APY)** on eligible uninvested brokerage cash with no cap and no minimum.
  - **3% match** on annual Individual Retirement Account (IRA) contributions (up to **$225** based on the 2026 contribution limit; requires staying subscribed to Gold for **1 year** and keeping the matched assets in the IRA for **5 years** to retain the full 3% match).
  - First **$1,000** of margin borrowing included at $0 interest.
  - **Robinhood Strategies** (SEC-registered investment advisor managed portfolios): **$0 management fees** on every dollar above **$100,000** for Gold members (base advisory percentage under $100,000 is not itemized on the Gold overview page).
  - **Robinhood Gold Card** (subject to credit approval, issued by Coastal Community Bank): 3% cash back across categories when redeemed into a Robinhood brokerage account.
- **Margin interest rates (settled balances, calculated on 360-day year):**
  - Up to $50,000: **5.25%**
  - $50,000 up to $100,000: **5.05%**
  - $100,000 up to $1 million: **4.75%**
  - $1 million up to $10 million: **4.5%**
  - $10 million up to $50 million: **4.45%**
  - $50 million+: **4.20%**
  - Published example on $3,000 settled margin at 5.25%: **$0.44/day** without Gold ($3,000 x 5.25% / 360), or **$0.29/day** with Gold ($2,000 x 5.25% / 360, since the first $1,000 is included).

### Alternative 1: Charles Schwab (`https://www.schwab.com/pricing`, checked November 15, 2026)
- **$0** online US stock and ETF trade commissions; **$0** minimum investment to open a brokerage or IRA account (Traditional, Roth, Rollover, Inherited IRA, SEP-IRA, SIMPLE-IRA).
- **Options & futures:** **$0** base commission + **$0.65 per options contract** ($0 commission for exercised and assigned options); **$2.25 per contract** for futures and futures options.
- **Other securities & broker-assisted fees:** **$6.95** online commission for Over-The-Counter (OTC) equities; **$50** foreign stock transaction fee online; **$0** online for US Treasuries; **$1 per bond** for secondary corporate and municipal bonds (**$10 minimum**, **$250 maximum**); up to **$74.95** per purchase on transaction-fee mutual funds ($0 on no-load, no-transaction-fee mutual funds); **$25** service charge added to broker-assisted phone trades.
- **Automated & managed investing:** **Schwab Intelligent Portfolios** charges **$0 advisory fee** and $0 commissions with a **$5,000** minimum investment; wealth advisory tiers with human advisors start at $25,000 to $2,000,000 minimums with advisory fees starting from 0.40% to 0.95% depending on program. Margin interest rates and cash sweep APYs are not itemized on `/pricing`.
- **Worked arithmetic:** An options trader buying and selling **20 equity options contracts per month** (240 contracts a year) pays **$156.00/year** in contract fees at Charles Schwab (240 x $0.65), compared with **$9.60/year** in combined $0.04 regulatory/clearing fees on Robinhood ($0 contract commission, 240 x $0.04), a **$146.40/year** difference before exchange passthroughs. Conversely, a passive investor putting $10,000 into Schwab Intelligent Portfolios pays **$0/year** in advisory and subscription fees with no $5/month membership required.

### Alternative 2: Interactive Brokers (`https://www.interactivebrokers.com/en/pricing/commissions-home.php`, checked November 15, 2026)
- Two account plans with **$0 minimum balance** and **$0 monthly maintenance fee** on both, plus multi-currency deposits and trading in up to **29 currencies**:
  1. **IBKR Lite** (for eligible US and Singapore retail investors on Individual, Joint, IRA, and natural-person Trust accounts): **$0 commissions** on US exchange-listed stocks and ETFs.
  2. **IBKR Pro** (for active traders wanting smart order routing and global markets): **Fixed** pricing at **$0.005 per share** (**$1.00** minimum per order) or **Tiered** pricing at **$0.0005 to $0.0035 per share** (**$0.35** minimum per order, plus exchange/clearing/regulatory passthroughs or rebates).
- **Options, futures & funds:** Options commissions run **$0.15 to $0.65 per contract** (tiered by volume; **$0.65** standard on Fixed/Lite, $1.00 order minimum applies on applicable schedules); futures commissions run **$0.25 to $0.85 per contract** (**$0.85** Fixed); over **150 ETFs** in its fee-waived program and no-transaction-fee mutual funds at **$0**.
- **Where it falls short vs Robinhood:** Equity options carry a **$0.15 to $0.65** per-contract commission (vs $0 commission + $0.04 combined fee on Robinhood), non-retail order patterns on IBKR Lite can be routed to Fixed commission pricing, and Interactive Brokers offers no IRA contribution match like Robinhood Gold's 3% match. Exact dollar margin rate percentages and uninvested cash APY percentages are referenced on separate rate subpages rather than printed in the `/commissions-home.php` overview table.
- **Worked arithmetic:** An active futures trader executing **50 futures contracts a month** (600 contracts a year) on Robinhood Gold pays **$5/month** ($60/year) plus **$0.50 per contract** ($300/year) = **$360/year**. On IBKR Pro Fixed at **$0.85 per contract** with $0 subscription, 600 contracts cost **$510/year** ($150 more), whereas a high-volume trader reaching IBKR Tiered's **$0.25 per contract** bottom tier pays **$150/year** before exchange fees.

### Alternative 3: E*TRADE from Morgan Stanley (`https://us.etrade.com/what-we-offer/pricing-and-rates`, checked November 15, 2026)
- **$0** online commissions for US-listed stocks, ETFs, and no-load no-transaction-fee mutual funds; **$0** minimum initial deposit and **$0** monthly account fee (unfunded new accounts close after 90 days; $2.00 quarterly paper statement fee applies unless waived via electronic statements or $10,000+ combined balance).
- **Options & futures:** **$0.65 per options contract** standard, dropping to **$0.50 per contract** for customers executing **30 or more** stock, ETF, and options trades per quarter; **$0 contract fee** when closing short options priced at **$0.10 or less**; **$1.50 per contract, per side** for standard futures (**$0.75** per contract, per side for E-nano Equity Index futures), plus exchange fees.
- **Bonds, OTC, managed portfolios & banking:** **$1 per bond** online for secondary corporate/municipal bonds (**$10 minimum**, **$250 maximum**); **$6.95** OTC stock commission (**$4.95** at 30+ trades/quarter); **Core Portfolios** automated robo-advisor charges a **0.30% annual advisory fee** with a **$500** minimum; **Premium Savings Account** advertises a **4.25% Promotional APY** for **6 months** on new accounts (reverting to variable Standard APY thereafter); **$75** fee for a full outgoing account transfer ($0 partial transfer, $25 outgoing wire).
- **Where it falls short vs Robinhood:** Higher options per-contract fees ($0.50 to $0.65 vs $0 commission + $0.04 fee on Robinhood), higher standard futures commissions ($1.50 per side vs $0.50 with Robinhood Gold), and its 4.25% savings rate on the pricing page is a 6-month promotional APY rather than an ongoing brokerage sweep rate.
- **Worked arithmetic:** A trader who qualifies for E*TRADE's 30-trades-per-quarter tier and trades **50 options contracts per month** (600 a year) pays **$300/year** at $0.50 per contract (or **$390/year** at the standard $0.65 rate if under 30 trades per quarter), compared with **$24/year** in $0.04 combined fees on Robinhood. On E*TRADE Core Portfolios, a **$10,000** automated account costs **0.30%**, or **$30/year**.

### Alternative 4: Webull (`https://www.webull.com/pricing`, checked November 15, 2026)
- **$0** commission on online US exchange-listed stock, ETF, and equity options trades; **$0** deposit minimum and **$0** account maintenance fee.
- **Index options, OTC, routing & transfer fees:** **$0.50 per contract** fee for certain index option trades; regulatory passthroughs apply (SEC fee **$0.0000206** x sale volume; CAT fee **$0.000003** x trade volume on equities/options and **$0.00000003** on OTC); low-priced OTC trades exceeding 100,000 shares under $1.00/share cost **$0.0002 per share** (max 5% of principal); foreign-settlement F-stocks cost **$5.00 per buy** and **$0.05 per sell**; optional Equity Algo Order Types cost **$0.0050 per share** and Direct Routing costs **$0.0040 per share** ($0.01 minimum per order); **$75** fee per outgoing stock transfer ($0 incoming). Note: cash management APY, margin interest rates, and Webull Premium subscription pricing are not printed on the `/pricing` overview table (`-- APY` placeholder shown on the page checked).
- **Where it falls short vs Robinhood:** Webull's `/pricing` page does not publish its cash APY or margin rate table directly on that page, charges **$0.50** per index options contract without a $0.35 Gold equivalent shown on `/pricing`, and lists a **$75** outgoing stock transfer fee via Apex Clearing.
- **Worked arithmetic:** For a trader executing **100 US stock options contracts per month** (1,200 contracts a year) using standard order routing, Webull charges **$0** in broker options commissions (only regulatory passthroughs), whereas Robinhood collects **$0.04** per contract in combined clearing/ORF fees (**$48.00/year** on 1,200 contracts) and Schwab or E*TRADE (under 30 trades/quarter) charges **$780.00/year** at $0.65 per contract.

### Alternative 5: tastytrade (`https://tastytrade.com/pricing/`, checked November 15, 2026)
- Built for derivatives traders with an asymmetric **"pay to open, $0 to close"** fee schedule and **$0** commissions on opening and closing stock and ETF trades:
  - **Stock & ETF options:** **$1.00 per contract to open**, capped at **$10 maximum per leg**, and **$0.00 per contract to close** (plus exchange, clearing, and regulatory fees).
  - **Broad-based index options:** **$1.00 per contract to open** (uncapped) and **$0.00 per contract to close**.
  - **Futures:** **$1.00 per contract** per side (open and close) for E-mini futures; **$0.75 per contract** per side for Micro E-mini and E-nano futures.
  - **Options on futures:** **$1.25 per contract** per side on E-mini futures options; **$0.75 per contract** per side on Micro E-mini and E-nano futures options.
  - **Cryptocurrency:** **0.75%** to open and **0.75%** to close.
  - **Margin promotion:** Advertises one flat margin rate for new margin accounts or $25,000+ in new assets (exact base margin rate table is not itemized on `/pricing/`).
- **Where it falls short vs Robinhood:** Opening small 1- to 5-contract equity option trades costs **$1.00 per contract** ($0.50 per contract round-trip) vs Robinhood's $0 commission ($0.04 combined fee per contract), futures round-trips cost $1.50 to $2.00 ($0.75 to $1.00 per side) vs $1.00 round-trip ($0.50 per side) on Robinhood Gold, and tastytrade lists no uninvested cash sweep APY or IRA contribution match on `/pricing/`.
- **Worked arithmetic:** Because tastytrade caps equity option opening commissions at **$10 per leg** and charges **$0 to close**, a single-leg order opening and later closing **50 contracts** costs **$10.00 total** in tastytrade commissions ($10 to open + $0 to close = **$0.10 per contract** across the 100 contract sides), whereas 50 single-contract orders opened separately cost **$50.00** ($1 x 50). On Robinhood, 100 total contract sides (50 open + 50 close) carry $0 commission plus **$4.00** in $0.04 combined clearing/ORF fees.

### Alternative 6: Public (`https://public.com/` and `https://public.com/disclosures`, checked November 15, 2026)
- Self-directed multi-asset investing app (Open to the Public Investing, Inc., FINRA/SIPC member up to $500,000 including $250,000 for cash claims) offering US stocks, ETFs, options, bonds, Treasuries, Crypto IRA, direct indexing, and an open trading Application Programming Interface (API).
- **Published rates and pricing on homepage:**
  - **Options Trading Rebate Program:** Instead of charging a per-contract commission, Public pays enrolled users a **rebate of $0.06 to $0.18 per stock or ETF options contract** traded, depending on monthly trading volume, underlying security, and whether placed via API.
  - **High-yield cash:** **3.30% APY** on cash with **no subscription fees** (and **3.75% APY** advertised for higher-tier accounts / $500,000+ exclusive program).
  - **Margin investing:** Base margin interest rate of **5.15%** across any account balance (compared against Robinhood's **5.25%** tier up to $50,000).
  - **Direct indexing:** Tax-loss harvesting across 100+ customizable indices starting at a **$1,000** minimum.
- **Where it falls short vs Robinhood:** Public's 3.30% fee-free cash APY is 0.30 percentage points lower than Robinhood Gold's 3.6% APY, Public does not publish futures trading commissions on its homepage (offering event contracts instead), and it does not advertise a 3% IRA contribution match on its homepage.
- **Worked arithmetic:** Holding **$10,000** in uninvested cash for a year on Public at **3.30% APY** with $0 subscription fees yields **$330.00**, whereas **$10,000** on Robinhood Gold at **3.6% APY** yields **$360.00** minus the **$60.00** annual Gold subscription (**$5/month x 12**) = **$300.00 net** — meaning Public nets **$30 more per year** on a $10,000 cash balance, while Robinhood Gold pulls ahead above **$20,000** in cash ($720 interest minus $60 fee = $660 vs $660 at 3.30%).
