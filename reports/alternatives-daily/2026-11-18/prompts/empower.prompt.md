## CLOSED FACT LIST (only these facts; checked 2026-11-18 on each vendor's own site)
Subject: Empower (empower.com)
- Empower pages describe personal investors, workplace retirement, employers/plan sponsors, financial professionals. Site says it helps "over 20 million people".
- Free financial tools on the personal dashboard: Retirement Planner, Net Worth, Budgeting & Cash Flow, Portfolio Analysis, Savings Planner, Debt Paydown, Emergency Fund, Transactions. The wealth-management page offers a free portfolio analysis with no obligation to work with Empower.
- Wealth Management: Personal Strategy = 1:1 work with a financial advisor backed by specialists, custom portfolio, tax optimization, financial planning; available when you invest $100,000 or more. Private Client = premium planning and portfolio management for $1,000,000+ in investments (incl. private equity, tax and legacy planning).
- Advisory fee: Empower states advisory fees are calculated based on the amount of assets managed, detailed in the Empower Advisory Group, LLC Form ADV; the pages read do not show a percentage. Write "Fee set by assets managed; percentage is in the Form ADV, not shown on the pages read" (quote-based on the page, no public price figure).
- Empower Personal Cash: high-yield cash account, no account fees, no minimums, direct deposit, aggregate FDIC insurance up to $5 million; standard APY 3.50% (as of 9/17/26; interest rate 3.4451%); 3.70% APY for eligible Personal Strategy Private Client customers (as of 10/14/2026); APY variable, set by UMB. 
- Investment accounts: commission-free trading, "low fees", managed accounts, mutual funds, ETFs, equities. IRAs and rollover IRAs offered. Tax filing via dashboard.
- Empower Advisory Group, LLC is an SEC-registered investment adviser. Investing involves risk.
Alternatives:
1. Monarch Money (monarchmoney.com): Core $99.99 per year or $14.99 per month; Plus $199.99 per year. Plus adds forecasting, multiple income source tracking, deeper investment tools. Money-back guarantee; says it is subscription based and ad-free; new-user code gives 30% off Core annual for one year (promotional). Household/family sharing noted by a reviewer quote only - do not state as feature.
2. YNAB (ynab.com): $14.99 per month or $109 per year ($9.08 a month equivalent); 34-day free trial. Bank import supports select US, Canadian, UK and EU banks.
3. Quicken Simplifi (simplifimoney.com): $3.99 per month shown ("billed annually"); $6.99 per month also shown; features: Spending Plan, 12-month projected cash flow, investments tracking, retirement planning, savings goals, net worth tracking, Watchlist categories; web and mobile.
4. Betterment (betterment.com/pricing): investing accounts $5 per month base price, or 0.25% annual on balance when you set up recurring deposits of $200+/month or reach $24,000 balance; Premium 0.65% annual (0.25% digital base plus 0.40% Premium) on first $1M, requires $100,000 minimum, access to financial experts; lower tiers above $1M (0.15% and 0.10% above $2M on those portions); Cash Reserve APY 3.50% as of 9/21/2026 plus 0.75% boost for new clients with qualifying deposit up to $1M (4.25%); no fees or minimums on cash account; FDIC up to $8M joint; solo 401(k), IRA and 401(k) rollovers.
5. Wealthfront (wealthfront.com/pricing): 0.25% annual advisory fee for the Wealthfront Portfolio; example on its page: $15,000 balance = $3.18 a month; tax-loss harvesting software; brokerage account also offered for ETFs and stocks; cash, invest, borrow.
6. Fidelity Go (fidelity.com): $0 advisory fee under $25,000; 0.35% annual advisory fee at $25,000 and above (adds coaching sessions, and tax-loss harvesting for taxable accounts); no minimum to open, invest from $10; no trading fees; 401(k) rollovers and IRA transfers supported.
