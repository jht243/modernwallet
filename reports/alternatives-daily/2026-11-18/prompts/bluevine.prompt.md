### Bluevine (subject)
- Bluevine says it is a financial technology company, not a bank. Deposits are FDIC-insured through Coastal Community Bank, Member FDIC, and its program banks. Bluevine advertises FDIC protection up to $3 million per depositor ($3,000,000 is reached by spreading deposits across multiple banks, multiplying the standard $250,000 coverage).
- Business checking: no monthly service fees on Standard, no overdraft fees, no incoming domestic wire or ACH fees, no in-network ATM fees, no minimum balance requirement, no limits on the number of transactions, free standard ACH. It does charge fees for certain transactions such as outgoing wire transfers and international payments.
- Plans: Standard, monthly fee none, 1.3% APY on balances up to $250,000, requirement: spend $500 per month with the Bluevine Business Debit Mastercard OR receive or deposit $2,500 per month in customer payments. Plus, $30/month (fee can be waived per Bluevine), 1.75% APY on balances up to $250,000, no requirements to earn. Premier, $95/month (fee can be waived per Bluevine), 3.3% APY on all balances, no requirements to earn. Up to 3.3% APY is the top advertised rate.
- Up to 50 sub-accounts with automatic transfer rules; physical and virtual debit cards; mobile check deposit; bill pay; invoicing, estimates, payment links and Tap to Pay; international payments in as fast as 24 hours; same-day ACH, domestic and international wire sending.
- Bluevine Line of Credit: up to $250,000 ("up to $250K"), no maintenance fees, issued by Celtic Bank and serviced by Bluevine; rates, lines and terms vary by creditworthiness; additional fees apply. Decision in as fast as five minutes; draws in as fast as 24 hours, or instantly into a Bluevine Business Checking account. Repayment automatic, weekly or monthly plan. Reported to Experian.
- Line of credit minimums: corporation or LLC, in business 12+ months, $10,000 monthly revenue ($120,000 annually), 625+ personal FICO score, no bankruptcies on file, good standing with Secretary of State. Ineligible states: Nevada, North Dakota, South Dakota, U.S. territories. Sole proprietorships cannot qualify for the line of credit directly. An approved line of credit comes with a complimentary Bluevine Business Checking account.
- Also: Business Term Loan up to $500,000 from lending partners with terms up to 24 months; SBA 7(a) loans up to $350,000 through a partnership with Fundera by NerdWallet.
- Bluevine says it has helped 1M+ businesses and 12+ years in business.
