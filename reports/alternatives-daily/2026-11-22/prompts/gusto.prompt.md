## CLOSED FACT LIST (use ONLY these facts; checked November 22, 2026 on each vendor's own site)

### Gusto (subject) - gusto.com/product/pricing
- Gusto says "Payroll, benefits, and HR all in one place. No hidden fees. Cancel any time." No contracts, free account setup, switch or cancel anytime.
- Simple: $49/mo plus $6/mo per person; "run basic payroll in one state"; unlimited payrolls per month, tax filings and payments, basic paid time off (PTO) policies and holiday pay.
- Plus (labeled best value): $80/mo plus $12/mo per person; "pay a mixed workforce and expand in new states"; adds time tracking and scheduling, unlimited next day pay, multi-state payroll, contractor invoice management.
- Premium: $180/mo plus $22/mo per person; a named advisor, built-in compliance, human resources (HR) guidance and financial tools; Premium Account Support, HR guidance and tools, Gusto Business Compliance, unlimited same day and instant pay, project based rules and costing, 25 invoice payments and bank transfers included.
- All three plans list: direct deposits or printable checks, payroll approvals, Payroll on AutoPilot, employee app, multiple pay rates and schedules, employer mobile app, domestic contractor payments, expenses and reimbursements. Contractor invoice upload and workforce cost reports and unlimited custom earning types are in Plus and Premium only; unlimited custom admin roles, same-day pay and instant pay are Premium only.
- Add-ons: global contractor payments in more than 120 countries, no monthly per-contractor fee, $5 per payment to US-based bank accounts, foreign exchange rates may vary. State tax registration, pricing varies by state. Research and development tax credits: pay 15% of identified credits. Next-Day pay $15/mo plus $3/mo per person (included in Plus and Premium). Instant pay $100 per payroll (included in Premium). Same-day pay $90 per payroll (included in Premium). Time & Attendance Plus: try free, then $6/mo per person. Gusto Business Compliance $85/mo per state on an annual contract (included in Premium).
- Benefits: health insurance, 401(k) with pricing varying by plan selected, health insurance broker integration $6/mo per eligible employee, life and disability insurance with employees paying only premiums and no administration fees, HSA/FSA/commuter accounts under one $200 annual service charge (health savings account = HSA, flexible spending account = FSA).
- Gusto points S corporation owners who want to pay themselves compliantly to a Gusto Solo plan (price not in this list).
- Gusto cites 4.6 star average on Capterra (4,137 reviews) and 4.6 on Software Advice (Gusto's claim).

### OnPay - onpay.com/pricing
- Payroll Essentials: $49 base plus $6 per worker per month. HR: $15 base plus $2 per worker. HR plus Time Tracking: $21 base plus $4 per worker. Time Tracking alone: $15 base plus $4 per worker. Bundled HR: $71 base plus $1 per worker; bundled HR plus Time Tracking: $81 base plus $4 per worker (bundles include Payroll Essentials).
- Included on all plans: W-2s, 1099s and year-end filings; OnPay says no hidden fees and free data migration ("White-glove setup, free data migration"). Integrated benefits: free integration and admin; pay only plan premiums for health insurance, workers' comp and 401(k).
- Page promotes a partner program for accounting firms (up to $10,000 when switching clients).

### Patriot Software - patriotsoftware.com/payroll/pricing
- Basic Payroll: $17 a month plus $4 per worker paid (promotion: 30 days free plus 50% off for 3 months, showing $8.50 plus $2). Unlimited payrolls, free direct deposit, free employee portal, auto payroll, pay contractors in payroll, free health benefit integration, robust payroll reports.
- Full Service Payroll: $37 a month plus $5 per worker paid. Adds federal, state and local tax filings and deposits and year-end payroll tax filings at no additional fee. Basic Payroll leaves tax filing to the employer.
- Add-ons: Time & Attendance $6 a month plus $2 per employee; HR Software $6 a month plus $2 per employee. Patriot also sells accounting software ($20 and $30 a month).
- In months a business does not run payroll, only the base price is charged.

### Square Payroll - squareup.com/us/en/payroll/pricing
- Full-service payroll: $35 a month plus $6 a month per person paid. Contractor-only payroll: $6 a month per person paid.
- Unlimited pay runs per month, no commitment or long-term contract, simple onboarding, automated tax filings. Integrated with Square point of sale: import timecards and tips. Syncs with QuickBooks Online accounting. Optional mailed paper W-2s or 1099s: annual fee of $3 per employee or contractor.
- Square also lists health insurance and a 401(k) option (no prices in this list).

### Justworks - justworks.com/pricing
- Payroll: $8 per month per employee plus $50 a month base fee; payroll and HR tools with access to time tracking, expert support.
- PEO Basic: $79 per month per employee, no base fee; payroll, HR essentials, support and compliance, 401(k), 24/7 support, applicant tracking system. A professional employer organization (PEO) shares employer responsibilities; workers' comp access is required for the PEO plans.
- PEO Plus (labeled popular): $124 per month per employee, no base fee; adds medical, dental and vision administration, HSA/FSA accounts, mental health and fertility benefits.
- Employer of record (EOR) for global hiring: $599 per month per employee.
- Justworks payroll plan lists multi-state payroll, contractor payments, off-cycle payments, tips processing, W-2 and 1099 filings, state unemployment insurance administration.

### Paychex Flex - paychex.com/payroll-services
- No public price: the page offers "Request Pricing" and "Get Your Free Quote". Solutions are grouped for 1-19, 20-99 and 100+ employees. Paychex Flex covers payroll, time and attendance, benefits, insurance and HR services; Paychex Flex PEO is separate.
- Run payroll in as little as 30 seconds; WISE, the artificial intelligence (AI) built into Paychex Flex; employees can review paychecks in advance with Paychex Pre-check; support by phone, chat or email. Primarily designed for U.S.-based employees and contractors.

### ADP RUN - adp.com/what-we-offer/products/run-powered-by-adp.aspx
- No public price: page shows "Get Pricing". For businesses with 1-49 employees. Pay employees by direct deposit, a Wisely debit card or paper check; recruit, hire and onboard employees; secure mobile access to pay and forms. Page showed a switch promotion of up to 6 months free payroll ending November 26 (terms apply).
