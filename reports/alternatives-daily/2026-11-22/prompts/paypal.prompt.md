## CLOSED FACT LIST (use ONLY these facts; checked November 22, 2026 on each vendor's own site)

### PayPal (subject) - paypal.com/us/digital-wallet/paypal-consumer-fees (US consumer fee page, last updated May 19, 2026)
- Buying: free to use PayPal to donate or pay for a purchase or other commercial transaction unless a currency conversion is involved (online or in-store: no fee when no currency conversion).
- Sending personal payments (Friends and Family tab) domestic: no fee from PayPal balance (including the PayPal Debit Card) or a bank account; cards 2.90% plus a fixed fee; Amex Send account no fee.
- Sending personal payments international: 5.00% with a minimum of 0.99 USD and maximum of 4.99 USD, in addition to the domestic fee.
- Receiving personal payments: no fee when no currency conversion is involved; fixed fee per currency where a fee applies (US dollar 0.30 USD).
- Currency conversion spread: 4.00% (or other amount disclosed during the transaction) when paying for goods in a different currency, sending money so a friend receives a different currency, or PayPal Payouts; 3.00% for all other transactions.
- Withdrawals from a personal account: standard transfer to an eligible linked bank account or debit card, no fee when no currency conversion; instant transfer to bank or card 1.75% of amount transferred (US dollar minimum 0.25 USD, maximum 25.00 USD); paper check 1.50 USD (only for accounts without a linked PayPal Balance account). Instant transfer limit for bank 25,000.00 USD per transaction; card transfer limits 5,000.00 USD per transaction, per day and per week, and 15,000.00 USD per month.
- Donations: sending domestic donations no fee; receiving through Donate Button or PayPal Checkout for Donations 2.89% plus a fixed fee (US dollar 0.49 USD); international donations add 1.50%.
- Crypto: buying or selling fee varies by amount: 1.00 to 74.99 USD 2.20%; 75.00 to 200.00 USD 2.00%; 200.01 to 1,000.00 USD 1.80%; 1,000.01 USD and above 1.50%. No fee to hold crypto. Transferring crypto out adds 1% on the USD value for crypto other than PYUSD and 1.5% for PYUSD, plus a network fee.
- Credit card and debit card link and confirmation fee may be charged to some users and is refunded after verification. Business Debit Mastercard and merchant selling fees are on separate pages.
- Seller fees are on a separate PayPal merchant fee page and not covered here.

### Venmo - venmo.com/about/fees/
- No fee to send money to a Venmo account or U.S. PayPal account from Venmo balance, debit card or bank account. 3.00% fee for sending using a credit card. 5.00% (minimum $0.99, maximum $4.99) to send to an eligible non-U.S. PayPal account.
- No fee to receive personal payments; a fee applies if the sender identifies the payment as goods and services. Business profile receiving 1.9% plus $0.10.
- Transfer to bank: standard free, typically 1-3 business days to a linked bank account and about 48 hours to an eligible debit card; instant 1.75% (minimum $0.25, maximum $25).
- Adding money by bank link free; check deposit in minutes 2.00% (minimum $6) for payroll or government checks, 5.00% (minimum $6) for other checks.

### Cash App - cash.app
- Cash App describes sending money as fast and free. Cash App Card: no hidden or monthly fees. Cash App Green: up to 3.5% savings interest, free overdraft coverage up to $200, free withdrawals from 40k in-network ATMs. Borrow up to $500 with no credit check (per Cash App). Get paycheck up to 2 days early. Pay over time with Afterpay. Buy stocks with no commission fees; buy and sell bitcoin; file taxes for free. Says 59 million+ active accounts. FDIC insurance subject to terms. Instant deposit and other fee amounts are not listed on the pages checked; do not state any.

### Zelle - zellepay.com/faq
- Sends money through a bank or credit union's mobile app or online banking, using the recipient's email address or U.S. mobile number; money typically available within minutes to an enrolled recipient. Typically no fees for consumers to send or receive, but confirm with the bank or credit union. Sender and recipient must have U.S. bank accounts; no international use. Payments cannot be reversed once the recipient is enrolled; can cancel only if recipient is not enrolled. Qualifying imposter scams may be eligible for reimbursement via the bank.

### Apple Cash - apple.com/apple-cash
- Send and receive money in Messages, Wallet or with Tap to Cash; spend with Apple Pay in stores, online and in apps. Load from a debit card; move money to a bank account. Apple Cash Family for people under 18 in Family Sharing. Must be 18 and a U.S. resident (family organizer can set up under-18 accounts). Services from Green Dot Bank. Apple Cash page lists no fee schedule; do not state any fee amount. Requires an Apple device (built into iPhone).

### Wise - wise.com/us/pricing
- No subscriptions or plans; registering free; holding money free. Sending fee varies by currency, from 0.23%; uses mid-market exchange rate with a small upfront fee. Discount automatically once transfers exceed 25,000 USD in a month. Same-currency send between Wise contacts free. Wise Multi-Currency Card: 9 USD to get; ATM withdrawals up to 250 USD a calendar month free, then 1.95 USD plus 1.95%. Receiving USD wire or Swift payments 6.11 USD per payment; domestic non-wire payments in AUD, CAD, EUR, GBP, HUF, NZD, PHP, SGD, USD free. Not a peer-to-peer wallet for friends who are not on Wise in the US sense; do not claim more than the above.

- Is Zelle free? Zelle says typically no fees for consumers; confirm with the bank.
- Which alternative suits international transfers? Wise lists fees from 0.23% that vary by currency; Zelle is US bank accounts only.
