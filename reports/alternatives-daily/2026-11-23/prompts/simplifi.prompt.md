### Monarch, monarch.com/pricing
- Two plans: Core at $99.99 per year or $14.99 per month; Plus at $199.99 per year. Plus adds forecasting tools, tracking for multiple income sources like a small business, and deeper investment features. Your first week is free. Banner: 30% off Core for one year with code WELCOME (terms apply; may expire). Cancel anytime, money-back guarantee. Monarch recommends signing up on the web rather than through app stores. No ads and it says it never sells financial data. Pricing page also links "vs. Simplifi" and "vs. YNAB" comparison pages.

### YNAB (You Need A Budget), ynab.com/pricing
- Annual plan: $109 paid annually ($9.08 per month, "$70 Savings"). Monthly plan: $14.99 per month. Plus tax where applicable; priced in US dollars. 34-day free trial, no credit card needed when signing up directly (app stores may require one). College students get a free 365-day trial. A subscription can be shared with a group of up to six people. Features: connect accounts, goal targets, debt tools with an integrated loan calculator, real-time syncing across devices. The YNAB Method asks what each dollar is for ("What's it for?"). YNAB does not support multiple currencies together in one spending plan.

### Copilot Money, copilot.money/pricing
- One plan with no tiers: $9 per month, "$109 billed yearly, instead of $119" marked "limited time offer". Connects to 16,000+ banks. Money assistant included. Platforms: iPhone, iPad, Mac and Web. Support from real humans included. "Invite your partner" is listed as coming this fall.
