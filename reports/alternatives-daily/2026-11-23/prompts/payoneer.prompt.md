## Payoneer (subject; payoneer.com/pricing, fees page last updated 1 January 2026)
- Payoneer says it helps businesses receive, send and manage cross-border payments across 190+ countries and territories and 70+ currencies. Products listed: receiving accounts, payment requests, payment links, checkout, stablecoin payments (USDT, USDC), single, batch (up to 1,000 payments) and mass payouts, VAT payments, Employer of Record, Agent of Record, Contractor Management System, corporate cards, expense management, Smart FX, Working Capital Advance, developer APIs.
- Page says opening an account typically has no monthly subscription fee.
- Receiving: from another Payoneer customer's balance free; from marketplaces and integrated platforms varies by marketplace; via a receiving account in your local currency free; via a receiving account in a currency that is not your local currency 1% (minimum fee 1.00 USD or equivalent); from a payer using a credit card up to 3.99% + 0.49 USD or equivalent; ACH bank debit (US only) 1%; EU or UK bank account 1%; PayPal (US only) up to 3.99% + 0.49 USD.
- Sending to a Payoneer account: from your balance, recipient in same country up to 4.00 USD/EUR/GBP; recipient in different country up to 1% + up to 4.00 USD or equivalent; using a credit card up to 3.99%; UK or EU bank account 1%; US bank account via ACH 1%; PayPal up to 3.99% + 0.49 USD.
- Withdrawals: to a bank account in the same country, local currency, in supported countries (example USA to USA, USD) 1.50 USD; to a bank account in the recipient's local currency, no conversion 1.2% to 4% (in some countries a minimum fee of up to 20.00 USD or equivalent may apply); to a bank account in a non-local currency with conversion 1.2% to 4%. For certain US customers (depending on registration date) a fixed fee of 1.50 USD applies per withdrawal or payment, up to 50,000.00 USD in withdrawals or payments per month, after which a percentage fee of up to 0.5% applies.
- Move funds between your Payoneer balances 0.50%.
- Cards: purchases without currency conversion up to 1.8% (free where merchant country equals card issuing country); with currency conversion up to 3.5%; cash withdrawals same currency 3.15 USD plus up to 1.8%; cash withdrawals with conversion 3.15 USD plus up to 3.5%; ATM balance check or decline 1.00 USD; standard card delivery free; annual card fee 29.95 USD; additional virtual cards free; replace card 12.95 USD.
- Annual account fee 29.95 USD, applies only if the account receives less than 6,000.00 USD or equivalent in any 12 consecutive months; not charged in the first year for paid annual plans. Certain account types in specific regions require an Annual Plan; in certain countries a registration fee may apply.
- Escheatment fee: variable per state if funds must be escheated.
- Businesses processing over 50K USD monthly: custom pricing, contact sales.
- Disclaimer: prices are an estimate based on the most standard fee structure; different prices may apply in different territories, account types and onboarding channels; exact fees are in the Fees link inside the account and shown before a payment is confirmed. Currency conversion rates are based on wholesale market rates at transaction time and include the conversion fee where indicated.
- Payoneer states a Payoneer and Upwork 15-year partnership extension (newsroom headline).
