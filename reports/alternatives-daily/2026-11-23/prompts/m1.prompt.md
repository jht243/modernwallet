### M1 (subject) - m1.com (the /pricing path returns the homepage)
- M1 calls itself "your AI wealth management platform": investing, saving and borrowing in one place, with M1 Advisor (a fiduciary AI financial advisor) alongside accounts. 400,000+ clients and $14B+ in client assets as of September 2026.
- Invest: taxable brokerage (individual, joint, custodial), retirement (Traditional IRA, Roth IRA, SEP IRA), trust and crypto offered by Bakkt. A portfolio is a "Pie" of stocks and ETFs the user chooses; build it yourself or let M1 Advisor help. Choose from 6,000+ US-listed stocks and ETFs; fractional shares let the user build to exact percentages.
- Cost: no commission, trading or management fees for self-directed brokerage accounts. Platform fee $3/month, waived for clients with more than $10,000 on the platform. M1 Advisor is a free advisory service for M1 clients. Other fees may apply (regulatory fees, account closure fees, ADR fees); see M1's fee schedule.
- Earn: High-Yield Cash Account 3.35% APY on uninvested cash; stated APY requires a minimum initial deposit of $100; rates subject to change. Cash swept to partner banks becomes eligible for FDIC insurance up to an aggregate $4.75 million.
- Borrow: margin loans at 5.90% against the portfolio; margin built into brokerage accounts with at least $2,000 in eligible securities; not available for retirement and custodial accounts. Personal Loans: "further availability soon".
- Limits: no day trading, extended-hours trading or limit orders; trades run once or twice a day in scheduled trade windows; options, futures, short selling, mutual funds and OTC stocks not available. Not a robo-advisor picking the portfolio.
- M1 is not a bank. Brokerage by M1 Finance LLC (FINRA and SIPC member); SIPC protects securities up to $500,000 including $250,000 cash. Advice by M1 Advisory Services, LLC (SEC-registered investment adviser).
- Transfers: ACAT transfers take 4-7 business days; no incoming transfer fee; rollovers of 401(k), 403(b), 457 into an M1 IRA, no fee to receive a rollover, most finish within two to three weeks.
- M1 says it is for long-term investors, "not a day-trading platform".

### Betterment - betterment.com/pricing
- Investing: $5/month for balances $0-$24K; 0.25% annual fee with $200+ monthly recurring deposit or a $24K-$1M balance. No minimum balance. Discounts above $1M ($1-$2M: 0.15%; over $2M: 0.10% on the portion above).
- Premium: 0.65% annual fee on the first $1M (0.25% base plus 0.40%), $100,000 minimum eligible investment balance, access to financial experts.
- Self-directed investing: no commissions; Betterment's management fee waived on those assets. Cash Reserve: no monthly management fees, 3.50% variable APY as of 9/21/2026 plus 0.75% boost for new clients with a qualifying deposit (4.25% shown); $10 minimum deposit for base APY. Checking with no foreign transaction or ATM withdrawal fees. Funds' embedded ETF fees apply to portfolios.

### Wealthfront - wealthfront.com/pricing and /cash
