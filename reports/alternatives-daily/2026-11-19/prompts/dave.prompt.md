## CLOSED FACT LIST (use ONLY these facts; checked November 19, 2026 on each vendor's own site)

### Dave (subject) - dave.com, dave.com/checking-account, dave.com/no-hidden-fees
- Dave is a financial technology company, not a bank. Accounts and services come from Coastal Community Bank, Member FDIC, or another partner bank; accounts are FDIC-insured up to $250,000 (conditions apply; pass-through basis; protects only against failure of the bank holding the funds).
- ExtraCash: advances of $25 to $500, typically authorized within 5 minutes; Dave says not all members qualify and few qualify for $500. No credit check, no interest, no late fees. Eligibility is determined by Dave's CashAI technology, which looks at 180+ data points including income, gig or irregular work, bank balance and spending patterns. Trusted by 14M+ members (Dave's claim).
- ExtraCash is structured as an overdraft with an overdraft fee equal to the greater of $5 or 5%; repayable on demand; multiple overdrafts may be required; requires an ExtraCash overdraft deposit account and a Dave Checking account.
- Membership: up to $5 monthly membership fee for ExtraCash, Income Opportunity Services and Financial Management Services. Dave's checking FAQ says any Dave feature requires the membership, which renews monthly; membership is not tied to ExtraCash eligibility and deleting the app does not end it.
- Optional 1.5% fee for external debit card transfers. Transferring ExtraCash to Dave Checking is at no added cost.
- Dave Checking: no credit check, no minimum balance, no minimum deposit (fund with as little as $1), no minimum-balance fees or account closing fees; digital Dave Debit Mastercard usable with Apple Pay and Google Pay, physical card on request (up to 10 business days by USPS); paycheck up to 2 days early with direct deposit (timing depends on payer); Round Up savings; budgeting tool; fee-free ATM withdrawals at 40K+ MoneyPass ATMs (out-of-network fees may apply); add cash at Walgreens and 90K other stores with a retail service fee up to $4.95 (service by Green Dot).
- Goals account exists for tracking savings. Dave is used through its mobile app (iOS and Android).

### MoneyLion - moneylion.com/instacash
- Financial technology company, not a bank; MoneyLion Spend account provided by Pathward, N.A., Member FDIC.
- Instacash: cash advances up to $500; limit up to $1,000 with qualifying recurring direct deposits into MoneyLion Spend; a paid MoneyLion membership can raise the limit up to $700. No interest, no monthly fee for Instacash, no credit check. Maximum $100 per disbursement per MoneyLion's how-to note.
- Delivery: to a MoneyLion Spend account, standard 1-2 business days $0 (the page also says 1-5 business days in marketing copy); optional Turbo delivery within minutes 7.5% of the advance, capped at $14.99. To an external debit card or eligible external checking account, standard delivery not available; Turbo is 9% of the advance amount. Turbo fees and tips are optional and do not affect eligibility.
- Repayment is automatic on the pay-cycle repayment date; no interest. Linked checking account should be at least two months old with income deposits and a positive balance.
- Peer Boosts: each month a user can increase 5 friends' limits.

### EarnIn - earnin.com
- Earned wage access (EWA): users access wages already earned; EarnIn says it is not a loan and charges no interest and no mandatory fees, no credit check. Cash limits vary by user eligibility; EarnIn pages cite Live Pay up to $1,500 per pay period.
- Standard transfers are free and usually arrive in 1-2 business days; Lightning Speed, an optional expedited transfer, starts at $3.99. Tips are optional. Early Pay: direct deposits up to 2 days sooner for $2.99. Other tools: Balance Shield, Credit Monitoring, Savings Jars, EarnIn Card with Live Pay.

### Varo - varomoney.com
- Varo Bank, N.A., Member FDIC, an independent bank with its own banking charter; deposits FDIC-insured up to $250,000.
- Cash Advance: up to $250 at first, building up to $1,000 over time with direct deposit; average loan $195; no interest; flat fees from $1.60 to $100 based on loan amount; qualifications apply.
- Personal Line of Credit: loans from $600 to $2,000, average loan $825 (qualification required, including bank account deposits).
- Bank account: no overdraft fees, no minimum balance fees, no in-network ATM fees; early payday up to 2 days with direct deposit; fee-free cash deposits at over 7,500 CVS locations; savings up to 3.75% APY on up to $5,000 and 1.00% APY on everything else; Believe Visa credit builder card.

### Cash App - cash.app, cash.app/borrow
- Borrow: up to $500 with no credit check (limit depends on eligibility; first-time borrowers' limits up to $400; $500 includes Cash App Green limit increase; average limit for first-time borrowers who are spend actives was $153 as of October 1, 2025). Pay only a flat fee (amount disclosed in the app). Minimum $20 borrow. Most people become eligible by direct depositing $300+ in paychecks monthly or linking an external account with $500+ monthly deposits. Not available in Colorado, Iowa or Oregon. Loans issued and serviced by Square Financial Services, Inc.
- Cash App Card: no hidden or monthly fees; free overdraft coverage up to $200 (Cash App says); paycheck up to 2 days early; savings interest up to 3.5% with Cash App Green; Green is earned by $500 in qualifying purchases or $300 in qualifying deposits per month; free withdrawals from 40k in-network ATMs for Green. Afterpay pay-over-time.

### Klover - klover.app
- Cash advance up to $750 (limits $25 to $750, average about $170), no credit check, no interest, no late fees. Homepage text also shows $200 in places; the FAQ and cash advance page state $750.
- Standard transfers are free, usually 2-5 business days; optional express transfer for a fee (amount not stated in the text read). Klover earns revenue through express fees and Klover+ memberships. Earn points by taking surveys and watching ads to raise advance size. Budget tools and optional credit score tracking.

### Albert - albert.com/cash
- Not a bank; Albert Cash held in a pooled account at Stride Bank, N.A.; Savings at Wells Fargo, N.A.; FDIC insurance up to $250,000 pass-through (conditions apply).
- Instant Advance: limits $25 to $1,000, subject to eligibility; few qualify for $1,000; no late fees or interest; no Albert subscription required for Instant Advance. Instant Line of Credit by FinWise Bank: limits $25 to $1,000, draw fees apply. Instant Loan: loans start at $1,000 with soft credit pull for rate check.
- Subscription plans range from $19.99 to $39.99 per month (30-day trial). High yield savings at 3.50% APY (variable, current as of 11/19/2026) requires an eligible subscription tier. Virtual cards with spending limits; budgeting and automatic savings.
