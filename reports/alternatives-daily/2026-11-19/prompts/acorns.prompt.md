## CLOSED FACT LIST (use ONLY these facts; checked November 19, 2026 on each vendor's own site)

### Acorns (subject) - acorns.com/pricing
- Acorns bundles products into subscription plans; page says "No hidden costs or transaction fees - just one, transparent monthly payment".
- Acorns Bronze: $4/mo. Investment account with an expert-built diversified portfolio; Round-Ups to save and invest spare change; Acorns Later retirement account; paycheck split; 450+ in-app partner brands for bonus investments plus a browser extension at 11,000+ partners; educational courses. Also lists a 1% IRA match on new contributions during the first year (Bronze).
- Acorns Silver: $8/mo. Everything in Bronze plus Acorns Checking (online banking that saves and invests), Real-Time Round-Ups, 3.59% APY on Emergency Savings, bonus investments matched up to 25%, live Q&As with investing experts.
- Acorns Gold: $12/mo. Everything in Silver plus a 3% IRA match on new contributions during the first year (max out the annual contribution for an extra $225), investment account for kids with a 1% match, ability to add individual stocks and ETFs to customize the portfolio, bonus investments matched up to 50%, Money Manager (splits deposits across investing, saving and spending), Acorns Early app and debit card for kids and teens, free federal/state/local tax filing through third-party providers, a $10,000 life insurance policy for eligible customers, a no-cost Will (valued at $299).
- Invest My Tips: personalized QR code, 100% of each tip invested, 1% match on qualifying tips.
- Acorns Checking: no overdraft fees; 55,000+ fee-free ATMs in the AllPoint network; Acorns is not a bank; Visa debit and banking services issued by Lincoln Savings Bank or nbkc bank, members FDIC.
- Acorns investment products are offered by Acorns Advisers, LLC, an SEC-registered investment adviser; brokerage by Acorns Securities, LLC (FINRA/SIPC); investments are not FDIC insured and may lose value.

### Stash - stash.com/pricing
- The Stash Plan: $12 per month, or $108 billed annually (save 25%). Includes 24/7 financial guidance, personalized investment plan, 3% retirement match (worth up to $225/year), $10,000 life insurance policy, Stock-Back rewards (worth up to $120/year). 7-day trial offered.
- No minimums stated on the pricing page. Choose your own stocks and ETFs or let the investing team manage a diversified portfolio. Brokerage, retirement and custodial accounts for kids.
- In addition to the subscription, Stash charges an assets-under-management (AUM) advisory fee of 0.25% annually on Smart Portfolios with balances of $1,000 or more and on Managed Retirement Accounts.

### Betterment - betterment.com/pricing
- Base price for investing accounts: $5 per month for balances $0 to $24K. Switches to 0.25% annually on the investing balance with $200+ monthly recurring deposits or a balance of $24,000 or more.
- Premium: 0.65% annual advisory fee on the first $1M (0.25% Digital base fee plus 0.40% Premium fee), requires $100,000 minimum in eligible investments per household; access to an advisor.
- Cash Reserve high-yield cash: no fees or minimums stated; variable APY 3.50% as of 9/21/2026, plus 0.75% APY Boost for new clients with a qualifying deposit (through 4/16/2027); FDIC insurance through program banks.
- Account types: automated investing, traditional/Roth/SEP IRAs, self-directed investing, custom portfolios, checking, Solo 401(k).

### Wealthfront - wealthfront.com/pricing
- Advisory fee for The Wealthfront Portfolio: 0.25% annually. Tax-loss harvesting and automatic rebalancing included. Also offers a Brokerage Account for ETFs and stocks.
- Cash account: no fees, $1 to get started.

### Fidelity Go - fidelity.com/managed-accounts/fidelity-go/overview
- No minimum to open an account; invest with as little as $10 (balance must reach $10 before investing starts).
- $0 advisory fee under $25,000; 0.35% per year for accounts of $25,000 and above. No trading fees stated.
- At $25,000+: unlimited one-on-one financial coaching sessions, and tax-loss harvesting for taxable accounts on Flex Funds that hold stocks.

### Schwab Intelligent Portfolios - schwab.com/intelligent-portfolios
- Get started with as little as $5,000 (minimum to open).
- No advisory fee and no commissions charged. Investors pay ETF operating expenses; a portion of the portfolio is held in cash at Charles Schwab Bank (FDIC-insured deposit), which the page notes can pay a lower yield than cash alternatives outside the program.
- Tax-loss harvesting available for accounts with $50,000 or more invested, no additional fee; must enroll.
- Portfolio of about 50 ETFs chosen by experts; automatic daily monitoring and rebalancing; 24/7 live support.
