### Albert (subject) - albert.com and albert.com/budgeting
- Albert calls itself "your personal financial assistant" and "the all-in-one money app". It says over 10 million customers trust it, with 200K+ 5-star app reviews, $2B+ saved and invested and $13B+ budgeted, tracked and managed.
- Genius is Albert's artificial intelligence (AI) financial assistant. Albert says Genius handles requests such as transferring funds or buying and selling investments, creates a budget automatically from spending, categorizes transactions, finds small amounts to move to savings, and can forecast net worth. Albert's disclosure says Genius can make mistakes and is not a financial advisor.
- Budgeting: automatic transaction categorization, spending and cash flow tracking, weekly spending report, bill and subscription tracking with bill increase alerts, bill lowering negotiation and subscription cancellation, net worth tracking, Forecast for planning big expenses months ahead, connection to thousands of institutions.
- Banking: Albert Cash debit account with cash back on spend, get paid early with direct deposit, fee-free ATMs. Albert is not a bank; banking services are provided by Stride Bank, N.A., Member FDIC. Savings account funds are held at FDIC-insured banks including Wells Fargo, N.A. Cash and Savings funds are eligible for up to $250,000 of FDIC insurance on a pass-through basis, subject to conditions.
- Savings: automatic savings and savings vaults; High Yield Savings has variable rates (current as of 11/21/2026, no minimum balance) and requires an eligible subscription tier. Fees to use Albert may reduce earnings.
- Investing: stocks, ETFs and strategies managed by Albert. Advisory services by Albert Investments, LLC, a Registered Investment Advisor; brokerage by Albert Securities, LLC, Member FINRA/SIPC. Securities are not FDIC insured and may lose value.
- Protection: identity protection with 24/7 monitoring, dark web monitoring, credit report alerts, fraud alerts, online data privacy, up to $1M of identity theft insurance.
- Subscription plans range from $19.99/mo to $39.99/mo. Try a plan for 30 days before being charged. Plans auto-renew until canceled. Cancel in the app.
- Instant Advance: available at Albert's discretion, no late fees or interest, limits $25 to $1,000, not all customers qualify and few qualify for $1,000. An Albert subscription is not required to use Instant Advance. Transfers to an external account within minutes may carry a transfer fee.
- Instant Line of Credit: unsecured, issued by FinWise Bank, a Utah chartered bank, Member FDIC. Limits $25 to $1,000, draw fees apply, no Albert subscription required.
- Albert does not publish a per-tier feature list on its home or budgeting pages; the page shows only the $19.99 to $39.99 range. Do not name tiers or say which features sit in which tier.

### Monarch Money - monarchmoney.com/pricing
- Two plans. Core: $99.99 per year or $14.99 per month. Plus: $199.99 per year. Monarch recommends signing up on the web rather than through app stores. Cancel anytime; money-back guarantee. Page says "Your first week is on us".
- Plus adds forecasting tools, tracking multiple income sources like a small business, and deeper investment tracking.
- Monarch says it shows no ads and never sells financial data. Page links comparisons against YNAB, Simplifi, Credit Karma and Mint (do not mention those on our page).
- No bank account and no cash advance on the pricing page.

### YNAB - ynab.com/pricing
- Annual plan $109 per year ($9.08 per month); monthly plan $14.99 per month; plus tax where applicable. 34-day free trial, no credit card required.
- Budgeting method built around giving every dollar a job; connects accounts; no bank account or cash advance on the pricing page.

### Quicken Simplifi - simplifimoney.com

- No bank account and no cash advance on the page.

### Dave - dave.com

- CashAI analyzes 180+ data points including income, gig or irregular work, bank balance and spending patterns.
