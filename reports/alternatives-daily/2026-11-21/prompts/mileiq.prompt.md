MileIQ (mileiq.com/pricing):
- Individuals: Free plan $0 with 40 free drives every month, automatic mileage tracking and auto-generated reports. Unlimited plan $11.66 per month billed annually, or $13.99 per month billed monthly, with unlimited drives, automatic mileage tracking and auto-generated reports; described as the best option for reimbursements and tax-compliant reporting.
- Classify a drive by swiping left or right, then submit a tax-compliant mileage report. MileIQ for Teams tracks employees' mileage automatically; team pricing is on a separate "business pricing" page that is not part of this individual pricing page, so no team price is stated here.
- MileIQ is published by Bending Spoons (app store listing: Bending Spoons US Inc.).
- Derived arithmetic (allowed): $11.66 x 12 = $139.92 per year billed annually; $13.99 x 12 = $167.88 per year billed monthly; 40 free drives per month = 480 per year.

Everlance (everlance.com/pricing):
- Basic $0, no payment required: 30 automatically detected trips per month, unlimited manual trips, mileage and expense reports.
- Starter $89.99 per year or $10.99 per month: unlimited automatic mileage tracking, expense tracking with receipt uploads, custom IRS-compliant reporting.
- Professional $119.99 per year or $19.99 per month: everything in Starter plus automatic expense tracking, AI deduction finder, free tax filing (all-inclusive 1099 tax filing) and $1 million audit defense.
- Every plan has a 7-day free trial; a credit card is needed to start the trial on a paid plan.
- Derived: $10.99 x 12 = $131.88; $19.99 x 12 = $239.88.

TripLog (triplog.net/pricing):
- Basic: free forever, unlimited automatic GPS mileage tracking and basic expense management.
- Premium: $59.99 paid annually (shown as $4.99 per month): adds unlimited reporting, tax-compliant reports, income and expense tracking, OCR receipt capture, web app access, auto-classification rules, bank and credit card integration, connection to SAP Concur and QuickBooks Online, custom business activities and mileage rates. Basic gets reporting only with a 7-day pass (one free pass per calendar year, available January 1; it includes the annual mileage report download).
- Optional: TripLog Drive dedicated GPS device $79.99 each; TripLog Beacon $19.99 each (Bluetooth beacon that triggers tracking); Time add-on $4 per month (Premium only).
- Team pricing: single user versus multi user toggle; no multi-user price is stated on the page, state "Quote-based; no public price" for teams.

Driversnote (driversnote.com/pricing):
- Free 0 USD per month: automatic tracking on iOS, Android and web, easy reporting of up to 15 trips per month.
- Pro 11 USD per month: unlimited trips and reporting, edit log, public API; includes a free iBeacon (40 USD value) with a yearly subscription.
- Teams 11 USD per license per month, for small teams of 2 to 10 users; web tools for report collection and validation by admins. Teams+ custom pricing for 11+ users, with SSO and a dedicated Customer Success Manager.
- Prices are excluding taxes. Support 24/7.
- Derived: 11 x 12 = 132 USD per year.

Expensify (expensify.com/pricing):
- Free plan for all members includes receipt scanning, mileage tracking, expense reports, categories and tags, custom distance and tax rates, personal card import.
- Collect $5 per member per month (small business: reimbursements, corporate card management, approvals, travel booking, QuickBooks and Xero integrations). Control: custom pricing, as low as $9 per active member per month.
- Individuals can use New Expensify free with unlimited SmartScans; extra functionality such as expense categorization requires a paid plan.
- Derived: $5 x 12 = $60 per year per member.

Hurdlr (hurdlr.com/pricing):
- Hurdlr Premium: expense and receipt capture, automatic mileage tracking, live tax estimates and reminders. Hurdlr Pro: invoicing, accounting, core financial reports (income statement, balance sheet, trial balance). The page lists no plan prices; state "No plan price shown on its pricing page". Enterprise requests go through a form.

MileageWise (mileagewise.com/pricing):
- Packages cover past mileage recovery (Google Maps Timeline export): Bronze $229 covers any 1 year; Silver $349 covers any 2 years; Gold $449 covers any 3 years; Gold Lifetime $559 covers unlimited years. Sales tax included.
- Includes car trip identification and filtering, location matching with addresses and points of interest, flexible trip editing, an automatic audit pre-check, a web dashboard and mobile app, and instant reports (PDF, CSV, XLSX). Page says the package is tax deductible as a business expense.

Stride (stridehealth.com):
- Free app for 1099 workers to track mileage and expenses and prepare for taxes; mileage and expense tracking; IRS-ready expense reports that can be handed to an accountant. Stride also sells health insurance marketplace and benefits products (not part of this comparison).
- Derived: $0 per year.
