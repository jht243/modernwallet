## CLOSED FACT LIST (use ONLY these facts; checked November 21, 2026 on each vendor's own site)

### Novo (subject) - novo.co and novo.co/business-checking
- Novo is a fintech, not a bank. Banking services provided by Middlesex Federal Savings, F.A., Member FDIC. Novo says it is trusted by 250,000+ independent businesses.
- No monthly service fee, no fee to open an account, no required minimum or maximum deposit, no minimum balance. Novo says there are no transaction limits, but its FAQ adds that transaction limits still apply to ACH transfers, check deposits, ATM withdrawals and debit card purchases, and some limits can be increased after opening by request in the app or on the web.
- Free standard ACH; accepts incoming domestic and international wires at no cost; can send domestic and international wires; instant transfers between Novo accounts.
- Novo does not charge ATM fees and automatically reimburses third-party ATM fees at the end of each month, up to $7, at domestic and international ATMs.
- FDIC insurance up to $5,000,000 per depositor through Middlesex Federal Savings, F.A. and other banks in its sweep network.
- Novo Business Checking does not earn interest. Novo Savings, opened alongside it, pays 2% APY once the balance is $50,000 or more, with no spending or deposit targets.
- Virtual and physical debit cards; cash back of 2% on eligible purchases with a checking balance of $5,000 or more and 1% with a balance under $5,000 (per Novo's footnote).
- One account covers checking, payments, invoicing (free invoices with automatic reminders, card and link payments), bill pay, expense management, payroll, bookkeeping tools; also lists business credit, business loans and a business credit card under its Products menu. Open an account in about 10 minutes (Novo's claim).

### Mercury - mercury.com/pricing
- Fintech, not an FDIC-insured bank. Banking services provided through Choice Financial Group and Column N.A., Members FDIC. Deposit insurance covers the failure of an insured bank.
- Pricing page: banking services and essential tools always $0 per month; annual pricing shown is 15% off.
- Mercury plan $0 per month: no monthly fees, overdraft penalties or minimum balances; up to $5M in FDIC insurance; free ACH (same-day and next-day), domestic wires, real-time payments in USD.
- Mercury Plus $29.90 per month on annual pricing (30-day free trial): adds invoice with ACH debit at $1 per transaction, $50 off eligible LegalZoom Compliance Plans, 6 months free Xero.
- Mercury Pro $299 per month on annual pricing: invoice with ACH debit at $0 per transaction.
- Add-ons: Mercury Books $35 per month and Mercury Payroll $100 per month plus $50 per person paid per month, both waived until Dec 31, 2026.
- USD international wires $0. Non-USD international wires carry a 1% currency exchange fee.
- Treasury: yield up to 4.42%, available to eligible customers with a $250K Mercury balance; offered by Mercury Advisory, LLC, an SEC-registered investment adviser; yield is subject to investment risk.
- Corporate credit cards with 1.5% cashback. Free Mercury Personal account with any business plan.

### Relay - relayfi.com/pricing
- Fintech, not an FDIC-insured bank; banking services provided by Thread Bank, Member FDIC. Deposits qualify for up to $3,000,000 FDIC coverage through a sweep program.
- Starter $0 per month: 1.19% APY on savings, 1% cash back on credit cards, 20 checking accounts per business, built-in spend controls. Rates accurate as of the date on Relay's page and may change.
- Grow $30 per month (14-day trial): 1.87% APY, 1.25% cash back, multi-step approval rules, recurring invoices, bookkeeping automations.
- Scale $90 per month (limited-time discount from $120; 14-day trial): 3.21% APY, 1.5% cash back, 50 checking accounts per business, no fee for same-day ACH.
- Accounting integrations and unlimited users on all plans. Additional transaction fees apply.

### Bluevine - bluevine.com/business-checking
- Fintech, not a bank; deposits FDIC-insured through Coastal Community Bank, Member FDIC; up to $3 million per depositor via multiple banks.
- Standard: no monthly fee, no overdraft fees, no minimum balance, free standard ACH, unlimited transactions, no incoming domestic wire or ACH fees. 1.3% APY on balances up to $250,000 when the monthly activity goal is met: spend $500 per month with the Bluevine Business Debit Mastercard OR receive or deposit $2,500 per month in customer payments.
- Plus $30 per month: 1.75% APY on balances up to $250,000, no activity requirement. Premier $95 per month: 3.3% APY on all balances, no activity requirement. Fees can be waived under conditions Bluevine lists.
- Free invoicing, Tap to Pay, bill pay by same-day ACH, free standard ACH, check, domestic or international wire.
- Lending: Line of Credit up to $250K; Term Loan up to $500K from lending partners with repayment terms up to 24 months; SBA 7(a) loans up to $350K.

### Lili - lili.co
- Fintech, not a bank. Banking services provided by Sunrise Banks, N.A., Member FDIC. FDIC insurance up to $3M through a network of sweep program banks.
- Lili Core: $0 monthly fees, no minimum balance, Lili Visa debit card, no debit card fees for foreign transactions, in-network ATM use or inactivity. Express ACH, domestic and international wires (fees apply to wires; international wires only for legal entities such as LLCs, corporations and partnerships), checks.
- Lili Savings: variable APY effective January 13, 2026: 2.25% on balances up to and including $500,000, 4.00% on balances over $500,000 up to and including $1,000,000; nothing earned above $1,000,000; available on all Lili plans.
- Up to $250,000 in flexible capital embedded in the account.
- Lili Pro, Smart and Premium are paid tiers with monthly fees; amounts are not shown on lili.co (Lili points to its fee schedule). Cash back is for Pro, Smart and Premium only; accounting, tax preparation and invoicing software is for Smart and Premium only.

### Wise Business - wise.com/us/business and wise.com/us/pricing/business
- Wise is a Money Service Business registered with FinCEN, not an FDIC-insured bank.
- One-time set-up fee of 31 USD for getting account details to receive in 22 currencies; registering is free; holding money is free; no monthly subscription.
- Send wire transfers from as low as $1.13; receive domestic wires for a fixed $6.11 (Wise's own comparison: US banks often charge $15 to $30 to receive).
- Sending fees from 0.23%, varying by currency; converting money from 0.23%, varying by currency.
- Wise Multi-Currency Card: ATM withdrawals up to 250 USD per calendar month carry no Wise fee; after that 1.95 USD + 1.95% per withdrawal; ATM operators may charge their own fees.
- Interest feature: 3.68% variable APY on USD balances; pass-through FDIC insurance up to $250K on interest-earning funds through program banks.

### Chase Business Complete Banking - chase.com/business/banking/checking
- Chase is a branch bank: access to more than 14,000 ATMs and 5,000 branches.
- Business Complete Banking: $15 monthly service fee, waivable five ways including a $2,000 minimum daily ending balance; up to 20 paper and teller transactions and $5,000 in-branch cash deposits included each statement cycle; wire transfers carry fees that may apply.
- Chase Performance Business Checking: $40 or $0 (fee waivable); up to 250 account debits and $20,000 in-branch cash deposits per statement cycle.
- Chase Platinum Business Checking: $95 or $0 (fee waivable); up to 500 account debits and $25,000 in-branch cash deposits per statement cycle.
