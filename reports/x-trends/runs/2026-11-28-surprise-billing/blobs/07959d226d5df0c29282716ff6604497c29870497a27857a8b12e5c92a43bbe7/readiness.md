## Primary-source verification (all saved verbatim in research/sources/, retrieved 2026-11-28 UTC)
Intake claims checked against CMS:
1. Protection: "protects you from unexpected out-of-network bills from emergency room visits; non-emergency care related to visits at in-network hospitals, hospital outpatient departments, and ambulatory surgical centers; and air ambulance services" (cms-know-your-rights-insurance.txt, cms-medical-bill-rights.txt). VERIFIED. ER: "can't be charged any more ... than the in-network cost-sharing rate" by hospital, providers, air ambulance provider. VERIFIED.
2. Specialists: notice and consent form not allowed for emergency medicine, anesthesiology, pathology, radiology, neonatology, diagnostic services including radiology and laboratory, assistant surgeons, hospitalists, intensivists (cms-know-your-rights-insurance.txt). VERIFIED: supports "anesthesiologists/radiologists/labs generally can't balance-bill" for covered settings. Limit: protections do not apply at other settings such as a doctor's office that is not a hospital outpatient department, or out-of-network facilities. Applies to most private plans (employer incl. FEHB, Marketplace, individual); excluded plan types listed (short-term limited duration, sharing ministries, fixed indemnity, vision/dental-only).
3. Good faith estimate: for people who don't have or don't use insurance; scheduled at least 3 business days ahead or on request; dispute if a provider/facility bill is at least $400 more than that provider's estimate; start within 120 days of the initial bill; estimates are per provider (cms-know-your-rights-without-insurance.txt). VERIFIED. Intake wording "$400+ over estimate ... uninsured/self-pay, not insured" matches. Note: CMS defines it by "don't have or don't use insurance", so an insured person who chooses to self-pay also qualifies; page must say so.
4. Ground ambulance: "Generally ... aren't covered by billing protections in the No Surprises Act (unless a state law has different rules)"; CMS action plan lists 10 states as of 2021-11-15 (CO, DE, FL, IL, ME, MD, NY, OH, VT, WV) (cms-ground-ambulance-bill.txt). VERIFIED; the state list is dated, writer must say "as of" and send readers to state rules.
5. Help line: 1-800-985-3059 No Surprises Help Desk, 7 days a week, 350+ languages; complaint review, CMS follows up within 60 days if more info needed (cms-submit-complaint.txt). VERIFIED.
6. EOB steps: EOB is not a bill; fields Provider Charges, Allowed Charges, Paid by Insurer, Patient Balance; "Your bill should not be higher than the Patient Balance. If it is, talk to your provider." Check-your-bill-errors page: ask for detailed bill, contact insurer (it "might be able to fix billing errors with your health care provider"), compare to EOB (cms-read-eob.txt, cms-check-bill-errors.txt). VERIFIED. "Ask the plan to reprocess the claim": CMS wording is "contact your health insurance company"; no formal reprocess/appeal procedure named for this situation. NARROWED: page may say ask the plan to review/correct the claim as a practical step, labeled as such, not as a CMS procedure.
7. Not verified, excluded from facts: cost-sharing counts toward the in-network deductible; collections hold; credit-report effects of these bills; viral post contents (x.com links untrusted, not fetched). Medicare/Medicaid/VA/TRICARE/IHS: CMS says those plans "already protect you from some unexpected out-of-network bills"; no detail claimed.
Recency: CMS pages last modified 2026-09-10 to 2026-11-13 (rules overview page 2026-11-13: May 2026 IDR operations final rule; IDR is provider-plan payment dispute, not a patient process, so out of scope except one sentence that patients are removed from payment disputes between plans and providers per /nosurprises consumers text).
