**Planned takeaway**

If you have private insurance and an out-of-network anesthesiologist, radiologist, pathologist or lab billed you after care at an in-network hospital, outpatient department or surgery center, the federal rule generally limits you to in-network cost-sharing and those specialties cannot ask you to waive it, so compare the bill to the EOB Patient Balance, call the plan, then file with the CMS Help Desk if the bill is higher. Based on CMS pages read 2026-11-28. Applies to most private plans only; does not apply to uninsured/self-pay readers (use a good faith estimate and the 120-day, $400-over dispute), ground ambulances (check state law), doctor's offices that are not hospital outpatient departments, out-of-network facilities, short-term, sharing-ministry or fixed-indemnity plans. Would change if: CMS revises the rules, the reader signed a valid notice-and-consent for a non-exempt service, or the plan's EOB shows the amount is in-network cost-sharing.
