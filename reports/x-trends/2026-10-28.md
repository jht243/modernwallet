- [RELEASE] The adoption credit refund you may get automatically: how unused 2020–2024 credits can now boost your refundable credit (up to $5,000 per child) — for tax year 2025 only, unused carryforwards can increase the refundable portion. The IRS is issuing most of these refunds automatically by Dec 31, 2026, and generally no amended return is needed. This rule does not apply to 2026. Why now: IRS and Treasury clarified the change on Oct 28.
