## What a 7.40% Mortgage Rate Means for Your Monthly Payment

[Freddie Mac's](https://www.freddiemac.com/pmms) 30-year fixed average is 7.40% as of October 15, 2026, which works out to $2,769.52 a month in principal and interest on a $400,000 loan. The Primary Mortgage Market Survey (PMMS) is a weekly national average, and its 30-year figure is unchanged from the prior week and up from 6.27% a year earlier. The $2,769.52 leaves out property taxes, homeowners insurance, and private mortgage insurance (PMI), so add those in the [PITI (principal, interest, taxes, and insurance) calculator](/mortgage/piti-calculator/) with the rate from your own quote.

A quarter point is worth $67.89 a month on a $400,000 loan, because the 30-year payment at 7.15% is $2,701.63 instead of $2,769.52. Freddie Mac's 15-year average is 6.71%, down from 6.73% the prior week, which puts the same loan at $3,530.77 a month, or $761.25 more than the 30-year. Over the full term, the 15-year loan costs about $235,539 in interest and the 30-year loan about $597,027, each figure being total payments minus the $400,000 borrowed.

A refinance breaks even when the monthly savings repay the closing costs. Using 7.15% as an illustration rather than a quote, $6,000 in closing costs divided by the $67.89 monthly saving comes to about 88 months, or roughly 7.4 years. Run your lender's actual fees through the [closing cost calculator](/mortgage/closing-cost-calculator/).

The September Producer Price Index (PPI), out October 15, rose 1.3% for the month and 6.3% for the year, against forecasts of 0.6% and 5.4%, [InvestmentNews reports](https://www.investmentnews.com/equities/ppi-comes-in-much-hotter-than-expected-putting-more-rate-hike-pressure-on-the-fed/268612). CME FedWatch market probabilities then put the odds of a Federal Reserve (Fed) hike at 33.8% for the October 27 to 28 meeting, up from 29.3%, and 63.2% by December. These are market probabilities, not decisions. They are not a forecast of mortgage rates, so base a lock decision on your closing date and budget, as covered under rate locks below.
