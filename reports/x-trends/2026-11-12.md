- The DOL's new PBM fee-disclosure rule: what it means for your employer drug plan and your copays — The final rule makes pharmacy benefit managers (and affiliated brokers and consultants) disclose direct and indirect compensation to fiduciaries of large self-insured ERISA plans before contracts and twice a year after. It takes effect Jan 4, 2027 and applies to plan years starting on or after July 1, 2027. Angle: what changes for workers (and what doesn't) during open enrollment. Why now: DOL promoted the finalized rule on X Nov 10, in the middle of open enrollment season.
