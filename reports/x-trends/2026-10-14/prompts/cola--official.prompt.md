1. The Social Security Administration announced on Wednesday, October 14, 2026 that the 2027 cost-of-living adjustment (COLA) is 3.6%.
2. It raises the average retired-worker benefit by roughly $75 a month, to about $2,170 a month, in January 2027.
3. More than 75 million Americans will see the increase (CBS News).
4. The increase starts with January 2027 payments; SSI recipients see it starting December 31, 2026.
5. The 2026 COLA was 2.8%; the 2025 COLA was 2.5% and the 2024 COLA was 3.2%. The 3.6% is the largest COLA since 2023 (CNN reporting).
6. Arithmetic: 3.6% of a $2,000 monthly benefit is $72 a month, making $2,072, or $864 a year. 3.6% of $1,500 is $54.
