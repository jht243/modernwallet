## The Official 2027 Social Security COLA Is 3.6%

The 2027 Social Security COLA is 3.6%, announced by the SSA on Wednesday, October 14, 2026. The average retired worker gets roughly $75 more a month, and more than 75 million Americans will see the increase, according to [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/social-security-cola-2027-increase/). Payments rise in January 2027, or starting December 31, 2026, for Supplemental Security Income (SSI) recipients.

On a $2,000 monthly check, 3.6% adds $72, for a new benefit of $2,072 a month and $864 more over a year. On a $1,500 check, the raise is $54, bringing it to $1,554. To estimate your own raise, multiply your current monthly benefit by 0.036.

Medicare has not announced its 2027 premiums, so your net raise is not final. The 2026 Trustees Report projects the standard Part B premium rising $6.60, from $202.90 to $209.50. On the $2,000 example, that leaves $72.00 minus $6.60, or $65.40 a month before other deductions.

At 3.6%, this is the largest COLA since 2023, above 3.2% for 2024, 2.5% for 2025, and 2.8% for 2026. It also matches the top of the 3.4% to 3.6% range forecasters published before the announcement.
