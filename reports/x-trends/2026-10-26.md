- Trump's housing-cost executive order: when your mortgage insurance could be cancelled automatically and the title-insurance waiver for refinances — The Oct 22 order tells FHFA to pilot proactive mortgage-insurance cancellation once current-value equity and payment-history tests are met. It applies only to loans originated after the pilot starts. It also makes the title-acceptance pilot (no lender's title insurance on eligible refinances) a permanent nationwide program. Explain who benefits now and who doesn't, and the PMI math. Why now: it was signed Oct 22, NAHB is promoting it, and borrowers are asking whether it applies to existing loans (it doesn't).
