# Audit the complete rendered page

This contract applies to every routine that creates or changes website content, metadata, templates, injected sections, dates, internal links or structured data. It applies equally to local, global and cloud execution. It strengthens the existing independent audit and publication gates; it adds no phase or human approval.

## Prepare the answer before the approval chart

Finish research needed for the proposed answer, setup instructions, pricing, product availability, comparisons and takeaway before marking preparation Passed. “Fetch/verify in Phase 3” is unfinished preparation when the source supports the proposed answer. Optional unsupported material must be explicitly excluded from the approved scope, not presented as planned content with a future verification condition. Missing paid metrics may retain the authorized labeled fallback. Keep one preparation summary row and every complete new-page brief.

## Define the actual affected scope

Record every changed route and all routes affected by a shared template/helper, including affiliate, related-content, survey, attribution and date transformations. Enumerate these through the site's route registry or build output. A shared change is not confined to the example page that exposed it. Preserve unrelated content, exclusions, professional review and the existing no-addition branch. If a shared change affects a large set, mechanically inspect every affected route and independently review every distinct changed copy/template variant plus its source-support mapping. Record the complete route inventory and justify representative visual samples; never call a sample a full route check.

Prepare a JSON manifest with `author`, `canonicalBase`, and a nonempty `routes` array. Each route has `path`, optional `expectedUpdatedDate`, required on-page `requiredText` where useful, `forbiddenText` for the defects being repaired, and optional `growthFile`/`evidenceFile`. Use actual source dates rather than today's date for untouched pages. A route expected to carry an updated date must set `expectedUpdatedDate`; document genuine date-policy exceptions in the independent review.
