Enter the benefit before any Medicare deduction, as shown on your 2026 COLA notice or in my Social Security. The calculator multiplies that amount by 1.036, which applies the 3.6% COLA for 2027. It then rounds the result down to the next lower dime, because SSA rounds any monthly benefit that is not a multiple of $0.10 that way. If you entered a Medicare Part B premium, the calculator subtracts it and rounds the payment down to the next lower whole dollar. On a $2,000 benefit, multiplying gives exactly $2,072.00, so no dime rounding is needed. The results show the 2027 benefit to the cent, the monthly increase and the increase over 12 months. For $2,000, those increases are $72.00 a month and $864.00 a year. Below the calculator, the 2027 COLA increase chart lists common benefit amounts. The rows run from $500 to $4,000 in steps of $250. Each row shows the benefit now, the 2027 benefit and the monthly increase, all before any Medicare deduction. A $1,000 benefit becomes $1,036.00, a raise of $36.00 a month. A $3,000 benefit becomes $3,108.00, or $108.00 more each month. Every row rises by 3.6%, so the dollar increase grows as the benefit grows. If your benefit falls between two rows, type the exact amount into the calculator instead. SSA applies the COLA to your primary insurance amount (PIA), which is your benefit at full retirement age, and then recomputes your benefit from it. The calculator applies 3.6% straight to the amount you enter and skips that recomputation. If you claimed early or receive a family benefit, your actual 2027 amount can differ from this estimate by a few cents.
