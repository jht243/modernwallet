The salary calculator also works in reverse. Enter an hourly wage, your weekly hours and your paid weeks to find annual pay, or choose another pay period to convert that amount. For example, enter $25 an hour with a 40-hour week and 52 paid weeks. Annual pay is $52,000, or $1,000.00 a week and $2,000.00 every two weeks. Monthly pay is $4,333.33, before taxes and deductions. Share Interactive — edit any field How the Salary Conversion Works The salary calculator first turns the pay amount you enter into annual gross pay. For an hourly amount, it multiplies the hourly rate by hours per week and weeks per year. To convert an annual salary back to an hourly rate, it divides annual pay by hours per week times weeks per year. A $52,000 salary spread across 2,080 work hours gives $25.00 an hour. The period you select tells the calculator how to interpret your pay amount. You can enter pay by the hour, day, week, every two weeks, half month, month or year. It then shows an hourly headline rate alongside annual, monthly, twice-a-month, every-two-weeks, weekly and daily amounts. Enter the hours and days you work so the time-based amounts use your schedule rather than the starting values. Monthly pay is annual pay divided by 12. Twice-a-month pay is annual pay divided by 24. Every-two-weeks pay divides annual pay by half the number of paid weeks, which means 26 checks across 52 paid weeks. Daily pay divides annual pay by days worked per week times weeks per year. The starting schedule of 40 hours across 52 weeks produces 2,080 hours because 40 multiplied by 52 equals 2,080. Change either input when a pay offer uses a different schedule. For instance, a $60,000 salary works out to $28.85 an hour across 52 paid weeks at 40 hours a week. Across 50 paid weeks at the same weekly hours, the hourly figure is $30.00. The calculator uses the paid weeks you enter. It does not model unpaid leave separately. You can start with a monthly or biweekly amount instead of annual pay. Entering $5,000 a month gives $60,000 a year, or $28.85 an hour on the starting schedule. Entering $2,000 every two weeks gives $52,000 a year when you use 52 paid weeks. The hourly result is $25.00. An hourly offer also responds to a change in weekly hours: $30 an hour at 37.5 hours a week gives $58,500 a year and $4,875.00 a month when you enter 52 paid weeks. Enter the hours and paid weeks that match the offer you want to compare. Comparing Two Pay Offers A higher salary can pay less per hour. A $62,000 salary at 45 hours a week works out to $26.50 an hour (62,000 divided by 2,340 hours), while the $60,000 salary at 40 hours a week works out to $28.85 an hour. Paid weeks change the comparison too. A rate of $25 an hour for 40 hours a week is $52,000 over 52 paid weeks. Over 50 paid weeks, the same rate is $50,000, which averages $24.04 an hour across 2,080 hours (50,000 divided by 2,080). The pay schedule changes the size of each check but not the annual total. Twenty-six checks of $2,000 and 24 checks of $2,166.67 (rounded to the cent) both come to $52,000 a year. Put each offer on the same hours and paid weeks before you decide which one pays more. What Gross Pay Leaves Out The salary calculator shows gross pay before taxes and deductions. An hourly conversion tells you how annual pay relates to work time. It does not tell you what will reach a bank account. Use the paycheck calculator when you need take-home pay after federal income tax and payroll tax. Overtime, bonuses, unpaid leave and benefits are not modeled. If you are paid overtime or commission, enter an average pay amount and average hours for the conversion. Keep any comparison on the same basis. Bonuses are not modeled, so account for them separately when comparing pay offers. The hourly answer also changes when you work more hours, have fewer paid weeks or earn overtime, so update the inputs when those parts of an offer differ. Frequently asked questions How do I convert my annual salary to an hourly wage? Divide annual salary by hours per week multiplied by weeks per year. A $52,000 salary divided by 2,080 work hours is $25.00 an hour. Enter your own hours and paid weeks in the salary calculator if they differ from the starting schedule. How do I convert hourly pay to an annual salary? Multiply your hourly rate by hours per week, then by weeks per year. At $25 an hour, working 40 hours a week across 52 paid weeks gives $52,000 a year. Change the hours or paid weeks to match the pay offer you are comparing. How many hours are in a work year? A 40-hour week across 52 weeks gives 2,080 work hours. That figure comes from multiplying the weekly hours by the weeks worked, so it changes with your schedule. For example, 37.5 hours a week across 52 weeks gives 1,950 hours. Is a salary calculator accurate for monthly or biweekly pay? The salary calculator converts monthly and every-two-weeks amounts using the annual pay implied by your input. Monthly pay is annual pay divided by 12. Every-two-weeks pay depends on your paid weeks. Across 52 paid weeks, it represents 26 checks. Twice-a-month pay is annual pay divided by 24. How do I turn a monthly salary into an hourly rate? Multiply monthly pay by 12 to get annual pay, then divide by hours per week times weeks per year. A $5,000 monthly salary becomes $60,000 a year. At 40 hours across 52 weeks, the hourly rate is $28.85. Does this salary calculator include overtime? No. The salary calculator converts the pay amount and work schedule you enter without modeling separate overtime pay. If your pay varies with overtime, enter an average pay amount and average hours for the conversion. The result remains gross pay. Does this salary calculator show pay after taxes? No. The salary calculator shows gross pay before taxes and deductions. To estimate take-home pay after federal income tax and payroll tax, use the paycheck calculator . Do not use the gross monthly figure as an after-tax budget amount. What if I work fewer than 52 weeks a year? Enter the number of paid weeks that matches your schedule. Fewer paid weeks change the hourly conversion for a fixed annual pay amount. At 40 hours a week, a $60,000 salary converts to $30.00 an hour across 50 paid weeks, compared with $28.85 across 52. The calculator does not model unpaid leave separately.
