Net earnings equal your Schedule C profit minus half of your self-employment tax. For self-employment tax, 92.35% of profit serves as the earnings base. That base faces 12.4% for Social Security and 2.9% for Medicare. There is no self-employment tax under $400 of net earnings. You can calculate your tax using our self-employment tax calculator . Under IRS Publication 560 , self-employed business owners use a reduced contribution rate. A 25% plan rate becomes 20% of net earnings. A SEP IRA allows only this employer contribution. Earnings above the annual compensation limit are ignored. Official limits change each tax year under IRS Notice 2026-66 . For 2027, the elective deferral limit is $25,500. That limit was $24,500 for 2026. The section 415(c) annual limit is $75,000 for 2027. The previous annual limit was $72,000 for 2026. The annual compensation limit is $375,000 for 2027. It was $360,000 for 2026. Catch-up contributions sit outside the annual limit. The catch-up for age 50 or older is $8,500 for 2027. That catch-up was $8,000 for 2026. Between ages 60 and 63, the catch-up contribution is $11,750. The corresponding limit was $11,250 for 2026. A solo 401(k) allows the employer share plus an employee elective deferral. A SEP IRA can be established as late as the due date of your return, including extensions. A sole proprietor with no employees can adopt a section 401(k) plan after the tax year ends, if adopted by the filing deadline without extensions. Employee deferrals for a solo 401(k) must be elected by the end of the tax year. The calculator assumes a sole proprietor or single-member LLC with Schedule C profit and no employees. Frequently asked questions How much am I allowed to contribute to my SEP IRA? You can contribute up to 20% of your net self-employment earnings. For 2027, total contributions cannot exceed $75,000. For 2026, the maximum cap is $72,000. On $100,000 of profit, an owner under 50 can contribute $18,587. How is SEP IRA contribution calculated for the self-employed? The contribution starts by subtracting half of your self-employment tax from Schedule C profit to find net earnings. You then multiply those net earnings by an effective rate of 20%. Any earnings above the annual compensation limit are excluded. What is the solo 401(k) contribution limit for 2027? The overall limit is $75,000 for 2027 under section 415(c). This total includes an employee elective deferral of up to $25,500 plus the employer share. Savers age 50 or older can add an $8,500 catch-up outside the annual limit. For ages 60 to 63, the catch-up contribution is $11,750. What is the SEP IRA contribution deadline? You can set up and fund a SEP IRA as late as the due date of your income tax return, including extensions. Contributions made by that deadline are deductible for that tax year. How do SEP IRA contributions affect taxes? Contributions are deducted from your gross income for the tax year the contribution is made for. You must make the contribution by the tax return due date, including extensions, to take the deduction. Can I have a SEP IRA and a solo 401(k) at the same time? Official guidance in IRS Publication 560 does not address maintaining both plans at the same time. You should confirm your eligibility with a qualified tax professional or your plan provider before attempting to contribute to both. Does a SEP IRA or solo 401(k) allow more at low income? A solo 401(k) allows significantly more at low income levels because you can contribute employee deferrals up to your net earnings. On $20,000 of profit, net earnings are $18,587 after self-employment tax. A solo 401(k) permits the full $18,587 contribution. A SEP IRA is limited to 20% of net earnings, which is $3,717. Can I contribute if I also have a 401(k) at my day job? Yes, but the employee elective deferral limit is shared across all 401(k) and 403(b) plans you participate in. If you contribute $25,500 at your day job for 2027, your solo 401(k) employee deferral is $0. You can still make the full employer contribution of up to 20% of net self-employment earnings in either plan.
