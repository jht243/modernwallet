1. An Honest Assessment

We tell you what’s realistically possible, not what sells. If we don’t think we can help, or if you don’t need a firm at all, we’ll say so plainly.

2. Flat Fees in Writing, Before You Pay

Your fee is quoted in writing before you pay anything, so you decide with the full price in front of you. No hourly meter, no surprise charges halfway through.

3. An Experienced Tax Professional on Your Case

An experienced tax professional handles your case — never a commissioned salesperson chasing a quota. The person reviewing your situation is there to solve your problem.

4. Clear Communication

You always know exactly where your case stands. No black box and no chasing us for updates. You get a real point of contact and plain-English answers.

5. We Deal With the IRS for You

Once we’re on your case, the IRS calls and letters come to us. You never face the IRS alone, and you can stop dreading the mailbox.

6. Confidentiality

Your information is protected and never sold. We treat your financial details with the privacy they deserve, full stop.

7. No High-Pressure Tactics, No False Urgency

We never use scare tactics, manufactured deadlines, or pushy sales pressure, and we never promise a specific outcome. You get straight information and the time to decide.

8. The Real Options

We tell you which IRS programs you actually qualify for, whether that’s the Fresh Start Program, an Offer in Compromise, an affordable payment plan, penalty relief, or hardship status — based on your facts, not a sales script.

9. A 30-day money-back guarantee

If you’re not satisfied within your first 30 days, you can cancel for a full refund of what you’ve paid. This 30-day window applies to agreements signed on or after October 16, 2026; an agreement signed before then carries the window stated in it. Cancel by completing and returning the Notice of Cancellation form attached to your agreement; any payments you’ve made are refunded, and any signed instrument returned, within 10 days of your cancellation notice, and any related security interest is canceled.

10. Your Federal Taxpayer Bill of Rights

On top of our promises, you have ten rights guaranteed by federal law in your dealings with the IRS, and we honor every one of them. Read the official IRS Taxpayer Bill of Rights for the full list.
