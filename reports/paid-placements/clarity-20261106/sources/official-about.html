Why We Started Clarity Tax Relief

The tax-relief industry has earned a bad reputation, and it earned it honestly. Too many firms promise "pennies on the dollar," collect a big up-front fee, and disappear. Founder Melissa Ly started Clarity Tax Relief in 2018 because taxpayers in a frightening situation deserve the opposite of that — a straight answer and a real plan.

"At Clarity Tax Relief, our mission is simple: provide honest guidance, exceptional service, and effective solutions that help taxpayers regain control of their financial future."

Melissa Ly, Founder & Chief Tax Officer

That mission shows up in small, concrete ways — we'll tell you when you don't need to hire us, we put every fee in writing before you commit, and the person who reviews your case is an experienced tax professional, not a closer working a script.

About Clarity Tax Relief, Answered

Is Clarity Tax Relief affiliated with the IRS?

No. Clarity Tax Relief is an independent tax resolution firm. We are not affiliated with the IRS or any government agency. We represent taxpayers, not the government, and deal with the IRS on your behalf under a power of attorney you sign.

Where is Clarity Tax Relief located, and who do you serve?

Our office is at 1551 N Tustin Ave, Santa Ana, CA 92705, and we represent taxpayers in all 50 states. Most of our work happens by phone, email, and secure document upload, so where you live is rarely a barrier.

What does Clarity Tax Relief charge?

We quote flat fees in writing before any work begins, so you know the full cost up front — no hourly surprises and no pressure. The first conversation is a free, no-obligation consultation.

Who will I actually work with at Clarity?

You work directly with experienced tax professionals who deal with the IRS every day, not a commissioned salesperson handing you off after you sign. The person who reviews your case is the kind of person who handles it.
