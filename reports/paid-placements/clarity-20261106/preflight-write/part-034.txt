## What qualifies

An actual source-backed client deliverable in the existing canonical paid queue: a verified paid page mention, client article/Company Deep Dive, supplied article, or already explicitly owner-approved publish-first invoice order. Genuine canonical client-service factual corrections also qualify under the owner's November 5 clarification below, even when the correction is unpaid. A caller's `isClient` flag, a test order, an unpaid sales inquiry or ordinary independent editorial content does not qualify. Source-backed canonical upsell pages use the separately authorized two-review lane below; never mislabel them as paid. A correction remains a service, not a new paid order, and any unresolved source hold such as Shepherd's remains protected.

## Review and release

1. Run the normal source work, independent complete-page review and existing maximum of TWO targeted writer reworks. Keep the independent reviewer separate. Do not add a third writer round, regenerate the page, reset persisted attempts, switch providers to evade the limit or restart the clock in another invocation.
2. If the client candidate still has editorial findings at that limit, automatically release the exact latest candidate under `client-live-feedback-2026-11-04.1`. Do not ask Jonathan for another review-pass approval. Preserve the original FAIL verdicts, all findings, reviewed snapshots and rework counts. Record `client-review-release` separately from an editorial PASS; the client has not reviewed or approved it yet.
3. Keep the candidate and whole rendered-page evidence intact through the ordinary scoped landing path. A resumed run must recover the actual latest candidate/reviews and match its semantic fingerprints; it must not buy a replacement draft or a new audit after the limit. Missing/stale artifacts are a technical reconciliation task, not permission to invent a receipt or silently regenerate.
4. Mechanical build/parse, exact paid scope/links, ad removal, native ownership/fences, fresh source/payment/Gmail checks, exact candidate-versus-live output and normal deployment checks still decide whether publication technically succeeded. Fix a syntax/build defect within the existing authorization without treating it as a new editorial pass. A broken build or incorrect/missing sold link is never sent as completed work.
5. The exception does not override a customer cancellation, explicit prepublication copy/budget approval promise, source hold, opt-out/owner hold, destination exclusion, required professional approval or evidence/test honesty. Never invent product testing, credentials, payments, medical claims or a passing review. Resolve those actual dependencies through their existing owners; do not disguise them as discretionary editorial findings.

## Delivery and client feedback

After real publication and live/link verification, move the SAME order to fulfilled and queue its existing native P9 completion automatically. Preserve first liveAt, original payment/deadline, the existing three-hour P9 floor, reply window, lease, caps and duplicate guards. Do not leave it parked as “review exhausted.” Reread the full current conversation/manual SENT/provider history immediately before sending. Acknowledge delivery only after actual Gmail SENT and canonical receipt landing. The live-links email is the invitation for client feedback, not proof that feedback or approval has already occurred. Subsequent requested corrections use the existing source-backed service path.

No completed client order is rebuilt to validate this policy. No API cap, model choice, price, send window, excluded client/site or unrelated editorial workflow changes.
