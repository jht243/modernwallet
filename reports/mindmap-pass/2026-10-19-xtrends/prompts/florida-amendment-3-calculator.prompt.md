Rules (Florida TaxWatch property-tax resource center; Pinellas County Property Appraiser Amendment 3 page):
- Amendment 3 is on the Nov 3, 2026 Florida ballot.
- The homestead exemption for non-school levies (county, city, special districts) is currently about $50,000; Florida TaxWatch's calculator uses $51,411.
- If passed: $150,000 on January 1, 2027; $250,000 on January 1, 2028; adjusted annually for inflation (CPI) starting January 1, 2029.
- The exemption does not apply to school taxes, which make up about 40% of the average tax bill. The amendment does not change school taxes.
- The annual assessment growth cap for commercial, rental/investment and vacation (non-homestead) property would drop from 10% to 5%.
- Save Our Homes (3% or CPI cap on homestead assessment growth) and existing personal exemptions are unchanged.
- Permanent Florida residents as of December 31, 2026 get the larger exemptions. Newcomers start at $50,000 and reach $250,000 after 5 years. After 2030 local governments could shorten that wait.
- The Legislature must create a process for local governments to exempt all remaining value; no timeline is set.
- State economists estimate a recurring local property tax revenue loss of nearly $12 billion.
- One mill is $1 of tax per $1,000 of taxable value. The statewide average non-school rate used in published examples is 10.5 mills.
Worked examples (computed by the calculator, 10.5 mills):
- Assessed value $400,000: non-school bill today $3,660.18; 2027 $2,625.00; 2028 $1,575.00. Savings $1,035.18 in 2027 and $2,085.18 in 2028 (about $1,035 and $2,085).
- Assessed value $100,000: non-school bill today $510.18; $0.00 in 2027 and 2028. Savings $510.18 both years, because the exemption cannot exceed the home's value.
- Assessed value $400,000 at 15 mills: bill today $5,228.84; 2027 $3,750.00; 2028 $2,250.00. Savings $1,478.84 in 2027 and $2,978.84 in 2028.
- The savings figure of the extra exempt value: $98,589 in 2027 ($150,000 minus $51,411) and $198,589 in 2028 ($250,000 minus $51,411).
