According to a September 2026 Wall Street Journal report, the IRS is actively challenging how certain GRATs were operated. The IRS is asserting a $736 million gift tax bill against a Nashville couple, Chuck and Trisha Elcan, over GRATs that held HCA Healthcare shares. The couple is disputing the assessment in U.S. Tax Court, and the case remains pending and unresolved. The IRS claims the taxpayers made administrative missteps, while the Elcans maintain their planning followed standard industry practice.

That dispute turns on trust administration rather than the legality of the structure itself. To survive an audit, follow the statutory rules exactly. The annuity must be paid at least annually. Hard-to-value assets need defensible independent valuations, and all accounting records and legal paperwork must hold up to audit.
