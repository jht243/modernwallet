How it works The calculator gives both paths the same cash each month until the loan's scheduled payoff date. That cash is the mortgage payment plus the extra. Paying off early sends the extra to principal. Once the loan is gone, the full payment plus the extra is invested every month, including the cash left over in the payoff month. Investing sends the extra to investments every month while the loan runs on schedule. Investments grow monthly at your annual return, and at the end, tax is charged on gains (balance minus contributions) at your tax rate. Both homes are paid off by the original payoff date, so the comparison comes down to the after-tax invested balance on each path. The break-even return is the annual return at which both paths end equal. Compare it with the return you expect after fees. Below the break-even, paying off early wins, and above it, investing wins.
