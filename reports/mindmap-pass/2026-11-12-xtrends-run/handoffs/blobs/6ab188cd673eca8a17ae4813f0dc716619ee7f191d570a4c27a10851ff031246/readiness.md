## Planned intent-based takeaway (Informational/regulatory row of the intent table)
Answer: during 2026 open enrollment the rule changes nothing in the plans a worker is choosing for 2027: it starts Jan 4, 2027, applies only from the first plan year beginning on or after July 1, 2027 (for a calendar-year plan, the year starting Jan 1, 2028), covers only large self-insured plans, and sets no copay, premium or formulary requirement. What it changes is who sees the PBM's money flows (the employer's plan fiduciaries, twice a year and before each contract) and what they can audit. Practical meaning: whether a copay ever falls depends on what an employer does with the information, and the Department's own cited research calls the direct effect on consumer spending uncertain. Applies to: workers in an employer plan; fully insured and small self-insured plans are outside the detailed disclosures. What would change it: a plan year starting on or after July 1, 2027 (earlier applicability), an employer redesign after reading disclosures, or a change to the rule. Final body sentence (next action): ask the benefits team whether the plan is self-insured and when its plan year begins, since those two answers decide whether and when the rule reaches it, and compare the plans on offer by their own published copays, deductible and formulary.
