[SpaceX](https://www.spacex.com/) says it will give one Class A share to every currently active [Trump Account](https://trumpaccounts.gov), but no source says you must claim your child's account to get it or defines active. At The Modern Wallet, we start from what has actually been published. In an October 19, 2026 post on [X](https://x.com/elonmusk), Elon Musk originally promised one share to every child in America without naming an account type or delivery vehicle.

A November 25, 2026 [SpaceX release](https://ir.spacex.com/updates/releases-details/2026/SpaceX-to-Invest-in-the-Next-Generation-of-Americans-2026-r_LYQ5cH94/default.aspx) confirmed the company will donate one newly issued Class A share to each active account, and Musk will personally match that gift. According to [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/25/spacex-musk-trump-account-stock.html), Musk posted on X that his matching share will transfer when his lockup expires in June 2027. Neither the release nor CNBC defines what makes an account active, and SpaceX has not published a transfer date for its own share donation.

Claiming still matters if you want the account fully operational for your child. You claim a Trump Account through the official app or trumpaccounts.gov to receive the one-time $1,000 Treasury seed deposit for eligible children and to unlock family or employer contributions. Auto-enrolled children can receive philanthropic donations without an active claim, but no published rule confirms whether that policy applies to the SpaceX stock.
