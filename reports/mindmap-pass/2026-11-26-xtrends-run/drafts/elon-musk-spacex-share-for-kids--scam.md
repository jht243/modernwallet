## Watch for Free SpaceX Share Scams

Any unsolicited message that asks you to pay a fee, send cryptocurrency, enter your child's Social Security number on an unverified site, or reserve a SpaceX share is a scam. The official [SpaceX release](https://ir.spacex.com/updates/releases-details/2026/SpaceX-to-Invest-in-the-Next-Generation-of-Americans-2026-r_LYQ5cH94/default.aspx) outlines no claim step to qualify for stock, and no source states whether claiming an account is required to receive the donation. Neither SpaceX nor [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/25/spacex-musk-trump-account-stock.html) defines what qualifies an account as active.

Official Trump Account email comes only from no-reply@TrumpAccounts.Treasury.gov. You manage and claim an account through the official mobile app or directly on [trumpaccounts.gov](https://trumpaccounts.gov). As of November 26, 2026, the official website carried no notices or instructions regarding SpaceX shares.

If a communication asks you to pay an administrative fee or speed up a stock transfer, stop and verify the sender. Call the Treasury support line at 1-866-USA-4547 before responding to any unexpected contact. To review additional ways to protect your child's data and spot fraudulent portals, see our guide on [Trump Account scams](/guides/trump-account-scams/).
