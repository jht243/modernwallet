## Do You Need to Claim a Trump Account to Get the SpaceX Share?

No official source states that claiming a Trump Account is required to receive the SpaceX share, nor does any source state that claiming makes an account active. [SpaceX](https://www.spacex.com/) announced in its [primary investor release](https://ir.spacex.com/updates/releases-details/2026/SpaceX-to-Invest-in-the-Next-Generation-of-Americans-2026-r_LYQ5cH94/default.aspx) that it will grant one newly issued Class A share to every currently active Trump Account, but active is not defined by SpaceX's release or CNBC. While auto-enrolled children can receive philanthropic contributions without a parent or guardian claiming an account, you should still consider completing the claim process on [trumpaccounts.gov](https://trumpaccounts.gov) to secure the one-time $1,000 Treasury deposit for eligible children born between 2025 and 2028, unlock family and employer contributions up to $5,000 per year, and control the account and manage investments.

If you decide to claim the account now, Treasury outlines six steps on [trumpaccounts.gov](https://trumpaccounts.gov) or through our [guide on how to claim a Trump Account](/guides/how-to-claim-a-trump-account/):

1. Download the official Trump Accounts app from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Verify your identity.
3. Confirm your relationship to the child.
4. Review the child's information.
5. Accept the account terms.
6. Optionally claim the one-time $1,000 Treasury contribution if the child is eligible.
