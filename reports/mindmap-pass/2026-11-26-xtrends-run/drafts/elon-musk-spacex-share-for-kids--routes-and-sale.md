## When Can the SpaceX Share Be Sold and What Is Still Unknown

[CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/25/spacex-musk-trump-account-stock.html), citing Treasury rules, reports that families cannot refuse stock gifts and that donated shares generally cannot be sold until the earlier of five years or the end of the calendar year in which the beneficiary turns 17. 

How those shares would land in an account remains unannounced. Under the temporary regulations in Treasury Decision (T.D.) 10056, published in the [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/09/30/2026-20026/trump-accounts), corporate or individual stock donations must route through an eligible 501(c)(3) charity or government entity. Federal rules treat those donations as general contributions split equally across an entire qualified class, and donors cannot select individual recipients. [SpaceX](https://www.spacex.com/) has not stated whether it will use this charitable general-contribution mechanism or another structure.

Inside Trump Accounts, families cannot trade or buy single stocks directly, as covered in our guide on [what Trump Accounts are invested in](/guides/what-are-trump-accounts-invested-in/). If shares were ever delivered outside Trump Accounts, children typically hold individual equities in a custodial Uniform Transfers to Minors Act (UTMA) or Uniform Gifts to Minors Act (UGMA) brokerage account managed by an adult. Our guide covers [how to open a custodial brokerage account for kids](/guides/how-to-open-a-custodial-brokerage-account-for-kids/) if you want one anyway.

Several material terms remain unconfirmed across corporate announcements and government records:

1. SpaceX transfer timing: CNBC reported that Gwynne Shotwell's separate personal gift of over 2 million shares is expected in December 2026, and Elon Musk stated on X that his matching share transfers when his lockup expires in June 2027. SpaceX has not announced a transfer date for its company-issued share.
2. The definition of an active account: The SpaceX announcement pledges one share to every currently active Trump Account, but active is not defined by SpaceX's release or CNBC. No source confirms whether claiming an account on [trumpaccounts.gov](https://trumpaccounts.gov) is required to receive the share.
3. Tax liability: No source has confirmed any tax treatment for the shares.
