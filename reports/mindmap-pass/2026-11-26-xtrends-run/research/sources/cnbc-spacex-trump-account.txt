- Treasury rules: families cannot refuse stock gifts; generally cannot sell until the earlier of five years or the end of the calendar year in which the beneficiary turns 17.
- Accounts open to any US child under 18 with an SSN; children born 2025-2028 may get one-time $1,000 Treasury deposit.
