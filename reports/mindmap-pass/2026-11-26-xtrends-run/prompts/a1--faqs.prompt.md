### Is Elon Musk really giving every kid a SpaceX share?
Elon Musk posted on X on October 19, 2026, stating that he plans to give one of his SpaceX shares to every child in America next year. However, this statement was an informal social media post. Neither Musk nor SpaceX has published any rules, claim form, or timing beyond the post.

### Do I need to sign up to get the free SpaceX share?
No sign-up form, registration portal, or official application exists today. Anyone claiming to offer an early registration form or asking for a processing fee is running a scam. Until Musk or SpaceX publishes terms, treat any sign-up page as fake.

### Will the SpaceX share go into my child's Trump Account?
Nobody knows, because Elon Musk did not mention Trump Accounts in his post. If he did use them, current federal rules would require the gift to go through a charity to a whole group of children. Under current rules, an unclaimed account could still receive that transfer. For now, there is nothing to do in the Trump Accounts app for the SpaceX share.

### How much is one SpaceX share worth?
SpaceX trades on Nasdaq under the ticker SPCX and closed at $146.03 on October 16, 2026, the last trading day before Musk's post. The equity has traded between $104.83 and $225.64 over its 52-week range following its June 2026 initial public offering at $135. The price changes every trading day, so check a live quote on [Stock Analysis](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/spcx/).

### Is the "claim your free SpaceX share" message I got a scam?
Yes, any communication directing you to claim a SpaceX share today is a scam. No distribution network, claim portal, or application exists. Official Trump Account email comes only from no-reply@TrumpAccounts.Treasury.gov. Never pay a fee, send cryptocurrency, or share your child's Social Security number to claim a SpaceX share.
