You cannot claim Elon Musk's [SpaceX](https://www.spacex.com/) share for your child, because Musk has not said how he will deliver it or through what account. Every guide we write at The Modern Wallet starts from what has actually been published, and here that is one post on [X](https://x.com/elonmusk).

On Monday, October 19, 2026, Musk posted: "Next year, I will give one of my SpaceX shares to every kid in America." He added: "This will be the first of many." [Census Bureau](https://www.census.gov/) estimates count about 72 million children under 18 in the United States. Musk's post does not mention Trump Accounts at all. They come up because nearly every U.S. child already has one, and in July a SpaceX executive pledged a similar gift to them. Claiming or opening a Trump Account is not a way to claim the share, and doing so does not reserve stock for your child.

2. Watch for official terms from Musk or SpaceX that name how the shares would be delivered.
3. Ignore any message offering to claim, reserve, or speed up the share.

Our guidance would change if Musk or SpaceX publishes official terms that name a delivery route.

Waiting is the wrong plan for families who want their children invested now. If you want your child to own SpaceX stock today, buy it in a custodial account opened by an adult. See our guide on [how to buy SpaceX stock](/guides/how-to-buy-spacex-stock/).

## Two Unconfirmed Ways the Shares Could Reach Kids

Musk has named no route, and the two realistic ones work very differently.

Route 1, through Trump Accounts: Under the temporary regulations in Treasury Decision (T.D.) 10056, published in the [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/09/30/2026-20026/trump-accounts), a person or company can donate stock only through an eligible 501(c)(3) charity. That gift counts as a general contribution, split equally across an entire qualified class. Donated shares must be issued by a domestic corporation, trade on a registered national exchange, and carry no transfer restrictions. The donor cannot select individual children, and recipient families cannot select the stock or refuse the gift.

Under these same Treasury rules, the trustee may hold donated shares for up to five years before selling them into the account's index fund, so your child may never keep the share there. Because unclaimed auto-enrolled accounts can still receive general contributions under current rules, you would not need to claim an account first for a transfer to land. Families cannot buy single stocks inside these accounts either, as explained in our guide on [what Trump Accounts are invested in](/guides/what-are-trump-accounts-invested-in/).

Route 2, outside Trump Accounts: A child usually holds stock in a custodial (UTMA/UGMA) account opened by an adult. Musk has described no process for delivering shares this way, so nobody knows whether parents would need a custodial account. He also did not say how the shares would be taxed or whether non-citizen children qualify. Our guide covers [how to open a custodial brokerage account for kids](/guides/how-to-open-a-custodial-brokerage-account-for-kids/) if you want one anyway.

Until Musk picks one, neither route asks anything of parents.

## Reasons to Claim a Trump Account That Have Nothing to Do With SpaceX

Claiming your child's Trump Account does nothing for the SpaceX share, but it matters for other money that reaches only claimed accounts. Treasury press release [SB-0642](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0642) says automatic enrollment of eligible children under 18 with a valid Social Security number was completed October 1, 2026. Unclaimed accounts currently sit in a Treasury master trust.

Unclaimed accounts can receive broad general contributions, but family and employer contributions remain blocked until a parent or guardian claims the account. To learn which minors qualify for automatic enrollment, see our guide on [Trump Account eligibility](/guides/trump-account-eligibility/). U.S.-citizen children born January 1, 2025 through December 31, 2028 can get a one-time $1,000 federal seed, but only if the account is claimed.

Claimed accounts also allow parents, relatives, and family friends to contribute up to $5,000 annually per child, with participating employers contributing up to $2,500 within that total limit. Money is locked until January 1 of the calendar year the child turns 18. You can model how regular deposits accumulate using our free [Trump Account calculator](/trump-account/).

Some private gifts already go through Trump Accounts. Michael and Susan Dell pledged $6.25 billion for $250 deposits to 25 million children born 2016 through 2024 in ZIP codes with median family income about $118,000 or less. No application is needed. Our [Dell Trump Account $250](/guides/dell-trump-account-250/) guide explains who qualifies. For a broader look at how the entire system operates, read our guide with [Trump Accounts explained](/guides/trump-accounts/).

## Watch for Free SpaceX Share Scams

Because no official claim process exists, every message instructing you to claim a share right now is fraudulent.

Official Trump Account email comes only from no-reply@TrumpAccounts.Treasury.gov. You should never pay an administrative processing fee, send cryptocurrency, or provide your child's Social Security number to an unverified third-party website.

If a notice claims to be about your child's Trump Account, call the Treasury support line at 1-866-USA-4547 or go to [TrumpAccounts.gov](https://trumpaccounts.gov). Treasury has published nothing about Musk's share. For additional methods to identify fraudulent portals and protect your family's records, review our detailed guide on [Trump Account scams](/guides/trump-account-scams/).
