Allstate outage, as reported:
- PYMNTS (published Nov 25, 2026; summary only): Allstate's phone system, email, payment services, website and mobile app have had outages since Monday, November 23, 2026 (per reports published Nov 25). Allstate told PYMNTS that some access has been restricted but it continues to respond to and settle claims, and that customers who cannot make a payment because of the disruption will not lose coverage or be charged late fees. The Roadside Assistance site was down Wednesday (Nov 25).
- TechCrunch (published Nov 25, 2026, 7:08 AM PST; summary only): a multiday outage since Monday morning; customers nationwide had trouble accessing accounts, policy information, claims and bill payments through the website or app; local agents were fielding calls. Allstate has not disclosed the cause or a resolution timeline. The company statement said some access was restricted for 48 hours, claims were still being handled, and customers whose payments are affected will not lose coverage or be charged late fees. (Neither report says how long the no-lapse and no-late-fee treatment lasts. Do not mention anything else from TechCrunch.)
- Our search found no first-party Allstate notice or state insurance department statement on the outage, so the statement is attributable only through PYMNTS and TechCrunch.
California Department of Insurance, Automobile Insurance guide (2021 edition, PDF dated May 12, 2021; full text read):
- Once issued, an auto policy can be canceled or non-renewed for only three reasons: fraud or material misrepresentation, nonpayment of premium, or a substantial increase in the hazard insured against.
- For cancellation for nonpayment of premium, the insurer must give you 10-day written notice after the payment due date before it can effectively cancel the policy.
- Your auto policy is a contract that explains your costs, coverages and exclusions. Read the Declarations page, a useful summary. Call your agent, broker or insurance company if you do not understand something. Tell them if you sell or buy a car.
- Insurers give proof of insurance that lists the insured vehicles and the insured's name, the policy number and the dates the policy starts and ends; keep the card in the car. California law requires evidence of financial responsibility in the vehicle; driving without insurance is illegal and can lead to a ticket, a license suspension or an impounded vehicle.
- Costs vary, even in the same area; compare costs and coverage from several companies. California Department of Insurance consumer hotline: 1-800-927-4357.
NAIC Compendium of State Laws, Compulsory Motor Vehicle Insurance chart (state entries marked (10/26); text read):
- Missouri: written notice at least 30 days before the proposed effective date of a cancellation or nonrenewal; where cancellation is for nonpayment of premium, at least 10 days' notice of cancellation.
- New Jersey: a cancellation notice is not effective unless mailed or delivered at least 20 days before the effective date; where cancellation is for nonpayment of premium, at least 15 days' notice accompanied by the reason.
- These are state rules for notice of cancellation. They say nothing specific about outages.
