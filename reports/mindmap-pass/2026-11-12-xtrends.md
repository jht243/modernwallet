**Planned takeaway**

For plan year 2027 choices, treat the rule as background: it starts Jan 4, 2027, reaches a plan only from its first plan year beginning on or after July 1, 2027 (for a calendar-year plan, Jan 1, 2028), covers only large self-insured plans, and gives PBM compensation details to the employer's fiduciaries, not to workers. Compare the plans on offer by their own published copays, deductible and formulary, because the rule sets none of those. Whether copays ever fall depends on what the employer does with the disclosures and the Department's cited research calls the direct effect uncertain. Applies to workers in employer plans; fully insured and small self-insured plans are outside the detailed disclosures. Changes if the plan year starts on or after July 1, 2027, if an employer redesigns the benefit after reading disclosures, or if the rule changes.
