Social Security retirement benefits rise 3.6% for 2027, an average increase of $75 a month, according to the [SSA press release](https://www.ssa.gov/news/en/press/releases/2026-10-14.html). The Social Security Act ties the COLA to the Consumer Price Index for Urban Wage Earners and Clerical Workers (CPI-W). The 2027 COLA reflects the CPI-W increase from the third quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026.

At 3.6%, this COLA is the largest since the 8.7% raise for 2023. The 2026 COLA was 2.8%. For more, see the SSA [COLA page](https://www.ssa.gov/cola/).

SSA estimates these average monthly benefits payable in January 2027, before and after the COLA:

| Beneficiary type | Before COLA | After 3.6% COLA | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| All retired workers | $2,095 | $2,170 | +$75 |
| Aged couple, both receiving benefits | $3,238 | $3,355 | +$117 |
| Widowed mother and two children | $3,932 | $4,074 | +$142 |
| Aged widow(er) alone | $1,936 | $2,005 | +$69 |

The maximum benefit for a worker retiring at full retirement age rises to $4,188 a month. It was $4,152 in 2026, a $36 increase. To estimate your own raise, multiply your current monthly benefit by 0.036, or enter it in the [Social Security COLA calculator](/social-security-cola-calculator/).

If you have a personal [my Social Security account](https://www.ssa.gov/myaccount/), you can view your COLA notice online starting in late November. Online notices arrive up to three weeks earlier than the paper notice. Account holders can also choose text or email alerts.

If you do not have an account, create one and enroll in paperless notices by November 18 to see the notice online. Everyone else gets the notice by mail in December. The one-page notice lists the exact dates and dollar amounts of your new benefit and any deductions.

The 2027 Medicare Part B premium has not been announced. Information about 2027 Medicare changes will be posted on [Medicare.gov](https://www.medicare.gov/). If you are enrolled in Medicare, your 2027 premium amount will appear in the my Social Security Message Center starting in late November.
