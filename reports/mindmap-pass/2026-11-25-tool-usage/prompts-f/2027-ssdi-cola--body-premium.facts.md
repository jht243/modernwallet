- CMS (fact sheet "2027 Medicare Parts A & B Premiums and Deductibles", November 17, 2026): the standard monthly Medicare Part B premium is $209.40 for 2027, an increase of $6.50 from $202.90 in 2026. The annual Part B deductible is $292 in 2027, up $9 from $283 in 2026. Higher-income enrollees pay an income-related monthly adjustment amount on top; the first tier brings the total to $293.10 (modified adjusted gross income above $112,000 for individuals or $224,000 for joint filers).
- ModernWallet's calculator: the 2027 Part B field starts at 0 so the gross raise shows first; one button fills in the $209.40 standard premium; the deposit-mode field for the 2026 premium starts at $202.90. Enrollees with an income-related surcharge enter the amount on their notice. The Message Center in my Social Security shows the premium and the COLA notice shows the exact figure.
- Example: a $1,642 benefit with a $200 premium entered pays $1,501 (existing page figure). A $1,850 benefit becomes $1,916.60 after the 3.6% COLA and pays $1,707 after the $209.40 premium (SSA rounds the benefit down to the dime and the payment down to the whole dollar).
