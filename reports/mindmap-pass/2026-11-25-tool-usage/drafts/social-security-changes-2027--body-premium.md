When the Part B premium increases, it reduces the net gain from your annual cost-of-living adjustment (COLA).

The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) fact sheet was published on November 17, 2026. The standard monthly Medicare Part B premium is $209.40 for 2027. That is a $6.50 increase over the $202.90 rate, alongside an annual deductible of $292.

 If you are enrolled in Medicare, your 2027 premium amount will appear in the [my Social Security](https://www.ssa.gov/myaccount/) Message Center starting in late November.
