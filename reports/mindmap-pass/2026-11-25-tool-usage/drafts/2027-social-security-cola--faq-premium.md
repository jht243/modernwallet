Yes, your Medicare Part B premium deduction increases by $6.50 over the 2026 standard rate of $202.90. The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) announced the 2027 standard premium of $209.40 on November 17, 2026, in its CMS fact sheet. On a $2,000 check with the official 3.6% cost-of-living adjustment (COLA), the net gain is $65.50 a month. If you pay an income-related monthly adjustment amount (IRMAA), that surcharge is deducted as well.
