Yes, one click applies the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) final $209.40 standard premium from the CMS fact sheet, though the field starts at 0 until you click. Higher-income enrollees enter their own amount from their notice, or check the Message Center of their [my Social Security](https://www.ssa.gov/myaccount/) account. A benefit of $1,642 with a $200 deduction leaves a net payment of $1,501.
