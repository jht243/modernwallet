The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) announced the 2027 standard Part B premium on November 17, 2026, at $209.40, up $6.50 from $202.90, in the CMS fact sheet. The net size of the SSDI raise is the COLA raise minus that increase. The COLA notice and the my Social Security Message Center show the exact premium and deductions. $59 is the gross raise on the $1,642 average benefit and the Part B increase of $6.50 comes out of it for standard-premium enrollees, leaving about $52.50.
