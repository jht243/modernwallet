answer: Verdict — yes. The rules that decide how much of a Social Security benefit is taxable did not change. What changed is a new deduction of up to $6,000 per person age 65 or older ($12,000 for a married couple filing jointly when both qualify), for tax years 2025 through 2028, that phases out by 6% of MAGI above $75,000 ($150,000 joint). It lowers taxable income, which can erase the tax on benefits for many middle-income retirees, but it does not exempt the benefit itself.
