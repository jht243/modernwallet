CLOSED FACT LIST:
- FDIC insures deposits to at least $250,000 per depositor, per ownership category, at each FDIC-insured bank; coverage is automatic for any deposit account opened at an FDIC-insured bank.
- An online bank's deposits are covered only if that bank is FDIC-insured; check with the FDIC's BankFind tool by bank name, FDIC certificate number, web address, location, or branch.
- FDIC warns that fake bank sites misuse the FDIC name or logo or display "Member FDIC" without being insured; compare the bank's official name against the URL, look for misspellings or unusual web addresses, and verify in BankFind before sharing personal information or sending money.
- The FDIC phone line for deposit insurance questions is 1-877-ASK-FDIC.
- Credit unions are insured by the NCUA, not the FDIC (see the credit union comparison on this site).
- General tendencies (present as tendencies that vary by institution, never as guarantees, tell the reader to compare current terms): online banks have no or few branches and often compete on higher savings yields and lower fees; traditional banks offer branches and in-person services and tend to make cash deposits simpler.
