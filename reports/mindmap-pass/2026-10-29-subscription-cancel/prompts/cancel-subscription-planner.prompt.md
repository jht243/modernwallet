- The Eighth Circuit vacated the FTC’s 2024 Negative Option Rule amendments because the FTC had not followed a statutory preliminary regulatory analysis requirement. This was procedural, not a decision on the merits; do not say the entire federal framework was abolished. Source checked 2026-10-29: https://ecf.ca8.uscourts.gov/opndir/25/07/243137P.pdf
- The FTC’s February 2026 Federal Register notice conformed the rule text to the vacatur and recodified pre-2024 text. It is not a new click-to-cancel law. Source checked 2026-10-29: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-02-12/pdf/FR-2026-02-12.pdf
- FTC’s current rule page records a March 11, 2026 advance notice seeking comment on possible amendments. An ANPRM is not binding law. Source checked 2026-10-29: https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/rules/negative-option-rule
- ROSCA, 15 U.S.C. § 8403, applies to covered internet transactions using a negative-option feature. It requires material-term disclosure before billing information, express informed consent, and a simple way to stop recurring charges. It does not create a universal one-click or same-channel rule for every subscription. Source checked 2026-10-29: https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/8403
- FTC consumer advice discusses recording terms, identifying the actual biller, monitoring charges, and reporting problems; it is not individualized legal advice. Source checked 2026-10-29: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/getting-and-out-free-trials-auto-renewals-and-negative-option-subscriptions
- California Automatic Renewal Law amendments took effect July 1, 2025. For covered offers, the law has consent and cancellation requirements, including online cancellation where covered enrollment was online. Coverage and exceptions depend on the transaction. Source checked 2026-10-29: https://www.oag.ca.gov/news/press-releases/attorney-general-bonta-issues-consumer-alert-california%E2%80%99s-automatic-renewal-law
- Colorado SB 25-145 sets channel-specific cancellation requirements for covered offers/renewals beginning February 16, 2026, with applicability based on how and when the contract was offered or renewed. Do not apply it to earlier or uncovered contracts. Source checked 2026-10-29: https://www.leg.colorado.gov/bills/sb25-145
- Maine 10 M.R.S. § 1210-C covers listed automatic renewals for online media players, mobile apps, online software and health-club memberships. Covered sellers must accept cancellation using the same method and manner as signup; definitions and exclusions control. Source checked 2026-10-29: https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/10/title10sec1210-C.html
- New York GBL § 527-a requires an easy cancellation mechanism through the same medium plus other cancellation methods for covered renewal offers; it prohibits obstruction or unreasonable delay. It exempts licensed security-system alarm operators and specified other businesses. Do not position it as an ADT remedy where the exemption applies. Source checked 2026-10-29: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GBS/527-A
- New York GBL § 624 gives health-club members cancellation rights and methods in specific circumstances, including a three-business-day right in covered contracts. It is not an unrestricted right to cancel every gym contract at any time. Source checked 2026-10-29: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GBS/624
- NYC’s Click-to-Cancel rule is effective October 1, 2026. For covered offers it requires cancellation through the same medium as consent and prohibits obstruction or unreasonable delay. Licensed alarm operators and other listed categories are exempt. Source checked 2026-10-29: https://www.nyc.gov/content/dam/nycgov/nyc-main/pdf/2026/Click-to-Cancel-Rule.pdf
- NYC DCWP provides a consumer summary and complaint route; filing a complaint is not a guaranteed individual resolution. Source checked 2026-10-29: https://www.nyc.gov/main/click-to-cancel
- AT&T publishes separate wireless and internet cancellation help routes. Account ownership, service and equipment-return requirements vary; do not promise one channel or instant cancellation.
