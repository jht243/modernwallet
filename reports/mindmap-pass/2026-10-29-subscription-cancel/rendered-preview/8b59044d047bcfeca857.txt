Why does the checklist ask for my state? Your state selection adjusts environmental hazard toggles such as freezing winters or coastal wind zones to keep tasks relevant. State statutes also govern security deposit limits, return deadlines, disclosure forms, and notice rules. Items on these topics tell you to check your state's rule instead of stating a number.
