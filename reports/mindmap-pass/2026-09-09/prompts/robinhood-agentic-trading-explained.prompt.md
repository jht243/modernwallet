**Robinhood Cortex** — Robinhood Gold's built-in AI research assistant. Announced March 2025, rolled out to Gold members through 2026 (~1 million users cited on a 2026 earnings call). No separate price; included with Robinhood Gold ($5/month or $50/year). Features: Stock Digests (why a stock moved), Portfolio Digests (plain-language portfolio insights), Trade Builder (turns a thesis into an options strategy), conversational assistant. CRITICAL: Cortex is NOT autonomous — Robinhood's own March 2025 page says it "is not placing trades for you"; it executes only USER-INITIATED trades and is positioned as insights, not investment advice. Source https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/introducing-strategies-banking-and-cortex/.
**Robinhood Agentic Trading** — a SEPARATE product. Lets a customer's OWN external AI agent place equity trades through Robinhood's official Model Context Protocol (MCP) server. Genuinely agentic. Launched in BETA on May 27, 2026, stocks only. How it works: you open a SEPARATE, dedicated ring-fenced account/wallet; the agent can only use funds pre-loaded there and cannot touch the rest of your portfolio; some trades generate a preview requiring your approval. Safety: one-tap kill switch, real-time notification of every agent trade, Robinhood fraud staff review. A companion agentic virtual credit card launched alongside for Gold Card holders. Announced-not-shipped: options, crypto, event contracts, futures, prediction markets. Whether agentic trading requires Gold is UNCONFIRMED — do not assert it does. Sources https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/27/robinhood-now-lets-your-ai-agents-trade-stocks/ and https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/hood-summit-2025-news/.
**Robinhood Strategies** — managed portfolios, no large-language-model involved. Mention only to disambiguate from the above two.
Safety framing (objective, not fear-mongering): regulators warn AI trading claims of guaranteed/high returns are red flags (CFTC advisory Jan 25, 2024, https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/8854-24). Robinhood's agentic product is walled-off and approval-gated, which is the opposite of an anonymous "money machine" bot — say so plainly.
