## CLOSED FACT LIST — anything not here you do not know. Never invent a number.
- AI stock trading spans three different things a reader conflates: (1) **AI research/analytics tools** that score or screen stocks (e.g. Danelfin's AI Score 1–10, Kavout's Kai Score) — https://danelfin.com, https://www.kavout.com; (2) **AI signal/scanner tools** that flag intraday trades (e.g. Trade Ideas' Holly) — https://www.trade-ideas.com; (3) **agentic trading**, where an AI agent actually places trades. Robinhood launched Agentic Trading in beta on May 27, 2026 — your own AI agent trades stocks via a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server into a separate ring-fenced wallet, with a kill switch and approval previews. Sources https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/27/robinhood-now-lets-your-ai-agents-trade-stocks/ and https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/hood-summit-2025-news/.
- What "agentic trading" means: an AI that can take actions (place orders) on your behalf, not just answer questions. Contrast with a research assistant like Robinhood Cortex, which only executes user-initiated trades. Cortex source https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/introducing-strategies-banking-and-cortex/.
- Does AI actually work / is it safe? The honest answer: AI tools can find patterns and speed up research, but no AI predicts the market. CFTC advisory Jan 25, 2024: "AI technology can't predict the future or sudden market changes"; claims of high or guaranteed returns are red flags (https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/8854-24). SEC "AI washing" enforcement March 18, 2024 fined two advisers for overstating AI use (https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2024-36). SEC/NASAA/FINRA Investor Alert Jan 25, 2024 (https://www.finra.org/investors/insights/artificial-intelligence-and-investment-fraud): verify an adviser's registration first.
