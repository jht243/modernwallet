## Regulators on AI trading / AI washing (use for the "is it legit / is it safe" angle)
- **CFTC Customer Advisory, "AI Won't Turn Trading Bots into Money Machines," January 25, 2024.** Warns fraudsters exploit AI hype to tout automated trading algorithms; "AI technology can't predict the future or sudden market changes"; claims of high or guaranteed returns (e.g. "100% win rates") are red flags of fraud. Cites Mirror Trading International, which promised at least 10% monthly returns and misappropriated over $1.7 billion in bitcoin from about 23,000 people. Source: https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/8854-24
- **SEC "AI washing" enforcement, March 18, 2024 (SEC press release 2024-36).** SEC charged two investment advisers, Delphia (USA) Inc. and Global Predictions Inc., with false/misleading statements about their use of AI. Penalties: Delphia $225,000; Global Predictions $175,000. SEC Chair Gary Gensler: "Investment advisers should not mislead the public by saying they are using an AI model when they are not. Such AI washing hurts investors." First SEC AI-washing enforcement actions. Source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2024-36
- **Joint Investor Alert "Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Investment Fraud," January 25, 2024**, from the SEC Office of Investor Education and Advocacy, NASAA, and FINRA. Warns AI's popularity is used to lure victims; recommends verifying an adviser's registration before investing. Sources: https://www.finra.org/investors/insights/artificial-intelligence-and-investment-fraud and https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-alerts/artificial-intelligence-fraud
- NOTE: Global Predictions Inc. (the SEC AI-washing case) is the same company behind PortfolioPilot. If PortfolioPilot appears on a page alongside this SEC fact, state both plainly; do not imply the current product is fraudulent — the 2024 action concerned past marketing claims.
