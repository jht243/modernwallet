# Phase 7 — sitemap + IndexNow prep (Coast FI pass, 2026-09-09)

## Sitemap
This repo has **no checked-in sitemap file**. `@astrojs/sitemap` generates `dist/sitemap-index.xml`
+ `dist/sitemap-0.xml` at build, filtered through `src/data/noindex.ts`. All three new routes are
present in the generated output, verified after the Phase 6 build:

- https://www.themodernwallet.com/guides/coast-fire-number-by-age/
- https://www.themodernwallet.com/compare/coast-fire-vs-fire/
- https://www.themodernwallet.com/roundup/best-coast-fire-calculators/

**`<lastmod>` is not emitted by this sitemap at all** (0 occurrences in `dist/sitemap-0.xml`; the
integration is configured with only a `filter`). There is therefore no lastmod to add for the new
routes, none to refresh on the substantively-changed pages, and — the case Phase 7 warns about —
no way to wrongly stamp lastmod on a link-only page. Steps 1 and 2 are structurally satisfied.
Freshness on this site is signalled instead by the `updated` field, which drives the visible
byline and JSON-LD `dateModified`; that field was bumped per the workflow's date-bump rule and is
audited in Phases 2, 5 and 6.
