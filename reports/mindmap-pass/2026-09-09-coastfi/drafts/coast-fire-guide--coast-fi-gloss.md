## Coast FI Definition and Calculation Formula

Coast FI and Coast FIRE are two names for the same milestone, built from financial independence (FI) and Financial Independence, Retire Early (FIRE). Coast FI is the older, shorter term used inside the community, while Coast FIRE is the phrasing most calculators use, including our [Coast FIRE calculator](/coast-fire/). Neither name means anything different from the other.

The formula is usually quoted as Coast FI = retirement savings goal / (1 + r)^t. Here, r is the expected annual rate of return on investments, and t is the number of years until retirement, calculated as retirement age minus current age. That calculation discounts the retirement number back to today at the expected return over the years remaining.

Consider a 35-year-old who needs $1,500,000 to retire at 65 on $60,000 a year. At a 7% return over 30 years, dividing $1,500,000 by 7.6123 leaves a Coast FI target of $197,051 today.
