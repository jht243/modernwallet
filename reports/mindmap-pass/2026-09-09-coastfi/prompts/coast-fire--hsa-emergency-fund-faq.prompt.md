## THE CLOSED FACT LIST
Anything not on this list, you do not know. Never invent a price, contribution limit, tax rate,
statistic, or URL.
- The "Current retirement savings" field on this calculator projects LIQUID, INVESTED assets
  compounding at a market return. That is the test for what belongs in it. The page already
  states this test in its real-estate FAQ.
- An HSA belongs in the field only to the extent it is actually INVESTED rather than sitting in
  the account's cash sweep, and only if the reader intends to leave it untouched to grow for
  retirement rather than spending it on medical costs along the way. An HSA balance that is
  being spent down on current medical bills is not compounding toward retirement and does not
  belong in the field.
- An emergency fund does NOT belong in the field. It is deliberately held in cash so it can be
  spent at short notice, it is not invested at a market return, and counting it would make the
  reader look closer to coasting than they are while also implying they would liquidate the
  cushion.
- Money you plan to spend before retirement does not belong in the field, whatever account it
  sits in.
- DO NOT state an HSA contribution limit, an HSA tax rule, a triple-tax-advantage claim, an
  age threshold, a cash-sweep interest rate, or how many months an emergency fund should cover.
  You have none of them, and this is not the page for them.
- DO NOT mention Social Security, a pension, rental income, real estate, a mortgage, or which
  account type (Roth, traditional, taxable) the balance sits in. Four separate FAQs on this
  same page already answer every one of those, and repeating them here is a defect.
