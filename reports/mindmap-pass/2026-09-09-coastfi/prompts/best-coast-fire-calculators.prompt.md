### WalletBurst Coast FIRE Calculator — https://walletburst.com/tools/coast-fire-calc/
- Inputs: current age, planned retirement age, annual retirement spending, current invested assets,
  investment rate of return (7% default), inflation rate (3% default), safe withdrawal rate (4% default).
- Output: a graph with two lines. A green line for the growth of current net worth over time with
  the monthly contribution, and a blue line for the amount needed at each age to reach the Coast
  FIRE milestone.
- Cost: free. No signup mentioned as required.
- The site separately sells a "Wealth Planning Toolkit for Google Sheets" for $20. That is a
  separate product, NOT a charge for the calculator. Say so if you mention it.
- The page does not state whether it saves user data. Do not claim either way.

### coastfirecalc.com — https://coastfirecalc.com/
- Inputs: current age, retirement age, annual spending, current invested assets, monthly
  contributions, investment growth rate, inflation rate, safe withdrawal rate, AND investment fees.
- Outputs: a red/green verdict on whether you have reached Coast FIRE, projected retirement
  savings, expected annual retirement income, and a visual graph of your Coast FIRE path.
- Cost: described on the page as a free calculator. No signup requirement mentioned. No pricing
  information appears anywhere on the page.
- It is the only tool on this list with an explicit investment-fees input.

### firenum Coast FIRE calculator — https://firenum.com/coast-fire
- Positioned as finding your Coast FIRE number by age.
- Supports 9 currencies: USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CAD, AUD, CHF, INR, NZD, switched from a selector in
  the navigation bar.
- Instant charts, no signup required.
- Defaults: a 5% REAL return, a 4% withdrawal rate, retirement at 65. Note this is a real
  (inflation-adjusted) default, unlike the 7% nominal defaults elsewhere.
- Published examples on that page: a 25-year-old needs $170,455 to cover $4,000 a month of
  retirement expenses; a 50-year-old needs $577,221 for the same lifestyle, roughly 3.4 times as much.
- The site states of its wider FIRE planner: "No account, no email, no sign-up. Every calculator,
  the Fire Planner, all simulations, and the guides are fully usable with nothing locked away or
  held back behind a paid tier."
