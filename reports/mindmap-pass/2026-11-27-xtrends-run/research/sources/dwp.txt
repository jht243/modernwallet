 Temporary disruption to some DWP online services
 Some DWP online services may be temporarily unavailable.
 From:
 Department for Work and Pensions
 Published:
 27 November 2026
 Last updated:
 27 November 2026
 —
 See all updates
 We have taken some DWP online services offline as a precaution after we identified a cyber incident affecting our systems.  
We are working closely with the National Cyber Security Centre and Government Cyber Coordination Centre to investigate and to bring services back online as quickly as possible and to minimise any disruption. 
Regular benefit and pension payments are not affected and will be paid as normal. If customers need help, they should call their usual benefit line, and DWP staff will support them. All benefit helpline numbers can be found on GOV.UK.
As always, we will never ask for your personal information via text or email.  Find out how to avoid and report internet scams and phishing.
