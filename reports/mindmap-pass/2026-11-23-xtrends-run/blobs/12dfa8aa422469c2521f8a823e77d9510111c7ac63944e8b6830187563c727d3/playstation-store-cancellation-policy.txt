Games/add-ons: cancel for refund within 14 days of purchase if download/stream not started; once started, not refundable unless faulty. Pre-orders: if main part not started (incl. pre-load): paid >14 days before release -> cancel any time before release; paid <=14 days before release -> cancel up to 14 days after payment. Faulty digital content refundable regardless of download status. Region: US page only; EU/UK rules not fetched.
