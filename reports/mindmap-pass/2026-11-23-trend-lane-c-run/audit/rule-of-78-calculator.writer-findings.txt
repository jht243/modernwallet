FINDING 1 [faqs[1].answer] inaccurate formula statement
QUOTE: "The formula divides the digits of the remaining months by the sum of all digits in the loan term."
DEFECT: Misdescribes the method. The fact list says month j of an n-month loan is credited (n - j + 1) divided by S of the finance charge, where S = n(n + 1) / 2. There are no digits of the remaining months; the numerator is the number of months counted from month j to the last month.
REPLACEMENT: "The formula credits each month with a share of the finance charge equal to the number of months from that month to the end of the loan, divided by the sum of all the month numbers in the term."

FINDING 2 [introText paragraph 1] consequential claim missing full statutory scope (CR-2)
QUOTE: "Federal law under [15 U.S.C. 1615](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title15/html/USCODE-2024-title15-chap41-subchapI-partA-sec1615.htm) requires a refund at least as favorable as the actuarial method on precomputed consumer credit transactions longer than 61 months."
DEFECT: The first answer omits that subsection (b) covers transactions consummated after September 30, 1993, and it does not say the rule is not a blanket ban. The fact list requires the full scope in the first answer.
REPLACEMENT: "Federal law under [15 U.S.C. 1615](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title15/html/USCODE-2024-title15-chap41-subchapI-partA-sec1615.htm) requires a refund at least as favorable as the actuarial method only for precomputed consumer credit transactions longer than 61 months that were made after September 30, 1993."
