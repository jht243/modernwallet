DEFECT: Contradicts the page's own text and can steer a reader away from applying. Not liability: no dosing, safety or fabricated testing, so no liabilityBlock.

'beneath the $2,000 individual ceiling' -> 'beneath the limit in 20 CFR 416.1205 ($2,000 for an individual, $3,000 for an individual and spouse)'.
