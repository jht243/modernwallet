QuickBooks Online costs less for one user with six or more billable clients and for exactly three users with 51 or more clients. FreshBooks costs less for one user with up to five clients, two users at any client count, three users with up to 50 clients, and four to 25 users at any client count. In our internal cost modeling, we compared monthly list prices across 49 user-and-client combinations using the QuickBooks pricing page and FreshBooks pricing page as inputs. Prices were checked November 16, 2026, and promotions are excluded. QuickBooks also has a $0 Free plan for one user sending up to two invoices a month. What the Price Comparison Leaves Out The grid compares published monthly list prices. It does not measure what each product can do for a particular business. QuickBooks inputs are limited to plan prices and user caps, plus the Free plan's invoice limit. No QuickBooks feature, support or accountant-access claim is used to choose a winner. FreshBooks describes plans built around the number of clients you invoice. Lite covers invoices to five clients, expense tracking and estimates. Plus raises the invoice limit to 50 clients and lists proposals, client retainers, financial and accounting reports, receipt scanning and accountant access. Premium allows unlimited clients and lists project profitability. Those published details can help you identify a requirement, but the cost grid gives none of them a score. The FreshBooks base prices in the grid also exclude optional add-ons other than modeled Team Members. FreshBooks lists Advanced Payments at $20 a month and Payroll at $40 a month plus $6 a month per user. Neither charge appears in a grid cell. Select has no public monthly price and is excluded as well, so the table cannot decide whether a Select quote would cost less for a given team. Promotions are excluded from every modeled figure. If you intend to buy at an advertised discount, compare the amounts and terms shown on the QuickBooks pricing page and FreshBooks pricing page with the list-price result. A discount can change the price comparison for the period it applies. The grid does not assign a discounted price to either product. Features, support and integrations can also change which product you would choose, even when the list-price arithmetic stays the same. Identify any requirement that could outweigh the monthly difference before using a $5 gap, such as the one-user, six-client case, to make the decision. Who Should Pick QuickBooks or FreshBooks QuickBooks Online has the lower modeled paid price for a single user invoicing six or more clients. At six to 50 clients, Simple Start is $38 against FreshBooks Plus at $43. At 51 or more clients, Simple Start remains $38 while FreshBooks Premium is $70. A single user sending up to two invoices a month also has the separate $0 QuickBooks Free option. QuickBooks Essentials has one other lower-price case: exactly three users with 51 or more clients. Its $85 monthly list price is $7 below FreshBooks Premium with two added Team Members at $92. At 50 clients, FreshBooks Plus with those seats is $65, so count billable clients before applying the three-user result. FreshBooks has the lower modeled paid price for a one-user business invoicing up to five clients. It also costs less for every modeled client count with two users and with four to 25 users. The margin varies. With two users and 51 or more clients, FreshBooks is $81 against QuickBooks at $85. With four users and that client count, FreshBooks is $103 against QuickBooks at $140.
