## A1) Automatic enrollment and claiming
- Treasury press release SB-0642 "Treasury Announces the Completion of Automatic Enrollment Today for Trump Accounts" is dated October 1, 2026. [home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0642]
- Treasury Secretary Bessent: over 60 million more eligible children now have an account ready to be claimed (on top of families who had already signed up). [sb0642]
- Who was auto-enrolled: every eligible child under age 18 with a valid Social Security number. [sb0642]
- A parent or guardian must claim the account before managing it. Family, friends and employers cannot contribute until it is claimed. [sb0642]
- Claim steps: download the official Trump Accounts app (iOS or Android), verify your identity and your relationship to the child, review the child's information, accept the account terms. [sb0642]
- A child's account must be claimed to receive the one-time $1,000 Treasury seed (for eligible children). [sb0642]
- Unclaimed accounts are held in a Treasury master group trust; they can receive only general contributions and the $1,000 pilot contribution; family and employer contributions are blocked until claimed. Temporary regulations TD 10056, effective September 29, 2026. [tax-practitioner analysis, currentfederaltaxdevelopments.com, Oct 1 2026 — present as "according to a tax-practitioner analysis of the regulations"]
- No claim deadline has been published (do not state one). Per the same practitioner analysis, claiming is available until the end of the year the child turns 17; if two people try to claim the same child, the first to activate becomes the responsible party.
- Totals differ by source: Treasury says over 60 million more children; CNBC (Oct 8) says nearly 70 million accounts created; Invest America says more than 70 million children have accounts. Present as "about 60 to 70 million, depending on the source".
- The older manual route, IRS Form 4547, requires an ID.me sign-in plus the child's SSN, date of birth and address. [irs.gov/trumpaccounts]
