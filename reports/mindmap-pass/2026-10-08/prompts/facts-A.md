## A1) Automatic enrollment and claiming
- Treasury press release SB-0642 "Treasury Announces the Completion of Automatic Enrollment Today for Trump Accounts" is dated October 1, 2026. [home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0642]
- Treasury Secretary Bessent: over 60 million more eligible children now have an account ready to be claimed (on top of families who had already signed up). [sb0642]
- Who was auto-enrolled: every eligible child under age 18 with a valid Social Security number. [sb0642]
- A parent or guardian must claim the account before managing it. Family, friends and employers cannot contribute until it is claimed. [sb0642]
- Claim steps: download the official Trump Accounts app (iOS or Android), verify your identity and your relationship to the child, review the child's information, accept the account terms. [sb0642]
- A child's account must be claimed to receive the one-time $1,000 Treasury seed (for eligible children). [sb0642]
- Unclaimed accounts are held in a Treasury master group trust; they can receive only general contributions and the $1,000 pilot contribution; family and employer contributions are blocked until claimed. Temporary regulations TD 10056, effective September 29, 2026. [tax-practitioner analysis, currentfederaltaxdevelopments.com, Oct 1 2026 — present as "according to a tax-practitioner analysis of the regulations"]
- No claim deadline has been published (do not state one). Per the same practitioner analysis, claiming is available until the end of the year the child turns 17; if two people try to claim the same child, the first to activate becomes the responsible party.
- Totals differ by source: Treasury says over 60 million more children; CNBC (Oct 8) says nearly 70 million accounts created; Invest America says more than 70 million children have accounts. Present as "about 60 to 70 million, depending on the source".
- The older manual route, IRS Form 4547, requires an ID.me sign-in plus the child's SSN, date of birth and address. [irs.gov/trumpaccounts]
- UNKNOWN (do not state): which identity provider the app uses for claiming; exact in-app status labels.

## A2) The app
- App Store name: "Trump Accounts: Official App". Seller: U.S. Department of the Treasury. Robinhood Securities is the trustee on Treasury's behalf (the app is built by Robinhood). [apps.apple.com/us/app/trump-accounts/id6767364919]
- Treasury announced the app on May 28, 2026 (press release SB-0508), on the Apple App Store and Google Play. [home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0508]
- A web version exists for people without a mobile device at trumpaccount.com; account management is also referenced at TrumpAccounts.gov. [sb0508]
- Official call center: 1-866-USA-4547. In-app and online callback requests are offered. Official emails come only from no-reply@TrumpAccounts.Treasury.gov. [sb0508]
- In the app you can: activate (claim) the account; track contributions, activity and performance; receive up to $5,000 a year combined from family, friends and employers. Money is automatically invested in a qualifying index fund. [App Store listing]
- A later app update added recurring contributions, bank linking and personalized guidance. [ICBA, Jul 8 2026]
- Problems reported in user reviews (anecdotal, not official): bank-link errors, linked debit cards disappearing, recurring contributions not showing, failed activation for some children, requests to resubmit information. Present as "issues users have reported in reviews".
- UNKNOWN: Google Play publisher name; app rating (do not state).

## A3) Dell $250
- Michael and Susan Dell pledged $6.25 billion to deposit $250 into the Trump Accounts of 25 million children (announced December 2025). [whitehouse.gov Dec 2025]
- Eligible: children born 2016 through 2024 (25 million children). [investamerica.org/check-dell-250-eligibility/]
- Final threshold: ZIP codes with a median family income of approximately $118,000 or less, using Census 2020–2024 American Community Survey 5-year estimates of median FAMILY income (not household income). [Invest America]
- $150,000 was the ORIGINAL figure in the December 2025 announcement; the cutoff fell to about $118,000 because auto-enrollment greatly increased the number of eligible children while the gift stayed fixed at 25 million x $250. Many older articles still say $150,000. [Invest America; White House Dec 2025]
- Not first-come: deposits began in the lowest-income ZIP codes and expanded until 25 million children were reached. [Invest America]
- No separate application. Families who claimed or filed Form 4547 before October 1 need no additional action. [Invest America]
- To check a ZIP, Invest America points to the Census Reporter table B19113 (median family income). There is no official checker tool (do not invent one). [Invest America]
- Timing: all Dell deposits were expected to be made by the end of the day Friday, October 9, 2026; families should check early the following week. [Invest America]
- Children on military bases are included. Money for foster children is reserved, with contributions expected next year. [Invest America]
- The Dell $250 is separate from the $1,000 Treasury seed: the $1,000 is for children born 2025–2028; the Dell $250 is for children born 2016–2024. A child gets one or the other based on birth year.
- Some older coverage describes eligibility as "age 10 and under"; the Invest America page states born 2016–2024.

## A4) Funding timing ($1,000 seed)
- $1,000 seed eligibility: U.S.-citizen children with a valid SSN born January 1, 2025 through December 31, 2028. [irs.gov/trumpaccounts]
- Seed deposits could begin as early as July 4, 2026. [sb0508]
- IRS CEO Frank Bisignano told CNBC (October 2, 2026) up to 25 million Trump Accounts could be funded by mid-October; CNBC noted it was unclear whether that counted the $1,000 seed, the Dell $250, or family and employer money. [cnbc.com, Oct 2 2026]
- About 72 companies have pledged employer contributions (per CNBC Oct 2 2026).
- In July 2026 Treasury called the gap between opening and receiving the seed "standard processing time, like receiving a tax refund"; most families got the money in 1 to 2 days, though some were told it could take up to 4 weeks. [NBC/AP via local10.com, Jul 22 2026]
- If a deposit is missing, the official channels are the 1-866-USA-4547 call center or an in-app callback request. [sb0508]
- Must be claimed first: an unclaimed account cannot be managed; the seed requires a claimed account. [sb0642]

## A0) Program basics (already on the site)
- Contributions up to $5,000 a year combined from family and friends; employers can add up to $2,500, which counts inside the $5,000. The $1,000 seed does not count against the cap.
- Money is invested in a low-cost fund tracking the S&P 500 or a similar U.S. stock index; growth is tax-deferred; withdrawals generally blocked until January 1 of the year the child turns 18, then taxed like a traditional IRA.
