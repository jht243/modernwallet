One was most likely opened already: on October 1, 2026 Treasury finished automatically enrolling every eligible child under 18 with a Social Security number, so most parents now claim the existing account in the official app instead of opening a new one. The [Department of the Treasury](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0642) created roughly 60 to 70 million of these accounts nationwide. A parent or guardian must finish claiming before family or employers can deposit funds.
