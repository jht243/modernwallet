# CLOSED FACT LIST — Is Robinhood safe?
- Robinhood Financial LLC and Robinhood Securities, LLC are SIPC members. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/how-youre-protected/]
- FINRA BrokerCheck: Robinhood Financial, LLC (CRD# 165998) is registered with the SEC and 1 self-regulatory organization. It was formed in Delaware on 08/24/2012. Its report lists 56 regulatory events and 12 arbitrations. [source: https://files.brokercheck.finra.org/firm/firm_165998.pdf]
- FINRA BrokerCheck: Robinhood Securities, LLC (CRD# 287900, SEC# 8-69916) is registered with the SEC, 1 self-regulatory organization, and 53 U.S. states and territories. It is "Regulated by FINRA Florida Office" and is not currently suspended with any regulator. [source: https://files.brokercheck.finra.org/firm/firm_287900.pdf]
- SIPC protection is limited to $500,000, which includes a $250,000 limit for cash. [source: https://www.sipc.org/for-investors/what-sipc-protects]
- SIPC "does not protect against the decline in value of your securities." [source: https://www.sipc.org/for-investors/what-sipc-protects]
- SIPC protects the custody function: when a brokerage firm is liquidated, SIPC works to restore customers' securities and cash that were in their accounts when the liquidation began. [source: https://www.sipc.org/for-investors/what-sipc-protects]
- SIPC: "Digital asset securities that are unregistered investment contracts do not qualify as 'securities' under SIPA." [source: https://www.sipc.org/for-investors/what-sipc-protects]
- Robinhood has an excess-of-SIPC policy underwritten by Lloyd's of London that applies once SIPC limits are exhausted. It covers up to an aggregate of $1 billion, limited to a combined return per customer of $50 million, including up to $1.9 million in uninvested cash. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/how-youre-protected/]
- Neither SIPC nor the excess insurance covers losses in market value. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/how-youre-protected/]
- Crypto held through Robinhood Crypto is not SIPC- or FDIC-protected. Futures through Robinhood Derivatives are not SIPC-protected. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/how-youre-protected/]
- Cash sweep: eligible swept cash qualifies for FDIC insurance up to $2.5 million for individual accounts and up to $5 million for joint accounts (as of January 15, 2025). This limit includes deposits you already hold at the same banks in the same ownership capacity. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/deposit-sweep-program/]
- The sweep program uses 15 FDIC-insured program banks as of July 1, 2026, including Goldman Sachs Bank USA, Wells Fargo, Citibank, U.S. Bank, and Truist Bank. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/deposit-sweep-program/]
- "Once funds are swept to a program bank, they are no longer held in your investing account and are not protected by SIPC." [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/deposit-sweep-program/]
- Robinhood announced two-factor authentication support on September 7, 2016. It was recommended, not mandatory. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/two-factor-authentication/]
- Device approvals: when you log in on a new device or update account information, Robinhood sends a notification to your mobile devices that you can approve or deny. A "trusted device" is a device you've used before to sign in. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/trusted-devices/]
- Robinhood may ask for extra verification at login or when you change your account, including device approval, SMS one-time code, bank verification, a three-point selfie, or a government ID. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/verifying-its-you/]
- Passkeys (Apple) let iOS 16+ users sign in without a password using Face ID or Touch ID. Robinhood says they are more resistant to phishing. They are iOS-only. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/passkeys/]
- Robinhood's security guarantee says "If eligible, we'll reimburse direct losses due to unauthorized activity." It excludes cases where the customer's own actions facilitated the unauthorized activity. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/security-guarantee/]
- Robinhood says it uses BCrypt password hashing, TLS encryption, and encrypted storage of sensitive data. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/how-youre-protected/]
- Data breach: late on November 3, 2021, an unauthorized party socially engineered a customer support employee by phone. The party obtained email addresses for about 5 million people and full names for a different group of about 2 million people. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/robinhood-announces-data-security-incident-update/]
- In the same incident, about 310 people had name, date of birth, and zip code exposed, and about 10 had more extensive account details revealed. Robinhood said no Social Security, bank account, or debit card numbers were exposed and there was no financial loss to customers. The attacker demanded an extortion payment. Robinhood notified law enforcement and engaged Mandiant. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/robinhood-announces-data-security-incident-update/]
- 2020 outages: per FINRA, Robinhood's website and mobile apps shut down on March 2–3, 2020, and a second significant outage followed on March 9, 2020. [source: https://www.finra.org/sites/default/files/2021-06/robinhood-financial-awc-063021.pdf]
- Beginning January 28, 2021, Robinhood Securities temporarily restricted or limited customers' purchases of certain securities, including GameStop Corp. and AMC Entertainment Holdings, Inc. Robinhood attributes this to increased deposit requirements imposed by the NSCC. [source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1783879/000178387925000145/hood-20250331.htm]
- Robinhood said on January 29, 2021 that its clearinghouse-mandated equities deposit requirements had "increased ten-fold" that week. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/what-happened-this-week/]
- FINRA (June 2021 AWC): Robinhood Financial was fined $57 million and ordered to pay restitution of $12,598,445.16 plus interest. The findings covered systems outages, false or misleading information, and options approvals. [source: https://www.finra.org/sites/default/files/2021-06/robinhood-financial-awc-063021.pdf]
- SEC, December 17, 2020: Robinhood Financial agreed to pay $65 million to settle charges that it misled customers about payment for order flow (2015 to late 2018) and failed to seek best execution. The SEC found inferior prices cost customers $34.1 million in aggregate, even after commission savings. [source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2020-321]
- SEC, January 13, 2025: Robinhood Securities ($33.5 million) and Robinhood Financial ($11.5 million) agreed to pay $45 million combined. The charges included Regulation SHO, Regulation S-P Rule 30(a), Regulation S-ID Rule 201, and recordkeeping/reporting violations. [source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2025-5]
- FINRA, March 7, 2025: FINRA fined Robinhood Financial and Robinhood Securities $26 million and ordered $3.75 million in restitution ($29.75 million total). The issues included market-order "collaring" disclosures, AML program failures, customer identification, clearing-system supervision in January 2021, and influencer communications. [source: https://www.finra.org/media-center/newsreleases/2025/finra-orders-robinhood-financial-pay-375-million-restitution]
- Robinhood Markets, Inc. Class A common stock trades under the symbol HOOD on The Nasdaq Stock Market LLC. [source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1783879/000178387925000145/hood-20250331.htm]
- Robinhood Agents were announced at HOOD Summit (newsroom dated September 29, 2026). Robinhood's disclosure: "You assume all risk for trades executed by AI agents and for any use of your data by third-party LLM providers." [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/hood-summit-2026/]
- Robinhood: "Robinhood does not control, supervise, monitor, recommend, or audit agents." Trade approvals are on by default. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/robinhood-agents-overview/]
- Agents can trade only in the dedicated Robinhood Agentic account. Robinhood says "You are ultimately responsible for the trades your AI agent places in your account." [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/agentic-trading-overview/]
- CoinDesk (Sept 30, 2026) reports that more than 150,000 customers have opened agentic trading accounts since the May 2026 launch [secondary: CoinDesk]. [source: https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/09/30/robinhood-is-giving-customers-an-ai-agent-that-trades-for-them-plus-10x-crypto-bets]
