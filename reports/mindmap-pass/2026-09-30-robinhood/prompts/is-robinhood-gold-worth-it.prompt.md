# CLOSED FACT LIST — Robinhood Gold (is it worth it?)
- Robinhood Gold costs $5 per month or $50 per year. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/gold-overview]
- Robinhood's billing article states the individual investing account "will be charged the $5 monthly or $50 annual subscription at the beginning of each billing cycle." [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/paying-for-robinhood-gold]
- Gold offers a one-time free trial for the first 30 days; the Gold landing page says "First 30 Days are free." [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/gold-overview] [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/gold/]
- If the subscription charge can't be collected, the customer may face an account deficit, a margin call (if margin is enabled), or an automatic downgrade from Gold. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/paying-for-robinhood-gold]
- To cancel: Account (person icon) → Menu (3 bars) → Robinhood Gold → Cancel membership. [source: https://robinhood.com/support/articles/360026472551/downgrading-from-gold]
- After cancelling, Gold features (including the High-Yield Cash Program) stay active until the end of the current billing cycle; the High-Yield Cash Program is disabled at the end of that cycle. [source: https://robinhood.com/support/articles/360026472551/downgrading-from-gold]
- To avoid another charge, cancel before the next billing date. The billing date is shown under Account → Menu → Robinhood Gold. [source: https://robinhood.com/support/articles/360026472551/downgrading-from-gold]
- Robinhood says you can cancel Gold only after you close your Robinhood Gold Credit Card account. [source: https://robinhood.com/support/articles/360026472551/downgrading-from-gold]
- IRA match: 3% on annual contributions with Gold, 1% on eligible annual IRA contributions without Gold. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/ira-match-faq/]
- IRA transfers and old 401(k) rollovers earn a 1% match for both Gold and non-Gold customers. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/ira-match-faq/]
- To keep a match, the matched funds must stay in the IRA for at least 5 years. Earlier withdrawals can trigger an early removal fee if the remaining balance falls below the matched deposit plus the match. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/ira-match-faq/]
- To keep the full 3% Gold match, you must stay a Gold member for at least 1 year from the date of your first Gold match. Cancelling sooner triggers a "Robinhood Gold cancellation IRA match removal fee" equal to the extra 2%, so you keep only the 1% match. Robinhood may sell positions to cover the fee if there isn't enough uninvested cash. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/ira-match-faq/]
- 2026 contribution limits per Robinhood: $7,500 under age 50 (match up to $75 without Gold, $225 with Gold) and $8,600 at age 50+ (up to $86 without Gold, $258 with Gold). [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/ira-match-faq/]
- Contributions from accounts managed by Robinhood Strategies are not eligible for the IRA match. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/ira-match-faq/]
- Cash interest (VARIABLE): the High-Yield Cash Program APY is 3.6% for Robinhood Gold members as of Sept 17, 2026. Robinhood says "The APY your cash earns is subject to change." [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/cash-program-interest-rate/]
- The High-Yield Cash Program is for Robinhood Gold subscribers. Interest compounds daily, is paid monthly, and has no minimum or maximum balance. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/high-yield-cash-program/] [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/cash-program-interest-rate/]
- Margin: Gold subscribers get their first $1,000 of margin included (interest-free). Interest applies only to margin used above $1,000. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/margin-rates]
- Margin rates as of September 17, 2026 (VARIABLE; applied to the full balance by tier): up to $50,000 = 5.25%; $50,000–$100,000 = 5.05%; $100,000–$1M = 4.75%; $1M–$10M = 4.5%; $10M–$50M = 4.45%; $50M+ = 4.20%. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/margin-rates]
- Margin interest is calculated daily on settled margin balances. The Gold $1,000 margin benefit applies to 1 account with margin investing enabled. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/margin-rates] [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/multiple-investing-accounts-faq/]
- Gold includes Level II Market Data from Nasdaq and professional research from Morningstar on self-directed taxable individual and joint accounts. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/multiple-investing-accounts-faq/]
- Level II data is powered by Nasdaq TotalView. It shows multiple bid and ask prices with quantities, but only from Nasdaq participants, not other venues or Robinhood. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/level-ii-market-data/]
- Robinhood first added Nasdaq Level 2 data to Gold, alongside Morningstar research reports, in a newsroom post dated June 20, 2019. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/nasdaq-level-2-market-data-is-here/]
- Instant Deposits without Gold: up to $1,000, or 2× portfolio value (rounded down to the nearest thousand) if greater. With Gold: up to $5,000, or 3× portfolio value if greater. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/bigger-instant-deposits/]
- Gold-level Instant Deposits are not available for IRAs; IRAs are capped at up to $1,000. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/bigger-instant-deposits/]
- Robinhood Strategies charges a 0.25% annual management fee, with a yearly cap of $250 for Gold members (announced March 27, 2025). [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/introducing-strategies-banking-and-cortex/]
- The Gold page says "No management fees above $100K for Gold members". The Gold overview says Strategies accounts are only charged management fees on the first $100,000. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/gold/] [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/gold-overview]
- Cortex Digests are AI-generated plain-language summaries (Asset Digests and Portfolio Digests), available to Robinhood Gold members. Robinhood says they are not research reports, recommendations, or investment advice. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/cortex-digests/]
- Gold also lists discounted index options fees and lower futures commissions. The Gold page shows $0.50 per futures contract and $0.35 per index options contract. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/gold-overview] [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/gold/]
- The Robinhood Gold Card is available only to Robinhood Gold members and requires an annual Gold subscription ($50). [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/robinhood-gold-card-waitlist/]
