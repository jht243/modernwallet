# CLOSED FACT LIST — Robinhood 24 Hour Market and extended-hours trading (as of 2026-09-30)

## Session hours
- The Robinhood 24 Hour Market runs from Sunday 8 PM ET through Friday 8 PM ET. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/24hour-market/]
- A 24 Hour Market limit order can execute 12 a.m.–8 p.m. ET on a full trading day, 12 a.m.–5 p.m. ET on a half-day, and 8 p.m.–12 a.m. ET on the day before a full trading day or half-day. [source: https://cdn.robinhood.com/assets/robinhood/legal/ExtendedHoursTradingDisclosure.pdf]
- Regular market hours are 9:30 AM–4 PM ET. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/extendedhours-trading/]
- Robinhood's pre-market session is 7–9:30 AM ET and its after-hours session is 4–8 PM ET. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/extendedhours-trading/]
- Fractional-share extended-hours trading runs 7 AM–9:30 AM and 4 PM–7:30 PM ET; fractional orders are not executable between 7:30 p.m. and 8:00 p.m. ET. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/extendedhours-trading/]
- Robinhood expanded pre-market to 7 a.m. (from 9 a.m.) ET and after-hours to 8 p.m. ET in an announcement dated March 29, 2022. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/the-future-of-investing-is-24-7/]
- Crypto trades 24/7 on Robinhood Crypto (with exceptions such as scheduled maintenance); futures trade 23 hours a day, 5 days a week. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/investing-on-weekends/]

## Order types
- Only whole-share limit orders are allowed in the 24 Hour Market, on assets in Robinhood's 24 Hour Market list. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/24hour-market/]
- Fractional orders are not eligible for Robinhood 24 Hour Market trading. [source: https://cdn.robinhood.com/assets/robinhood/legal/ExtendedHoursTradingDisclosure.pdf]
- Time-in-force: Good-for-Day orders expire at 8 PM ET; Good-'til-Canceled orders last up to 90 calendar days. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/24hour-market/]
- Market orders are not supported in extended hours and are queued for the next regular open; stop orders won't execute during extended or overnight hours. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/extendedhours-trading/]
- When placing a limit order you choose the session it applies to (regular market, extended hours, or 24 Hour Market). [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/extendedhours-trading/]

## Eligible securities
- The 24 Hour Market covers a curated list of popular, top-traded stocks and ETFs; the support page gives no count. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/24hour-market/]
- Robinhood's Sep 29, 2026 newsroom post says the 24 Hour Market lets customers trade "hundreds of stocks and ETFs," a "curated list of stocks and ETFs across all account types." [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/hood-summit-2026/]
- Launch history: announced May 10, 2023 with 43 stocks and ETFs; 95 by September 2023; rolled out to all users by June 2023. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/the-robinhood-24-hour-market-is-here/]
- As of February 2024, 922 symbols were tradable; by March 6, 2024 customers had traded more than $10B in total overnight volume, with up to 25% of daily volume outside traditional hours on peak days. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/robinhood-24-hour-market-reaches-10b-in-total-volume-traded-overnight/]

## Execution venue
- Between 8 p.m. and 4 a.m. ET ("Overnight Hours"), Robinhood Securities routes 24 Hour Market orders to "24H Market Makers," which may execute as principal or route to alternative trading systems (ATSs). [source: https://cdn.robinhood.com/assets/robinhood/legal/ExtendedHoursTradingDisclosure.pdf]
- The Sep 29, 2026 newsroom post names Bruce ATS as the alternative trading system powering the 24 Hour Market. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/hood-summit-2026/]
- Prices are bounded by ATS pricing bands based on the prior 8 PM ET closing price. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/24hour-market/]

## Risks Robinhood discloses
- Extended-hours risks listed: lower liquidity, higher volatility, changing prices (may not reflect regular-session prices), unlinked markets, news announcements, and wider spreads. [source: https://cdn.robinhood.com/assets/robinhood/legal/ExtendedHoursTradingDisclosure.pdf]
- Overnight ATSs "are not required to display prices publicly and may have very limited liquidity and/or high volatility"; 24 Hour Market orders "may not be price protected" and may fill worse than prices at other venues. [source: https://cdn.robinhood.com/assets/robinhood/legal/ExtendedHoursTradingDisclosure.pdf]
- Robinhood 24 Hour Market trading "may be suspended at any time without notice." [source: https://cdn.robinhood.com/assets/robinhood/legal/ExtendedHoursTradingDisclosure.pdf]
- Unfilled overnight orders may be canceled and re-routed at session boundaries and are not executable in the gap between cancellation and re-routing. [source: https://cdn.robinhood.com/assets/robinhood/legal/ExtendedHoursTradingDisclosure.pdf]

## Options hours
- Starting October 2026, Robinhood options trading runs 7:30am–4:15pm ET, which Robinhood calls "a 35% increase in trading hours." [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/hood-summit-2026/]
- Robinhood's disclosure notes Cboe Global Trading Hours for SPX, VIX and XSP index options run 8:15 PM–9:15 AM ET Mon–Fri, and Curb hours 4:15–5:00 PM ET. [source: https://cdn.robinhood.com/assets/robinhood/legal/ExtendedHoursTradingDisclosure.pdf]

## 24/7 / weekend stock trading status (2026)
- Weekend equity trading is "coming soon, pending regulatory review" per Robinhood's Sep 29, 2026 HOOD Summit post. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/hood-summit-2026/]

## Industry developments
- On Aug 7, 2026 the SEC granted 24X National Exchange temporary conditional exemptive relief (Release No. 34-106061) to permit overnight trading in the "24X Market Session" (9:00 p.m.–4:00 a.m. ET, Sunday–Thursday nights before a U.S. business day), effective January 24, 2027 until the earlier of implementation of the Extended Hours Amendments or July 2, 2027. [source: https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/exorders/2026/34-106061.pdf]
- As reported July 16, 2025: 24X planned a phase-1 launch Sep 29, 2025 (4am–8pm ET weekdays), with 23/5 trading later; Nasdaq targeted 24-hour trading in the second half of 2026; NYSE Arca planned 22-hour weekday trading (1:30am–11:30pm ET), pending approval. [secondary: Markets Media] [source: https://www.marketsmedia.com/24-hour-u-s-equities-trading-advances]
