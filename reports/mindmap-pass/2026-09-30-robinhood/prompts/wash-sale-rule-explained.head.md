answer: Definition + worked example. The rule disallows a loss when you buy the same or substantially identical security within 30 days before or after the sale (so a window spanning 30 days on each side of the sale date); the disallowed loss is added to the replacement shares' basis and their holding period includes the old one, so the loss is deferred, not destroyed (except when the replacement is bought in an IRA).
