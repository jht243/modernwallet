# CLOSED FACT LIST — Robinhood vs Fidelity

Compiled 2026-09-30. Every fact below was read from the URL cited, fetched on 2026-09-30. Rates are variable and carry the as-of date the source printed.

## Robinhood

### Commissions and trading fees
- Investing through a Robinhood investing account is commission-free for stocks, ETFs, and their options. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/trading-fees-on-robinhood]
- Standard stock and ETF options have no Robinhood base contract fee. Robinhood collects a combined $0.04 regulatory fee per options contract (effective January 10, 2025), plus a Consolidated Audit Trail fee of $0.0003 per options contract. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/trading-fees-on-robinhood]
- Index options carry a contract fee of $0.35 per contract for Robinhood Gold customers and $0.50 per contract without Gold. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/trading-fees-on-robinhood]
- Robinhood charges a $100 fee for each partial or full ACATS transfer out. The fee comes out of the Robinhood account's cash, or from the receiving brokerage account if there isn't enough cash. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/transfer-your-assets-out]

### Account minimums and account types
- You don't need to deposit any money to open a Robinhood brokerage account. You fund the account when you're ready to make your first purchase. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/learn/articles/3fIjIU3bey2l8fwYQdYHBI/what-is-a-brokerage-account/]
- Investing on margin requires at least $2,000 or 100% of the security's purchase price, whichever is less (a FINRA requirement). [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/minimum-margin/]
- Account types listed: individual investing account, joint investing account (two co-owners), Traditional IRA, Roth IRA, managed accounts through Robinhood Strategies, revocable trust account, and custodial account. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/open-an-account/]
- The account-opening page does not list HSA, 529, or 401(k) accounts. It mentions a 401(k) only as an outside workplace plan that does not stop you from opening an IRA at Robinhood. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/open-an-account/]
- Custodial accounts started rolling out on March 4, 2026. Trust accounts (for revocable living trusts, supporting equities, options, and uninvested cash) were announced to roll out later in 2026. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/robinhood-unveils-the-future-of-family-finance-at-robinhood-presents-take-flight]

### IRA match
- The IRA match is 3% on annual contributions with a Robinhood Gold subscription ($5 per month), or 1% without Gold. IRA transfers and old 401(k) rollovers get a 1% match on any amount. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/ira-overview/]
- To avoid a possible withdrawal fee, the match has to stay in the Robinhood IRA for at least 5 years. To keep the full Gold match, you must stay subscribed to Gold for 1 year after your first Gold match. Accounts managed by Robinhood Strategies can't get the match on contributions. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/ira-overview/]
- Robinhood's Gold page says the 3% match is worth up to $225, based on the 2026 maximum contribution. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/gold/]

### Fractional shares and what you can buy
- Fractional orders must be at least $1. Eligible stocks trade above $1 per share, have a market cap over $25,000,000, and are listed on a national exchange. OTC stocks are not eligible. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/fractional-shares]
- Robinhood Financial supports US exchange-listed stocks, ETFs, and CEFs, options on those, ADRs, and certain warrants and OTC equities, covering "over 11,200 securities." [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/investments-you-can-make-on-robinhood/]
- Robinhood Financial does not support mutual funds or bond and fixed-income trading. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/investments-you-can-make-on-robinhood/]

### Cash interest
- The High-Yield Cash Program pays a 3.6% APY to Robinhood Gold members as of Sept 17, 2026. The rate is variable. [source: https://www.robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/cash-sweep-program-interest-rate]
- Robinhood Gold costs $5/month, with the first 30 days free. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/gold/]

### Trading hours
- Robinhood 24 Hour Market runs from Sunday 8 PM ET through Friday 8 PM ET for select stocks and ETFs. It accepts only whole-share limit orders, so market orders and fractional shares are not available in that session. Overnight orders execute through Alternative Trading Systems. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/24hour-market]
- At HOOD Summit (September 29, 2026), Robinhood said 24/7 weekend equities trading is "coming early next year, pending regulatory approval." It also announced extended options hours of 7:30am–4:15pm starting in October. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/hood-summit-2026/]

### Crypto and futures
- Robinhood Crypto lists coins including BTC, ETH, DOGE, SHIB, AVAX, LTC, UNI, ETC, LINK, XLM, and AAVE. Recurring buys and staking start at $1. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/crypto/]
- Futures are offered through a separate entity, Robinhood Derivatives, LLC, which is a CFTC-registered futures commission merchant and NFA member. The commission is $0.75 per contract, or $0.50 for Gold customers. On top of that there is a $0.02 NFA fee and exchange fees of $0.20–$7.50 per contract. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/before-trading-a-futures-contract]

### Margin
- Margin rates as of September 17, 2026 (variable): up to $50,000 is 5.25%; $50,000 up to $100,000 is 5.05%; $100,000 up to $1 million is 4.75%; $1 million up to $10 million is 4.5%; $10 million up to $50 million is 4.45%; $50+ million is 4.20%. Gold includes the first $1,000 of margin. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/margin-rates]

### Research, tools, AI
- Robinhood Legend is a desktop trading and analysis platform that anyone with a Robinhood account can use at no additional cost. It offers real-time charts, technical indicators, and customizable multi-monitor layouts. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/legend/]
- Robinhood Cortex is an AI assistant for Robinhood Gold subscribers only. It covers portfolio questions, education, screeners, and drafting orders that the user reviews and submits. It "cannot give buy or sell recommendations." [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/cortex]
- Robinhood Strategies is a managed-portfolio service. It charges an annual management fee of 0.25% of assets under management. Gold members pay that fee on only $100,000 of assets. The minimum investment is $50, and it is available in individual, Traditional IRA, and Roth IRA accounts. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/about-strategies]
- Robinhood Agents was announced September 29, 2026 at HOOD Summit as "a fully embedded AI experience that can analyze the market, build strategies, and trade on your behalf around the clock." It is "coming soon" to eligible U.S. customers. Agents can only use the funds in a dedicated agentic trading account, and manual trade approval is on by default. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/hood-summit-2026/]
- Robinhood's earlier agentic trading beta launched May 27, 2026. It supported equities only and let customers connect their own third-party AI agents through a separate account. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/robinhood-is-now-open-to-agents/]

### Customer service
- Chat support is available 24/7. Phone support is by in-app callback, "typically in less than 30 minutes," from 7 AM to 9 PM ET, Monday through Friday, for investing features. Not every inquiry is eligible for a phone call. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/how-to-contact-support/]

### Protection
- Robinhood Financial LLC and Robinhood Securities, LLC are SIPC members. SIPC protects up to $500,000, including a $250,000 limit for cash, per account type. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/how-youre-protected]
- Robinhood also carries excess insurance underwritten by Lloyd's of London. It covers up to $1 billion in aggregate, with a per-customer limit of $50 million in securities, including $1.9 million in uninvested cash. Crypto (Robinhood Crypto) and futures (Robinhood Derivatives) are not SIPC-protected. [source: https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/how-youre-protected]

## Fidelity

### Commissions and trading fees
- Online US stock, ETF, and option trades have $0 commissions. [source: https://www.fidelity.com/trading/commissions-margin-rates]
- Online option trades carry a $0.65 per-contract fee. Professional Options Traders pay an additional $0.50 per contract. [source: https://www.fidelity.com/why-fidelity/faqs-pricing-fees]
- Buy-to-close option orders of $0.65 or less have no contract fee. [source: https://www.fidelity.com/trading/commissions-margin-rates]
- Fidelity has no account transfer-out fees and no IRA closeout fees. [source: https://www.fidelity.com/why-fidelity/pricing-fees]
- A $49.95 fee applies when certain mutual funds are redeemed after being held less than 60 days. [source: https://www.fidelity.com/trading/commissions-margin-rates]

### Account minimums and account types
- Retail brokerage accounts, including IRAs, have no account fees and no minimums to open. [source: https://www.fidelity.com/why-fidelity/pricing-fees]
- Account types listed include: The Fidelity Account (brokerage), Traditional, Roth, and Rollover IRA, Inherited IRAs, Fidelity HSA, 529 Account, Trust Account, Estate Account, Custodial Account, Fidelity Youth Account, Roth IRA for Kids, SEP IRA, SIMPLE IRA, Self-employed 401(k), ABLE account, and Fidelity Crypto IRAs. [source: https://www.fidelity.com/open-account/all-accounts]

### Fractional shares and mutual funds
- Stocks by the Slice lets you buy fractional shares of NMS exchange-listed stocks and ETFs for as little as $1.00, in quantities down to three decimal places (.001). [source: https://fidelity.com/stocksbytheslice]
- Fidelity has four ZERO index funds with a 0% expense ratio and no investment minimum: Fidelity ZERO Large Cap Index (FNILX), Extended Market Index (FZIPX), Total Market Index (FZROX), and International Index (FZILX). [source: https://www.fidelity.com/mutual-funds/investing-ideas/index-funds]
- Fidelity funds and "hundreds of other funds" trade with no transaction fee. [source: https://www.fidelity.com/why-fidelity/pricing-fees]

### Cash interest
- Uninvested cash in a new retail brokerage account goes into the Fidelity Government Money Market Fund (SPAXX) by default. Its 7-day yield was 3.46% as of September 24, 2026, and the yield is variable. Money market funds are not FDIC-insured. [source: https://fidelity.com/go/manage-cash-rising-costs]

### Trading hours
- Premarket orders can be placed from 7:00 a.m. to 9:28 a.m. ET, and after-hours orders from 4:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m. ET. The page describes no overnight session. [source: https://www.fidelity.com/trading/faqs-placing-orders]

### Crypto
- Fidelity Crypto supports Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Fidelity Digital Dollar (FIDD), Litecoin (LTC), and Solana (SOL). Buys and sells carry a 1% fee, trades can be as small as $1, and trading runs 23 hours a day, from 1 a.m. ET until midnight. [source: https://www.fidelity.com/crypto/help/trading/trading-basics]
- Fidelity Crypto accounts are custodied by Fidelity Digital Assets, N.A., a national trust bank. Crypto is also available inside Fidelity Crypto for IRAs, and through crypto ETPs FBTC, FETH, and FSOL in a brokerage account. [source: https://www.fidelity.com/crypto/overview]

### Margin
- Fidelity's margin base rate is 10.825%, effective September 18, 2026 (variable). The effective rate is 12.075% for balances of $0–$24,999 and 7.75% for balances of $1M+. [source: https://www.fidelity.com/trading/commissions-margin-rates]

### Robo-advisor
- Fidelity Go charges $0 in advisory fees under $25,000 and 0.35%/yr at $25,000 and above. [source: https://www.fidelity.com/why-fidelity/pricing-fees]
- Fidelity Go has no minimum to open, but the balance must be at least $10 to start investing. It supports taxable (individual/joint), Traditional, Roth, Rollover, and SEP IRA, and HSA accounts. Financial coaching requires $25,000 or more in an eligible account. [source: https://www.fidelity.com/managed-accounts/fidelity-go/overview]

### Research and service
- Fidelity's Equity Summary Score, from StarMine, combines ratings from independent research providers. The provider count "historically... has been between 10 and 12." [source: https://fidelity.com/quick-content/etf/help/research/learn_er_opinions.shtml]
- Fidelity lists phone support as "Call anytime: 800-343-3548." [source: https://www.fidelity.com/why-fidelity/overview]
- Fidelity has "215 Investor Centers." It was founded in 1946 and is headquartered in Boston. [source: https://about.fidelity.com/]

### Protection
- Fidelity Brokerage Services LLC is a member of NYSE and SIPC. SIPC covers up to $500,000, including a $250,000 limit for cash. [source: https://www.fidelity.com/why-fidelity/safeguarding-your-accounts]
- Fidelity's excess-of-SIPC coverage has a $1 billion aggregate limit and a $1.9 million per-customer limit on cash awaiting investment. It has no per-customer dollar limit on securities. [source: https://www.fidelity.com/why-fidelity/safeguarding-your-accounts]

## Contested / unverified
- Robinhood Agents' launch date: the brief says "launched 2026-09-30." Robinhood's newsroom dates the announcement September 29, 2026 and calls it "coming soon" to eligible customers. Write "announced Sept 29, 2026; rolling out," not "launched."
- Number of coins on Robinhood Crypto: a summary of the coin-availability page (https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/coin-availability/) came back with 168 tradable coins, but that was a tool-generated count, not a number Robinhood published. Don't use a count.
- Robinhood cash APY for non-Gold customers: the official page gives only the Gold rate. A 1.5% figure appeared only in search snippets and was not verified.
- Fidelity futures: no Fidelity primary page was found either confirming or denying direct futures trading. Secondary sources conflict or couldn't be fetched.
- Fidelity overnight / 24-hour trading: Fidelity's order FAQ lists only premarket and after-hours sessions. Whether Fidelity plans overnight trading is unconfirmed.
- Fidelity IRA match: no Fidelity page reviewed mentions an IRA contribution match. "Fidelity has no IRA match" is an inference, not a sourced fact. The safe wording is "Fidelity doesn't advertise an IRA match comparable to Robinhood's."
- Fidelity branch count: secondary snippets said "more than 190." The official about.fidelity.com page says 215, so use 215.
- Robinhood's Gold page lists "$0.35 per index options contract" for Gold, which matches the support page. Do not describe $0.35/$0.50 as a fee on standard equity options.

## DO NOT STATE
- Any Robinhood fee on standard stock or ETF options beyond the $0.04 regulatory and $0.0003 CAT pass-through fees.
- Any Robinhood crypto coin count.
- A non-Gold Robinhood cash APY.
- That Fidelity offers or does not offer futures, or overnight/24-hour stock trading.
- That Robinhood Agents "launched" on 2026-09-30 or is available to all customers now.
- That Robinhood offers HSA, 529, or 401(k) accounts, or mutual funds or individual bonds.
- Any Fidelity crypto fee other than the 1% trade fee, or any Robinhood crypto spread/fee number.
- Any interest rate without its as-of date, or presented as fixed.
- Fidelity Go fund expense ratios.
- Morningstar reports or Level II data as current Gold perks. The current Gold page doesn't list them.
