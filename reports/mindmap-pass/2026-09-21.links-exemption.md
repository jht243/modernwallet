# Run-level decision — first-mention link rule vs the closed URL list (mindmap-pass 2026-09-21)

**Raised by:** the Phase 4 adversarial auditor, as a run-level (Rung 2 policy) finding, not a per-page fail.

**The conflict.** `_content-standard.md` GATE — Links makes an unlinked first mention of a named organisation a hard fail. `_content-generation.md` requires every external link to come from the run's closed URL list, and `facts-core.md` explicitly forbids adding The Wall Street Journal to any page's `sources`. Seven entities are named across the eight new pages with no URL on `allowed-urls.txt`: HCA Healthcare, The Wall Street Journal, Armanino Advisory, the Tax Law Center at New York University, GRATtrack, Amazon.com, and the byline Ashlea Ebeling. The writer could not satisfy both rules.

**Decision: documented exemption — `links-exempt: closed URL list`.** The closed URL list stands; no company homepages are added. Rationale:

1. Every one of these names appears **inside a single WSJ-attributed narrative**, not as a claim this site is sourcing independently. The link rule exists so a reader can verify a claim we made about a company. Here the claim's source is named in prose ("as reported by The Wall Street Journal"), which is the verification path, and the underlying matter is a pending Tax Court case with no linkable primary document.
2. Linking WSJ would put a paywalled news URL in a `sources` array reserved on this site for primary sources (IRS, IRC/CFR). `facts-core.md` forbids it for exactly that reason.
3. `Amazon.com` appears only as the ticker-level subject of a third-party practitioner's historical estimate; `GRATtrack` and `Armanino Advisory` appear only as the affiliation of a quoted person. A homepage link on any of them adds no verification value and reads as an outbound-link tic.
4. The pages' actual substantive claims — the 2026 figures, the §7520 rate, the statutory mechanics — are all linked to irs.gov and law.cornell.edu as required.

**What this does NOT excuse.** Any future page that makes a claim ABOUT one of these companies (a price, a product, a rating) must link that company's official page and the URL must be added to that run's allowed list first.

**Carried into the Phase 8 summary** so the exemption is visible with the run, per the auditor's instruction.
