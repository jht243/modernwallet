Trump Account Trustees page. Existing FAQ: Can I move my Trump Account from Robinhood to Fidelity? You cannot move the account to Fidelity right now. Fidelity has announced that it plans to accept rollovers in the future, but it cannot do so until the Treasury Department issues official regulatory guidelines. Does Robinhood charge fees on Trump Accounts? Robinhood does not charge other account maintenance fees according to its September 30, 2026 customer agreement. It also charges no fee to transfer the account out.
