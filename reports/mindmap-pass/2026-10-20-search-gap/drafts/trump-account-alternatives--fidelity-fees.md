{"heading": "What a Trump Account Costs and Whether Fidelity Charges Anything", "body": "Fidelity cannot hold a Trump Account yet and has published no Trump Account fees. Every Trump Account is held at Robinhood Securities, LLC, the initial trustee the U.S. Treasury chose. As of October 2026, no other firm can hold one, according to the [Treasury announcement](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0642).\n\nFidelity said on June 23, 2026 that it plans to support Trump Account rollovers once Treasury publishes formal operating guidance. As of October 8, 2026, Treasury had not published that guidance, so Fidelity does not accept a Trump Account rollover. Vanguard, Schwab and J.P. Morgan Chase are in the same position, and any list that tells you to open a child's Trump Account at Fidelity or Schwab is incorrect.\n\nRobinhood's [Trump Account customer agreement](https://cdn.robinhood.com/assets/robinhood/legal/trump_account_customer_agreement.pdf), dated September 30, 2026, says Robinhood does not charge other account fees to maintain the balance. The same agreement says Robinhood charges no fee to transfer the account out.\n\nThe funds inside the account carry their own yearly cost. InvestmentNews reported on July 1, 2026 that expense ratios on the fund menu run about 0.02% to 0.03% a year, under the 0.10% legal cap, and that the default fund is SPYM. On a $10,000 balance, a 0.03% expense ratio comes to $3 a year, and the 0.10% cap would come to $10 a year.\n\nIf you plan to move a Trump Account to Fidelity once it accepts rollovers, ask Fidelity for its Trump Account fees in writing first, because none are published today. Compare that answer with Robinhood's free transfer-out and the 0.02% to 0.03% fund costs reported for the current menu. A child may hold only one funded Trump Account at a time, and any move must be a direct trustee-to-trustee transfer.\n\nDo not withdraw the money and deposit it at the new firm yourself. Our [Trump Account trustee comparison](/compare/trump-account-trustees/) lays out where Robinhood, Fidelity, Vanguard, Schwab and J.P. Morgan Chase each stand, so open it before you request any transfer."}
