## Release-gate integration (authority snapshots, compositions, record-time proof)

- **Authority, not wrapper.** A projection or composition is only a wrapper. Risk text, profile, reviewer and the recorded report digest are verified against the ORIGINAL independent snapshot/report that judged each page. Never verify against a re-saved copy: canonical saving sorts JSON keys and changes the passage order the reviewer hashed.
- **Separately recorded passes.** When different pages passed in different recorded reports (for example one page in a later round and its unchanged siblings earlier), join them for one publication snapshot with `content-review-policy.py compose --snapshot FINAL_SNAPSHOT --spec SPEC.json --out-review COMPOSITE_REVIEW.json`. `SPEC.json` lists `groups` (`snapshot`, `review`, `routes`) and, on Layer3, `bindings` (`policyCommit`, `workflowPolicySha256`, `writer` and `reviewer` bundle `manifest.json`). Each route must carry its exact recorded PASS or authorized final capped FAIL row, an identical fingerprint, semantic content, text hash and manifest entry in the final snapshot, one author and one independent reviewer, and its own latest eligible journal verdict. It writes no verdict, changes no recorded digest and fails closed on anything missing, forged or edited after review. `sources --review COMPOSITE_REVIEW.json` lists every file to retain, including the bound bundles.
- **Layer3 instruction proof.** A recorded PASS must be releasable. `record` therefore requires, BEFORE spending the round, either the existing current `workflowPolicySha256` + `instructionApplications`, or `releaseBindings` (`policyCommit`, `workflowPolicySha256`, `writer`, `reviewer`) that the immutable-delivery verifier accepts: the actual author and reviewer actors and roles, one invocation, the canonical job's handoff, the policy recomputed at the named commit, and every bundle source equal to that commit. `record` validates every page of a report before appending any, so a later failing page cannot leave a partly recorded report.
- **Earlier policy hashes.** Editing the policy tool changes its own hash. `compatiblePolicies` in `.claude/content-review-policy.json` lists exact earlier hashes whose already-admitted and recorded reviews stay valid; new reservations always need the current hash.
- **Admission.** `audit-admission.local.py` and `fresh-preview.local.py` refuse a candidate whose manifest lacks `workflowKind` or, for `mindmap-pass`, whose capture is not stamped with the active policy, before any reviewer is assigned.
