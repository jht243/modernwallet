Owner instruction, 2026-11-08. Applies across Mindmap Pass and vendor-producing callers. ## Resolve the task before opening vendor sites

Keep the COMPLETE original intake, canonical job and each original candidate ID. Identify the actual company/product and ORIGINAL target page/reader intent. Inspect current own-page coverage first. The same company on a different page, a footer link, another vendor sharing a hosting domain, an unrelated mention, or a prior cut never proves coverage. A new product/model/version, launch, news item, price update, consequential claim, contradictory evidence or explicit refresh is not covered merely because the company was previously listed.

For a genuine vendor-addition task, an exact current first-column vendor row on the original roundup can support an independently reviewed no-addition decision without reopening its homepage/pricing page.
