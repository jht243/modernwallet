# CLOSED FACT LIST
**Anything not on this list, you do not know.**
- A donor-advised fund (DAF) is a charitable account at a public-charity sponsor. The gift is irrevocable. The donor takes the deduction in the year of the gift and recommends grants to charities later.
- The deduction only lowers your tax if your itemized deductions exceed the standard deduction (2026 standard deduction for married filing jointly: $32,200).
- Cash to a public charity is generally deductible up to 60% of AGI; long-term appreciated stock up to 30% of AGI (IRS Publication 526, https://www.irs.gov/publications/p526). Excess carries over to later years.
- Most inherited stocks and property get a step-up in basis to fair market value on the date of death, so selling soon after inheriting may produce little or no capital gains tax. That means donating inherited shares avoids little gain; the main benefit is the deduction.
- Fidelity Charitable, as one example sponsor, has no minimum initial contribution; Vanguard Charitable requires $25,000 to open an account.
