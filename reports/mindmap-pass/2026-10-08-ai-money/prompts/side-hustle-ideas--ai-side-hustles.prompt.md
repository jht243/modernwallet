## 1. Earnings and demand data

### Upwork Future Workforce Index 2026 (Upwork Research Institute)
- Upwork released the report on July 14, 2026. It is the second annual edition. [P] https://www.upwork.com/press/releases/upworks-future-workforce-index-2026-how-ai-is-redefining-the-value-of-work-as-skilled-freelancing-accelerates (2026-07-14)
- Method: a survey of 2,400 U.S.-based skilled knowledge workers, run March–April 2026, with a margin of error of 2% at 95% confidence. Upwork combined it with its own Marketplace data, using an LLM-based pipeline to classify AI-related jobs. [P] same URL (2026-07-14)
- Freelancers who do AI work on the Upwork Marketplace earn 34% more per hour than those who don't use AI. Upwork itself adds that "not all AI work is becoming more valuable." [P] same URL (2026-07-14)
- Generative AI and creative production work: contract starts grew 90% year over year, but per-contract earnings fell 13%. Upwork reads this as a sign that lower-complexity AI execution may become less lucrative as it scales. [P] same URL (2026-07-14)
- Freelancers doing more complex work with AI saw earnings rise 45% year over year. [P] same URL (2026-07-14)
- AI-augmented professional services grew 72% in volume, with earnings up 22%. [P] same URL (2026-07-14)
- The report names an emerging role, the "AI Orchestrator": someone who connects AI tools to domain expertise, applies judgment, and turns AI output into business results. [P] same URL (2026-07-14)
- Skilled freelancers make up 38% of U.S. knowledge workers, up from 28% the year before. 58% of full-time employees say they are considering freelancing, up from 36%. [P] same URL (2026-07-14)
- Full report page: https://www.upwork.com/research/research-future-workforce-index-2026 (fetch blocked, not read)

- "Contributors are responsible for declaring their earnings and meeting tax obligations in their own country of residence." The blog also says "no employment relationship exists."

**Onboarding**
- The process is: CV, then an assessment, then platform access. The blog says joining and assessments should be free. Whether assessment time is paid is **not stated**. [https://mindrift.ai/] [https://mindrift.ai/blog/how-mindrift-pays]
