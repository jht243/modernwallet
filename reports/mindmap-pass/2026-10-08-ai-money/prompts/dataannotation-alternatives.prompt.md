## DataAnnotation (dataannotation.tech)

**Who runs it**
- The FAQ does not name a parent company. It names Persona as its identity-verification partner. [https://www.dataannotation.tech/faqs] (seen 2026-10-09)
- Wikipedia says that in 2023, The Verge and New York Magazine linked DataAnnotation to Surge AI (Surge Labs Inc.). **secondary — unverified**: we did not read the original articles, and DataAnnotation's site does not confirm it. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surge_AI] (seen 2026-10-09)

**What the work is**
- The FAQ lists the tasks: "evaluate chatbot responses for accuracy, compare AI outputs, flag factual errors, test AI-generated images, write challenging prompts, or review code for errors." [https://www.dataannotation.tech/faqs] (seen 2026-10-09)
- The FAQ says it "connects over 100,000 contractors globally." [https://www.dataannotation.tech/faqs] (seen 2026-10-09)

**Published pay (exact wording, Pay section of FAQ)** [https://www.dataannotation.tech/faqs] (seen 2026-10-09)
- General projects: "Starting at $25-$50+ per hour"
- Multilingual projects: "Starting at $20+ per hour"
- Coding projects: "Starting at $40-$150+ per hour"
- STEM projects: "Starting at $40-$125+ per hour". Requires a master's/PhD, or a bachelor's plus 10+ years of experience.
- Professional projects: "Starting at $40-$125+ per hour". Requires credentials in law, finance, or medicine.
- "All tiers include opportunities for higher rates based on strong performance."
- The same FAQ gives different numbers in other answers. It says general projects "start at $25-$30+ per hour" and "Coding projects that range from $50 to $100 per hour". Cite the Pay-section figures and note that the page itself is inconsistent.
- The FAQ says the company has "paid over $20 million to contractors since 2020." [https://www.dataannotation.tech/faqs] (seen 2026-10-09)

**Payment delivery and timing**
- "We send payments via PayPal. Deposits will be delivered within a few days after you request them." [https://www.dataannotation.tech/faqs] (seen 2026-10-09)

**Assessment / onboarding**
- "Most Starter Assessments take about an hour to complete." Specialized assessments "may take between one to two hours." [https://www.dataannotation.tech/faqs] (seen 2026-10-09)
- "You can only take the Starter Assessment once. There are no retakes or second chances." [https://www.dataannotation.tech/faqs] (seen 2026-10-09)
- Approval notification comes "within a few days." The FAQ adds: "We will contact you if your assessment passes our review and we have work that matches your skill sets." [https://www.dataannotation.tech/faqs] (seen 2026-10-09)
- The baseline requirement is "a bachelor's degree or equivalent real-world experience". All workers must "be fluent in English." [https://www.dataannotation.tech/faqs] (seen 2026-10-09)
- Identity verification is done through Persona with a government photo ID and a selfie. The FAQ says: "We will never ask for money from you for anything." [https://www.dataannotation.tech/faqs] (seen 2026-10-09)
- Whether the assessment is paid is **not stated** on the FAQ.

**Contractor status / tax**
- The FAQ calls workers "contractors". It gives no tax-form policy.

**Country availability**
- No country list is published. The FAQ says only "globally" and "fluent in English."

**Lawsuits / regulatory**
- On 2025-05-20, Clarkson Law Firm filed a class action against **Surge AI** in San Francisco Superior Court. It alleges that "Data Annotator" workers were misclassified as independent contractors and that training and project-familiarization time was unpaid. The firm's page does not name the DataAnnotation platform. [https://clarksonlawfirm.com/clarkson-represents-surge-ai-workers-in-labor-law-class-action] (pub 2025-05; seen 2026-10-09)
- Bloomberg Law headline: "AI Training Firm Surge AI Hit With Worker Misclassification Suit". [https://news.bloomberglaw.com/financial-accounting/ai-training-firm-surge-ai-hit-with-worker-misclassification-suit] (pub 2025-05; seen in search only)
- We found **no** federal docket on CourtListener naming "DataAnnotation." (searched 2026-10-09)

**Complaint patterns (themes only)**
- Trustpilot showed a TrustScore of 3.9 from 1,973 reviews (21% 1-star). The recurring complaint themes are:
  - long stretches with no available work
  - unpaid qualification steps
  - balances held during account review
  - ID re-verification loops
  - unresponsive support

  Praise centres on flexibility and ease of use. [https://www.trustpilot.com/review/dataannotation.tech] (seen 2026-10-09)
- The FAQ itself acknowledges that "Given the number of applications... we are unable to provide direct responses for every applicant." It also says loss of dashboard projects can follow "subpar or problematic contributions." [https://www.dataannotation.tech/faqs] (seen 2026-10-09)

## Outlier (outlier.ai) — Scale AI

**Who runs it**
- "Outlier is a platform operated by Scale AI." The page footer reads "powered by Scale AI · © 2026 Smart Ecosystems." [https://outlier.ai/faq] (seen 2026-10-09)

**What the work is**

**Published pay**
- "Rates vary by expertise, project complexity, and location. You'll always see the tasking rate before starting any project." The FAQ publishes no dollar figure. [https://outlier.ai/faq] (seen 2026-10-09)

**Payment delivery and timing**

- Onboarding "typically takes 30 to 90 minutes" and includes skill screenings and identity verification, followed by project-specific onboarding. [https://outlier.ai/faq] (seen 2026-10-09)

TechCrunch reported Mercor pays "more than $1.5 million per day" to "more than 30,000" experts averaging "over $85 per hour." [https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/27/mercor-quintuples-valuation-to-10b-with-350m-series-c/] (pub 2025-10-27)

**Published pay**
- Mercor publishes no platform-wide rate card; rates are set per job listing. One listing for news analysts and journalists showed "$70-$110 / hr." [https://work.mercor.com/jobs/list_AAABoCWePXSd7eviYb5KxJNg/news-analysts-reporters-and-journalists] (seen in search 2026-10-09; listing not opened)

**Payment delivery and timing (Mercor help docs)** [https://talent.docs.mercor.com/how-to/payments] (seen 2026-10-09)
- Hourly contractors are paid "every Wednesday around 12:00 PM PST" for the prior Saturday–Friday week.
- "Mercor automatically assigns your payment provider based on your region." Stripe Connect is used where supported; Wise is used elsewhere.
- "Stripe requires a 7-day holding period on your first payout."
- "All Mercor contracts are denominated in USD."
- Payouts go only to a bank account "in your own legal name."

- US persons submit a W-9. "If you submitted a W9 and earned over $600 during the year, you will receive a 1099-NEC form by February through Stripe."
- Non-US persons submit a W-8BEN and receive no 1099.

## Mindrift — Toloka

**Who runs it / work**
- "Mindrift is built by" Toloka; the footer reads "© 2026 Toloka AI BV." It describes itself as a "project-based platform connecting experts with AI training opportunities." [https://mindrift.ai/] (seen 2026-10-09)

**Published pay**
- "Entry-level: $15-30 per hour." "Domain experts: $60-100+/hr."
- Listing cards show monthly caps, e.g. "Up to $6,400/month" (STEM) and "Up to $8,000/month" (Consulting).
- Pay is "per completed task" and "the amount is shown before you start."

[https://mindrift.ai/] (seen 2026-10-09)
- Mindrift's blog says quoted hourly figures are estimates based on expected effort. [https://mindrift.ai/blog/how-mindrift-pays] (byline 2026-09-03)

**Payment delivery and timing** [https://mindrift.ai/blog/how-mindrift-pays] (byline 2026-09-03)
- "Contributors receive payment through Payoneer or Tipalti (PayPal)." Availability depends on country.
- Pay periods are the 1st–15th and 16th–end of month, with "payouts processed on the 5th and 20th of each month."
- Only approved tasks are paid. Expired or rejected tasks are not.
- "Contributors are responsible for declaring their earnings and meeting tax obligations in their own country of residence." The blog also says "no employment relationship exists."

**Onboarding**
- The process is: CV, then an assessment, then platform access. The blog says joining and assessments should be free. Whether assessment time is paid is **not stated**. [https://mindrift.ai/] [https://mindrift.ai/blog/how-mindrift-pays]

**Country availability** [https://mindrift.ai/legal/eligibility-and-geographic-restrictions] (last updated 2026-05-27; seen 2026-10-09)
- The page lists sanctioned/restricted jurisdictions, including Russia, Belarus, China (incl. HK and Macau), Iran, Cuba and North Korea.
- It lists separate "not currently supported" locations, including **Kenya, Switzerland, Taiwan, and DR Congo**, because ID-verification or payment rails are unavailable.
- It says VPN use to circumvent restrictions risks "forfeiture of pending Payments."

## Handshake AI (Handshake AI Fellowship)

**Who runs it / work** [https://support.joinhandshake.com/hc/en-us/articles/32264709473303] (seen 2026-10-09)
- The fellowship is run by Handshake, the career platform. Fellows create prompts, evaluate AI responses, and correct content with citations.
- "All Fellows are hired as 1099 independent contractors."
- "Hourly rates are $30–$125, based on education level, expertise, and project."
- "Fellows are contracted and paid directly by Handshake, not the partnering AI lab."
- Fellows set their own schedule, "typically 5 - 20 hrs/week."
- Most roles need a Master's, PhD or Postdoc.
- The page says fellows "are compensated for" the general onboarding (about 2 hours of modules, a quiz, and a practice task).
- External AI tools such as ChatGPT or Claude are "strictly prohibited."

**Onboarding pay — note the conflict** [https://support.joinhandshake.com/hc/en-us/articles/33614619791767-Earning-on-Handshake-AI] (updated about 3 months before 2026-10-09)
- This page says: "Some projects include paid onboarding time, while others do not... If onboarding pay is not explicitly mentioned, time spent onboarding is not paid."
- Not paid: optional meetings, Slack time, and "Time lost due to technical issues."
- With time caps, payment is "limited to the maximum handling time."
- This page was updated more recently than the EDU-partner page above. Treat it as current policy.

**Payment delivery / tax** [https://support.joinhandshake.com/hc/en-us/articles/32263614087575-Handshake-AI-payments-processing] (seen 2026-10-09)
- Tax setup uses a W-8 or W-9. Payouts are configured via **Stripe**.
- "Handshake typically sends payouts to our partner pay platforms by Wednesday afternoon," for the prior Monday–Sunday (PST). It "can often take 48 hours."
- "You are responsible for taxes on your own income."

**Lawsuits / news**
- *Boggs v. Stryder Corp.* (d/b/a Handshake), N.D. Cal. **3:26-cv-07498**, was filed **2026-07-20**. The cause of action is "29:206 Collect Unpaid Wages" (FLSA). Defendants' response deadline was extended to answer a forthcoming First Amended Complaint (order filed 2026-09-08). [https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/73643581/boggs-v-stryder-corp/] (seen 2026-10-09)
- Business Insider (via AOL, pub 2026-03-04) reported that contractors on OpenAI projects said Handshake AI suspended accounts and withheld pay over alleged rule violations, with "no appeal process." One small-claims suit sought $9,600. Another case was resolved with a $6,475 award to the contractor. CEO Garrett Lord said on a podcast that contractors average "over $100 to $125 an hour." [https://www.aol.com/articles/contractors-worked-openai-projects-handshake-050101409.html] (pub 2026-03-04)
