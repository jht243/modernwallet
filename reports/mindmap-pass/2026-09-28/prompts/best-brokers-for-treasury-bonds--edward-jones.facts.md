  may also receive underwriting/management fees on new issues (specific amounts not disclosed on

  minimum investment to open, though individual investments inside it (e.g. annuities) can carry
  their own minimums. Source: edwardjones.com/us-en/investment-services/accounts-overview/brokerage.

  not the commission-based Select account — has a $50,000+ relevance threshold specifically noted
