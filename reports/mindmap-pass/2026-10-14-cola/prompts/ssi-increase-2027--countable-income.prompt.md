## THE CLOSED FACT LIST — every number this section may state
- 2027 SSI federal payment standard: $1,029 a month for an individual (SSA 2027 COLA fact sheet). 2026: $994.
- SSA's method (SSA, "SSI Income" page): Step 1, subtract the income SSA does not count from total gross income; what remains is "countable income". Step 2, subtract countable income from the SSI federal benefit rate; the result is the monthly SSI federal benefit. Generally, the more countable income, the lower the SSI benefit; countable income over the allowable limit means no SSI.
- Income types (SSA): earned income is wages and net earnings from self-employment (among others); unearned income includes Social Security benefits, pensions, unemployment benefits, interest and cash from friends and relatives.
- Not counted (SSA examples): the first $20 of income in SSA's worked examples; the first $65 of earnings and one half of earnings over $65 in a month; SNAP (food stamps); income tax refunds. Since September 30, 2024, the value of food no longer reduces an SSI payment.
- SSA's own worked examples use the 2026 rate of $994: a $300 Social Security benefit → $280 countable → $714 SSI; $317 of wages → minus $20, minus $65 = $232, half = $116 countable → $878 SSI.
- The same two examples at the 2027 rate (arithmetic): $1,029 − $280 = $749 SSI; $1,029 − $116 = $913 SSI.
- A federally administered state supplement, where one exists, is added on top of the federal benefit (SSA example); amounts vary by state — do not state any.
Anything not on this list, you do not know. Do not state any state supplement amount, living-arrangement reduction, or deeming figure.
