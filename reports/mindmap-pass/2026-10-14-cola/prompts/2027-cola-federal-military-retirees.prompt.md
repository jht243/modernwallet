## THE CLOSED FACT LIST — every number/date/limit this page may state

- The Social Security Administration (SSA) announced on Wednesday, October 14, 2026 a 3.6 percent cost-of-living adjustment (COLA) for 2027 (SSA press release).
- The COLA is based on the increase in the Consumer Price Index for Urban Wage Earners and Clerical Workers (CPI-W) from the third quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026; the Social Security Act ties the COLA to the CPI-W as determined by the Bureau of Labor Statistics (SSA press release and fact sheet).
- CSRS (5 U.S.C. 8340): each CSRS annuity is increased each December 1 by the percent change in the price index for the base quarter (the quarter ending September 30), rounded to the nearest one-tenth of a percent. A retiree's FIRST increase is prorated: 1/12 of the percent for each month the annuity was payable before the increase, up to 12 months.
- FERS (5 U.S.C. 8462): effective December 1. If the price-index change is 2% or less, the annuity rises by that change; if it is more than 2% but not more than 3%, it rises 2%; if it is more than 3%, it rises by the change minus 1 percentage point. The increase does not apply to a FERS annuitant under age 62 as of the effective date, except annuitants retired on disability, and the special-provision retirement cases (such as law enforcement officers, firefighters and air traffic controllers) covered by the statute; survivor annuities get the increase.
- 2027 result (FEDweek; Federal News Network): CSRS annuitants get 3.6% and eligible FERS annuitants get 2.6%, paid in January 2027 (effective December 1, 2026). The FERS annuity supplement does not receive a COLA (Federal News Network).
- Military retired pay (10 U.S.C. 1401a): effective December 1 each year, retired pay rises by the percent increase in the Consumer Price Index for the base quarter ending September 30, rounded to the nearest one-tenth of a percent. Members who first became members on or after August 1, 1986 and elected the Career Status Bonus (often called REDUX) get that percent minus 1 percentage point when the percent exceeds 1%.
- 2027 result (Military Times, Oct 14, 2026): military retirees and veterans' benefit recipients are slated for the 3.6% COLA; Military Times gives the example that a military retiree receiving $2,500 in retired pay would see about $90 more per month. For CSB/REDUX retirees 3.6% minus 1 point = 2.6% (arithmetic from the statute).
- VA disability compensation: there is no automatic VA COLA. Congress passes a Veterans' Compensation Cost-of-Living Adjustment Act each year. The 2026 act (S. 4487 in the Senate) would raise VA disability compensation, dependency and indemnity compensation (DIC) and the clothing allowance by the same percentage as the Social Security COLA effective December 1, 2026. As of October 14, 2026 it had been introduced (May 11, 2026) and referred to the Senate Committee on Veterans' Affairs; it had not been signed into law (Congress.gov). VA has published no 2027 rate table. Do NOT state any VA dollar rate.

Anything not on this list, you do not know. Never invent a figure, date, rate, premium, payment date, or URL. Do not state the 2027 Medicare Part B premium, any VA 2027 dollar rate, any state SSI supplement amount, or any 2027 payment calendar date other than December 31, 2026 for SSI. Where a reader's own figure depends on their record, tell them to check their COLA notice or my Social Security. Do not give personal financial advice; explain what changes and what to check.
