## THE CLOSED FACT LIST — every number/date/limit this page may state

- The Social Security Administration (SSA) announced on Wednesday, October 14, 2026 a 3.6 percent cost-of-living adjustment (COLA) for 2027 (SSA press release).
- The COLA is based on the increase in the Consumer Price Index for Urban Wage Earners and Clerical Workers (CPI-W) from the third quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026; the Social Security Act ties the COLA to the CPI-W as determined by the Bureau of Labor Statistics (SSA press release and fact sheet).
- Nearly 72 million Social Security beneficiaries see the 3.6 percent COLA beginning in January 2027. Increased payments to about 7.3 million people receiving Supplemental Security Income (SSI) begin on December 31, 2026. Some people receive both. SSA says it will apply the COLA for 76 million Americans (SSA press release).
- On average, Social Security retirement benefits increase by $75 per month (SSA press release).
- Estimated average monthly benefits payable in January 2027, before vs after the 3.6% COLA (SSA fact sheet): all retired workers $2,095 → $2,170; aged couple, both receiving benefits $3,238 → $3,355; widowed mother and two children $3,932 → $4,074; aged widow(er) alone $1,936 → $2,005; disabled worker, spouse and one or more children $2,970 → $3,077; all disabled workers $1,642 → $1,701.
- Tax rate, 2026 and 2027 unchanged: employee 7.65%, self-employed 15.30%. The 7.65% is Social Security (OASDI) 6.2% on earnings up to the taxable maximum plus Medicare (HI) 1.45% on all earnings; earned income above $200,000 ($250,000 married filing jointly) pays an additional 0.9% Medicare tax, not included in 7.65% (SSA fact sheet).
- Maximum taxable earnings for Social Security (OASDI): $184,500 in 2026 → $192,300 in 2027. Medicare (HI) has no limit (SSA fact sheet).
- Quarter of coverage (earnings needed for one work credit): $1,890 in 2026 → $1,970 in 2027 (SSA fact sheet).
- Retirement earnings test exempt amounts: under full retirement age, $24,480/yr ($2,040/mo) in 2026 → $25,560/yr ($2,130/mo) in 2027; $1 in benefits withheld for every $2 earned above the limit. In the year an individual reaches full retirement age: $65,160/yr ($5,430/mo) in 2026 → $67,920/yr ($5,660/mo) in 2027; applies only to earnings for months before reaching full retirement age; $1 withheld for every $3 above the limit. Beginning the month an individual reaches full retirement age: no limit (SSA fact sheet).
- Substantial Gainful Activity (SGA): non-blind $1,690/mo in 2026 → $1,770/mo in 2027; blind $2,830/mo → $2,950/mo. Trial Work Period (TWP) month threshold: $1,210/mo → $1,270/mo (SSA fact sheet).
- Maximum Social Security benefit for a worker retiring at full retirement age: $4,152/mo in 2026 → $4,188/mo in 2027 (SSA fact sheet).
- SSI federal payment standard: individual $994/mo in 2026 → $1,029/mo in 2027; couple $1,491/mo → $1,544/mo. SSI resource limits unchanged: $2,000 individual, $3,000 couple. SSI student exclusion: monthly limit $2,410 → $2,500; annual limit $9,730 → $10,080 (SSA fact sheet).
- COLA notices: people with a personal my Social Security account can view their COLA notice online starting in late November, up to three weeks earlier than the paper notice. People without an account must create one and enroll in paperless notices by November 18 to see the notice online. Others receive the COLA notice by mail in December. The notice is a simplified one-page notice with exact dates and dollar amounts of the new benefit and any deductions. Account holders can choose text or email alerts (SSA press release).
- Medicare: information about Medicare changes for 2027 will be available at medicare.gov; for Medicare enrollees the 2027 premium amount will be available in the my Social Security Message Center starting in late November (SSA press release). The 2027 Medicare Part B premium has NOT been announced — do not state any 2027 premium.
- Prior COLAs: 2.8% for 2026; 8.7% for 2023 (the 2027 COLA is the largest since 2023).

Anything not on this list, you do not know. Never invent a figure, date, rate, premium, payment date, or URL. Do not state the 2027 Medicare Part B premium, any VA 2027 dollar rate, any state SSI supplement amount, or any 2027 payment calendar date other than December 31, 2026 for SSI. Where a reader's own figure depends on their record, tell them to check their COLA notice or my Social Security. Do not give personal financial advice; explain what changes and what to check.
