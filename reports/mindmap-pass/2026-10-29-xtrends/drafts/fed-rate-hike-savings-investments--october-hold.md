## What the October 2026 Fed Hold Means for Savings, CDs and Mortgages

The [Federal Open Market Committee](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomc.htm) (FOMC) held the federal funds target range at 3-3/4 to 4 percent on October 28, 2026. That hold changes no rate you already have. The [Federal Reserve's statement](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20261028a.htm) reports a 10 to 2 vote. Lisa D. Cook and Beth M. Hammack dissented because they preferred a 1/4 percentage point increase at this meeting. The statement says "Inflation remains elevated" and describes economic activity as expanding at a strong pace, with the unemployment rate little changed.

A certificate of deposit (CD) you already opened keeps its rate for the full term, as the CD section below explains. Each bank sets its own rates on new CDs and savings accounts. Enter a bank's quote in the [CD calculator](/cd-calculator/) or read the [high-yield savings account (HYSA) vs CD comparison](/compare/hysa-vs-cd/) before you pick one. The two dissenting votes are not a decision, and the target range stayed where the September 16 hike put it.

A fixed-rate loan you already have is unaffected by the hold. [Freddie Mac's weekly survey](https://www.freddiemac.com/pmms) put the average 30-year fixed rate at 7.24% on October 29, down from 7.32% a week earlier. Freddie Mac builds that average from rates offered Thursday through Wednesday, so the figure ends on October 28, the day of the decision, and cannot show how lenders reacted to it. Your own quote can differ, so compare it using the [mortgage rates guide](/guides/current-mortgage-rates-guide/).

The statement gives no explicit guidance about the next meeting. If you hold bonds, the sections below explain how a rate change affects their price. Before you lock a CD term, compare current bank quotes. Then read the Federal Reserve statement after the [December 8-9, 2026 meeting](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm), which comes with a Summary of Economic Projections.
