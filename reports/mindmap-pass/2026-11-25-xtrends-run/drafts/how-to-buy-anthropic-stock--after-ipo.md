 According to CNBC, Anthropic raised $81.3 billion in the largest offering of all time, with an oversubscribed order book where about half of institutional investors received no shares. Canaccord Genuity issued a buy rating with a $135 price target, Wedbush assigned an outperform rating with a $120 price target, and Oppenheimer established a $150 price target. These third-party targets represent analyst projections rather than verified price performance.

Future market supply also depends on legal lock-up provisions. The initial Form S-1 described a 180-day lock-up for company insiders and certain stockholders, along with a 365-day lock-up for specified holders, though underwriting representatives may release lock-up securities in whole or part. The final prospectus controls all terms. Investors should read the "Shares Eligible for Future Sale" section in the official [Form 424B4 filing index](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026074944/0001628280-26-074944-index.htm) filed on Nov 19, 2026. SEC filings do not publish specific calendar unlock dates, but blocks of restricted stock can enter the market after expiration, as detailed in the [SEC lock-up guide](https://www.sec.gov/fast-answers/answerslockuphtm.html).
