## Lock-Ups and What Happens After Listing

In its Form S-1 registration statement, Anthropic described a 180-day lock-up for company insiders and certain stockholders, along with a 365-day lock-up for specified holders. Underwriting representatives may release lock-up securities in whole or in part before those periods end.

The final prospectus controls the actual terms. Read the "Shares Eligible for Future Sale" section in the official [Form 424B4 filing index](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026074944/0001628280-26-074944-index.htm) to verify the governing restrictions.

The [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/anthropic-stock-ipo-price.html) article from November 24, 2026, which reported that shares closed below the $83 initial public offering (IPO) price, does not mention lock-ups. According to the [Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) lock-up guide](https://www.sec.gov/fast-answers/answerslockuphtm.html), a lock-up agreement temporarily prevents company insiders and early investors from selling their equity. When lock-up restrictions expire or underwriters authorize an early release, blocks of previously restricted shares can reach the market and increase the circulating supply.
