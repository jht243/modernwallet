Yes, you can buy Anthropic stock through any brokerage account that offers Nasdaq stocks. Retail initial public offering (IPO) allocation is over. To purchase shares on the public market, follow these four steps:

1. Confirm the ticker ANT on the Nasdaq.
2. Select an order type: a market order executes immediately at current market prices, while a limit order executes only at or below a maximum purchase price you choose.
3. Size the position relative to your entire portfolio, keeping [concentrated stock position risk](/guides/concentrated-stock-position-risk/) in mind before allocating capital.
4. Skip private pre-IPO offers, and consult the guidance in the pre-IPO section.
