**Planned outline**

- **section:** Build Your Cancellation Plan
**purpose:** Directly answer PAA: “What to do if a company won't let you cancel a subscription?” Inputs: service/provider, actual biller, signup route, jurisdiction, what happened on cancellation, and continued charges. Output: official route, dated script, proof checklist. Collect no account data.
- **section:** Find the Company That Bills You
**purpose:** Distinguish the service brand from app-store/platform/franchise/monitoring biller and link the matching official cancellation route.
- **section:** What the Federal Rule Change Means
**purpose:** Explain the Eighth Circuit vacatur of the FTC 2024 amendments, procedural ground, surviving ROSCA scope and FTC 2026 ANPRM versus binding law.
- **section:** State and NYC Protections: Selected Examples
**purpose:** Cover CA, CO, Maine, NY and NYC with applicability dates, service scope and exceptions. Say selected examples, not 50-state survey; flag alarm-operator exclusions relevant to ADT.
- **section:** Use These Scripts and Keep Proof
**purpose:** Original call/written scripts; ask for end date, final balance, confirmation and return instructions; save dated proof; no guaranteed outcome.
- **section:** If Charges Continue After You Ask
**purpose:** Escalate to actual biller and relevant regulator; FCC only for in-scope communications, FTC for suspected deceptive practices; complaints do not guarantee resolution.
- **section:** After Cancellation: Check the Next Statement
**purpose:** Tell the reader to keep confirmation and check the next statement for continued billing. Prompt a review of other recurring charges and link to the existing subscription-creep budgeting guide.
- **section:** FAQ plan from observed PAA and Autocomplete
**questionsAndPurpose:** - **question:** Can I cancel a subscription straight away?
**purpose:** Explain that timing depends on provider terms and applicable law; ask for effective date and final billing confirmation.
- **question:** How do I cancel streaming channels?
**purpose:** Route by the actual biller: streamer, Apple, Google Play or Roku; deleting an app is not cancellation.
- **question:** How do I force cancel a subscription?
**purpose:** Do not promise forced cancellation; provide documented request, evidence and relevant complaint steps.
- **question:** How do I find all my streaming subscriptions?
**purpose:** Check platform lists and statements, then identify the biller for each service.
- **question:** Why is it so hard to cancel subscriptions?
**purpose:** Explain distinct billers, signup paths, contract terms and retained options; move reader to the tool’s biller-identification step.
