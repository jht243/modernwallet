## Real competing pair 1 — term vs permanent cash-value life insurance

**Both routes**
- `/compare/whole-life-vs-term-life-insurance/` — `src/data/comparisons.ts:3992`, `targetKeyword: "whole life vs term life insurance"`
- `/compare/term-life-vs-universal-life-insurance/` — `src/data/comparisons.ts:5507`, `targetKeyword: "term life vs universal life insurance"`

**The shared query cluster**
"Term life vs permanent cash-value life insurance" — the buy-term-and-invest-the-difference decision. The two pages are nominally different entity pairs, but they argue the same case with the same evidence: both frame term as cheap/temporary/no-cash-value against a permanent policy costing "5 to 15 times" more for the same death benefit; both resolve to a cost-vs-lifelong-need verdict; and the "buy term, invest the difference" math is a named section on the term-vs-UL page while being the core argument of the term-vs-whole page. Queries such as *term vs permanent life insurance*, *is permanent life insurance worth it*, and *buy term and invest the difference* have no single dedicated owner and are split between them.

**Recommendation**
Signals are close — 3 vs 1 inbound internal links and 9,134 vs 8,755 entry characters, well inside the near-tie band; impressions/clicks were unavailable. **DO NOT consolidate — differentiate the two pages' angles or add internal links instead.** Concretely: keep `whole-life-vs-term-life-insurance` as the cost/permanence decision for a general buyer, and re-scope `term-life-vs-universal-life-insurance` onto what only universal life brings to the comparison (premium flexibility, lapse risk, the annual statement) rather than re-running the generic term-vs-permanent cost argument; the term-vs-UL page already links to `/compare/whole-life-vs-universal-life-insurance/` and should also link up to the term-vs-whole page as the general-case parent.

## Pairs considered and dismissed

**The four life-insurance comparisons do not split one cluster.** They are three distinct pairwise permutations of {term, whole, universal} plus one distinct product, not four takes on one query:

- **`whole-life-vs-universal-life-insurance` vs `term-life-vs-universal-life-insurance`** — dismissed. One is permanent-vs-permanent (guarantees vs flexibility, the illustration trap, the IUL section); the other is temporary-vs-permanent. Different decisions, different SERPs. The term-vs-UL page already links to the whole-vs-UL page, which is the correct relationship.
- **`whole-life-vs-universal-life-insurance` vs `whole-life-vs-term-life-insurance`** — dismissed. Same reason: distinct entity pairs with non-overlapping headings.
- **`final-expense-insurance-vs-whole-life-insurance` vs the other three** — dismissed. It carries an explicit distinct `segment` ("Seniors and near-retirees shopping for a small policy... ages 50 to 85, including many with health conditions"), and its unique content (face-value caps, guaranteed/simplified issue, cost per $1,000, the graded-death-benefit trap) appears nowhere else on the site. Different product, different buyer, different query set. It already links out correctly to both whole-life comparisons and to the sizing guide.

**`how-much-life-insurance-do-i-need` vs `borrowing-against-life-insurance`** — dismissed, not close. The first is a coverage-sizing calculation (10 to 12x income rule, DIME method, worked example); the second is a policy-loan mechanics page (interest accrual, death-benefit offset, lapse and the tax bomb, MEC treatment, withdrawal vs loan vs surrender). No shared query, no shared heading. The sizing guide does contain one "Why Term Usually Beats Whole Life" section that touches `/compare/whole-life-vs-term-life-insurance/`'s cluster, but that is a section inside a differently-targeted page pointing at the dedicated comparison — normal hub/spoke, not cannibalization.

**`roth-conversion-rules` vs `roth-conversion-ladder`** — dismissed. Measured overlap is near zero: the rules guide mentions the five-year rule once and pro-rata nine times; the ladder guide mentions the five-year rule eighteen times and pro-rata zero times. The first owns the mechanics that go wrong in any conversion (pro-rata, bracket filling, no-do-over, the RMD-first rule); the second owns the early-retirement bridge (year-by-year schedule, withdrawal ordering, Rule of 55 / 72(t) / 457(b) alternatives). Cleanly differentiated already.

**The five retirement-account comparisons** — all dismissed. They form the standard pairwise matrix over {traditional 401(k), Roth 401(k), traditional IRA, Roth IRA}, each with its own `targetKeyword` and its own decision:

- **`401k-vs-roth-ira` vs `roth-ira-vs-roth-401k`** — dismissed despite the shared Roth IRA entity. The first is a pre-tax-vs-Roth tax-timing question (its headings: which saves more in taxes, the break-even tax rate); the second is Roth-vs-Roth, where tax timing is identical and the whole decision turns on the income limit, the match, and early-access flexibility. Consolidating would destroy a legitimate distinct head, so no canonical is suggested even though the link gap is wide (22 vs 3).
- **`roth-401k-vs-traditional-401k` vs `roth-ira-vs-traditional-ira`** — dismissed. Same decision framework (pay tax now or later) applied to different vehicles with materially different rules: contribution limits, income phase-outs, RMD treatment, and early-withdrawal access all differ. Adjacent, not competing.
- **`traditional-ira-vs-401k` vs `401k-vs-roth-ira`** — dismissed. Different pairs; the first is a funding-order question between two pre-tax accounts (match first, then IRA for fund choice), the second a tax-treatment question.

**`/taxable-vs-tax-deferred/` calculator vs a tax-free-account guide** — dismissed: **no second route exists.** A slug scan of `src/data/` returns zero `tax-free-*` slugs; the planned `/guides/tax-free-retirement-account/` is net-new in this pass. There is nothing to cannibalize today. Worth flagging forward, not as a cannibalization finding but as a build constraint: the calculator's existing FAQs "What's the difference between tax-deferred and tax-exempt?" and "How do taxable, tax-deferred, and tax-exempt accounts compare side by side?" (`src/data/calculators.ts:1061`, `:1066`) already answer part of the new guide's inventory question. The new guide should link to the calculator for the three-way tax-treatment math rather than restate it.
