 Your Money, Your Goals | Consumer Financial Protection Bureau Skip to main content Your Money, Your Goals Your Money, Your Goals is a set of financial empowerment materials for organizations that help people meet their financial goals by increasing their knowledge, skills, and resources. Whether you’re helping people get a job, find a place to live, or deal with a legal problem, money always comes up. You can use these tools to start the conversation. Printed publications are no longer available from the CFPB. Please use our downloadable PDFs and our "print as handout" buttons to print your own copies. Resources for the people you serve Your Money, Your Goals materials can be used in many different settings. If you’re working with people to help set goals and solve financial problems, you’ll find helpful tools and information here. Toolkit The toolkit has information that helps you have the money conversation with the people you serve. Use the tools to help achieve goals and work through challenges.
