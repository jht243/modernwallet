# Phase 4 findings — moving-checklist (round 2). Apply each FIX in place; keep everything else. The name expansions are now on the fact list.
M3 commonMistakes[0] "Hiring an interstate mover without verifying their USDOT or MC registration number on the official FMCSA database." — acronyms unexpanded. FIX: "Hiring an interstate mover without looking up its U.S. Department of Transportation (USDOT) or Motor Carrier (MC) number with FMCSA."
F1 FAQ 3 "If applicable, inform the Social Security Administration, Veterans Affairs, and USCIS." FIX: "If you receive benefits or have an immigration case, also update the Social Security Administration, Veterans Affairs or U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS), all listed on [USA.gov's change-of-address page](https://www.usa.gov/change-address)."
C1 FAQ 4 "Ask whether the business operates as an actual carrier with its own fleet or acts as a broker arranging service." — unsupported ("own fleet"). FIX: "Ask whether the company is the mover or a broker; a broker books your move with a moving company and has no trucks."
C2 FAQ 6 "Update your voter registration through your local election office or Vote.gov." — fact says state election office. FIX: "Update your voter registration with your state election office through [Vote.gov](https://vote.gov/)."
C3 FAQ 6 "You should also consult a qualified tax professional to determine whether you must file part-year state tax returns in both jurisdictions." — inflated. FIX: "Ask a tax preparer whether you'll file part-year returns in both states."
