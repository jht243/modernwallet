Source A: Thomson Reuters Checkpoint News, "Key 2027 figures calculated by Thomson Reuters Checkpoint based on inflation data now available", published September 11, 2026. These are estimates, not IRS figures. Methodology: based on IRC section 1(f) and 11 months of published BLS CPI data, September 2025 through August 2026; the October 2025 CPI was not published because of the 2025 government shutdown. Checkpoint states the IRS will publish official figures later this year.
PROJECTED 2027 brackets (taxable income):
Single: 10% $0-$12,800; 12% $12,800-$52,025; 22% $52,025-$109,125; 24% $109,125-$208,325; 32% $208,325-$264,550; 35% $264,550-$661,375; 37% over $661,375.
Married filing jointly / surviving spouses: 10% $0-$25,600; 12% $25,600-$104,050; 22% $104,050-$218,250; 24% $218,250-$416,650; 32% $416,650-$529,100; 35% $529,100-$793,650; 37% over $793,650.
Head of household: 10% $0-$18,250; 12% $18,250-$69,650; 22% $69,650-$109,100; 24% $109,100-$208,300; 32% $208,300-$264,550; 35% $264,550-$661,350; 37% over $661,350.
PROJECTED 2027 standard deduction (2026 official in parentheses): joint/surviving spouse $33,200 ($32,200); single $16,600 ($16,100); head of household $24,900 ($24,150); married filing separately $16,600 ($16,100).
OFFICIAL 2026 (IRS Revenue Procedure 2025-32), single: 10% to $12,400; 12% to $50,400; 22% to $105,700; 24% to $201,775; 32% to $256,225; 35% to $640,600; 37% above $640,600. 2026 joint: 10% to $24,800; 12% to $100,800; 22% to $211,400; 24% to $403,550; 32% to $512,450; 35% to $768,700; 37% above $768,700.
As of Oct 2, 2026 the IRS had not published 2027 inflation adjustments (Main Street Dollars check of irs.gov). The IRS has not announced a release date on pages checked. The 2026 adjustments were released in October 2025 (Revenue Procedure 2025-32); do not state a specific day.
Marginal system: each slice of income is taxed at its own rate.
WORKED EXAMPLE (computed; use exactly): single filer, $70,000 gross wages, standard deduction only. Projected 2027: taxable income $53,400; tax = $1,280 (10% of $12,800) + $4,707 (12% of $39,225) + $302.50 (22% of $1,375) = $6,289.50. Official 2026 for the same wages: taxable $53,900, tax $6,570. Difference: $280.50 less tax on projections. Treat as illustration: ignores credits, FICA, state tax, other income, and assumes wages stay the same.
Do not state any projected 2027 figure for capital gains rates, 401(k) or IRA limits, or FICA wage base: they are not on this list.
