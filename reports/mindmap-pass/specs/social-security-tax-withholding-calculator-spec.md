# Spec — tax on Social Security benefits + W-4V withholding (tool-usage pass 2026-10-28, next in line)

- **Decision first (duplicate gate):** a new page `/social-security-tax-withholding-calculator/` vs a "federal tax on your benefits" panel added to /social-security-taxable-benefits-calculator/. Evidence: `tax on social security calculator` 3,600/mo KD 30 and `social security withholding calculator` 720/mo KD 28 (DataForSEO 2026-10-28); Autocomplete for "social security withholding" is mostly payroll FICA (6.2%) intent; PAA on the head asks "How do I calculate my income tax on Social Security benefits?" and "What is the new $6000 tax break for seniors?". If the dominant intent is the tax owed on benefits, prefer the panel on the existing tool (same reader).
- **What it does:** inputs filing status, ages (65+ each), SS benefits, other income, tax-exempt interest, other deductions; outputs taxable benefits (existing Pub 915 engine), federal income tax with 2026 brackets and standard deduction + additional 65+ amount (Rev. Proc. 2025-32) and the $6,000-per-person senior deduction with its 6% phase-out above $75,000 / $150,000 MAGI (P.L. 119-21, 2025–2028), the share of tax attributable to benefits, and the W-4V rate (7, 10, 12 or 22%) that comes closest without going under.
- **Data sources (read at build time):** IRS Pub 915; Rev. Proc. 2025-32; IRS guidance on the senior deduction (OBBBA); Form W-4V instructions.
- **Seed tool / persona:** social-security-taxable-benefits-calculator (30 people) — the married retiree with $22,198 taxable.
- **Dependencies:** `src/lib/social-security-taxable.ts`, `src/lib/tax-bracket.ts`; book picks via `ToolPicks`.
