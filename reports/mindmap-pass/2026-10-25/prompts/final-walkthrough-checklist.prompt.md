## TOOL ITEM LIST (facts: the exact items the tool can show; the page may describe any of them)
Bring with You:
- The purchase contract and the list of included items
- The signed repair agreement [only if: Seller agreed to repairs]
- A phone charger or small lamp to test outlets, and your phone for photos
Agreed Repairs [only if: Seller agreed to repairs]:
- Each agreed repair is finished
- Receipts, permits and any transferable warranties for the repair work
New Construction [only if: New Construction]:
- Every punch-list item is fixed
- Certificate of occupancy or final inspection is issued
- Builder warranty documents and who to call for claims
- Final grading slopes away from the house; landscaping and driveway match the contract
- Utility meters are installed and accounts set up
Room by Room:
- No new damage to walls, floors or doors from the move-out [only if: Seller moved out, or a resale home]
- Included items are still there: appliances, fixtures, window coverings, mounted items
- Seller's belongings, trash and paint cans are gone [only if: Resale, seller moved out]
- Agree in writing when the seller will be out and what condition they'll leave it in [only if: Resale, seller still living there]
- Windows and doors open, close and lock
- No new water stains on ceilings or under sinks
- No signs of pests in cabinets or closets that were hidden by furniture
Systems and Appliances:
- Run every faucet and shower; flush every toilet
- Heat and AC both turn on and blow the right temperature
- Hot water reaches the far bathroom
- Run the dishwasher, washer, dryer, oven, burners and disposal
- Lights, ceiling fans and outlets work
- Garage door opener and its safety reverse work [only if: Not a condo]
- Smoke and CO alarms are present and test
- Pool equipment runs and the water is clear [only if: Pool]
- Well pump runs and you have the latest water test [only if: Private well]
- You have the septic inspection and pumping records [only if: Septic system]
Handover:
- Keys, mailbox key, garage remotes, gate and alarm codes
- Appliance manuals and warranty paperwork
- If something is wrong, tell your agent before you sign: ask for a repair, a credit, money held in escrow, or a delayed closing
