## New findings
N1 (factual). Text (Who Can Sponsor...): "One exception covers a plan only for employees under a collective bargaining agreement, if the SIMPLE IRA plan excludes them." IRS FAQ lists two exceptions to the one-plan requirement: the collective-bargaining plan, and "Your business was part of an acquisition, disposition or similar transaction during the current calendar year or the 2 prior calendar years, and only your separate employees participate in the SIMPLE IRA plan." The closed fact list (F20) repeated the same omission. Fix: "Two exceptions apply: a plan only for employees under a collective bargaining agreement when the SIMPLE IRA plan excludes them, and an employer involved in an acquisition or similar transaction in the current or 2 prior calendar years whose separate employees alone participate in the SIMPLE IRA plan."
N2 (factual, low). Text: "Neither plan suits money you will need before 59½, because both charge the 10% additional tax unless an exception applies." A SIMPLE IRA in its first two years charges 25%, not 10%. Fix: "because both charge an additional tax of at least 10% unless an exception applies."
