Under IRS rules, you are eligible for an employer's SIMPLE IRA if you earned at least $5,000 in any two prior years and expect to earn at least $5,000 in the current year. Once you qualify, employees cannot opt out of participation in the SIMPLE IRA. A business qualifies to sponsor a SIMPLE IRA only if it employed 100 or fewer workers who earned at least $5,000 in the preceding year.
