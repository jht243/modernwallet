## CLOSED FACT LIST — every number the page may state. Anything not on this list, you do not know. Never invent a rate, point, fee, term, advance percentage, statistic or URL; never say what lenders typically charge or require.
- Conventions: loan = purchase price x purchase advance % + rehab budget x rehab advance %. Points = loan x points %, paid at closing. Interest is simple and interest-only: balance x APR / 12 for each month held. With rehab funded at closing the full loan accrues interest from month 1; with rehab drawn evenly, month k carries k/N of the rehab advance. Extension: extra months at the full balance plus a fee of extension fee % x loan balance. Balloon payoff = the full loan balance. Loan to cost (LTC) = loan / (purchase price + rehab budget). Loan to ARV = loan / after-repair value. Simple annualized cost = total financing cost / loan / (years held); it is simple, not an internal rate of return (IRR). Profit before tax = ARV - selling costs - purchase price - rehab - total financing cost - other holding costs.
- Example deal (illustrative inputs, not lender data): purchase $200,000, rehab $50,000, lender advances 90% of the purchase ($180,000) and 100% of the rehab ($50,000), 12% APR, 2 points, 6 months, $2,500 other fees.
  - Loan $230,000. Down payment $20,000. Points $4,600. Other fees $2,500. Cash to close $27,100 (down payment + points + fees).
  - Interest-only payment $2,300 a month. Interest over 6 months $13,800.
  - Total financing cost $20,900 (points $4,600 + fees $2,500 + interest $13,800). Balloon payoff $230,000. LTC 92%. Simple annualized cost 18.17%.
- Loan to ARV with an example after-repair value of $330,000: 69.7%. With 6% selling costs ($19,800) and $1,000 a month of other holding costs ($6,000 over 6 months), profit before tax is $33,300.
- Sensitivity on the example (total financing cost; change vs $20,900): 3 months held $14,000 (-$6,900); 9 months held $27,800 (+$6,900); 1 point $18,600 (-$2,300); 3 points $23,200 (+$2,300); 10% APR $18,600 (-$2,300); 14% APR $23,200 (+$2,300). Each point is 1% of the $230,000 loan, which is $2,300. Each extra month is $2,300 of interest.
- Extension on the example: a 3-month extension at a 1% fee adds $6,900 of interest and a $2,300 fee, $9,200 in all, for a total financing cost of $30,100.
- Rehab drawn evenly instead of funded at closing: interest over 6 months falls from $13,800 to $12,550 and total financing cost to $19,650, because less of the rehab advance is outstanding in the early months.
- At 0% APR the total on the example is $7,100 (points and fees only). With 0 points, points are $0. With 100% of the purchase advanced the down payment is $0. With no rehab the loan is $180,000.
- Not modelled: income tax on the profit, draw inspection fees unless the reader enters them in the fees field, default interest, prepayment minimums, closing costs on the sale beyond the selling-cost percent entered, and whether the deal is a good one.
