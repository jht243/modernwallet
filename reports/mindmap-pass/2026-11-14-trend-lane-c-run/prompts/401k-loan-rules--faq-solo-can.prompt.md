## CLOSED FACT LIST — anything not on this list, you do not know
- The 26 U.S.C. 72(p) limits apply the same way to a loan from an owner-only 401(k).
- A participant loan avoids being a prohibited transaction only if it meets the conditions in 26 U.S.C. 4975(d)(1): reasonably equivalent availability, no larger amounts for highly compensated employees, specific plan provisions, a reasonable rate of interest and adequate security. A plan provision is one of them.
- The plan document and its administrator decide whether a given loan meets the conditions.
- Under 26 U.S.C. 4975(f)(6), an IRA-based plan (a SEP, SIMPLE or traditional IRA) cannot lend to its owner.
