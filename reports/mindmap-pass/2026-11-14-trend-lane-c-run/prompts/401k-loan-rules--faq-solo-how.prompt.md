## CLOSED FACT LIST — anything not on this list, you do not know
- The 26 U.S.C. 72(p) rules for the amount, the 5-year term (except a loan to buy a main home) and level payments at least quarterly apply the same way to a loan from an owner-only 401(k). Page section 1 and the "Repayment Rules" section give them; do not restate figures.
- The plan document must provide for loans, and a participant loan must meet the conditions in 26 U.S.C. 4975(d)(1), which include more than a plan provision (do not list all five here).
- An owner is generally a key employee (a 5-percent owner is one, 26 U.S.C. 416(i)(1)(A)(ii)), so interest on the owner's own plan loan is not deductible under 26 U.S.C. 72(p)(3).
