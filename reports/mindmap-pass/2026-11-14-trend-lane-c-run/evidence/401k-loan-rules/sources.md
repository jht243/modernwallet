- Finding that matters: 4975(f)(6)(B)(iii) limits the owner-employee loan exclusion to IRA-type owners (individual retirement plan participants and employers or associations that establish IRAs under 408(c)), so a solo 401(k) owner is not categorically barred while an IRA-based plan (SEP, SIMPLE, traditional IRA) cannot lend to its owner.
