Yes, you can take a loan from a solo 401(k) if its plan document provides for loans, and the same limits under 26 U.S.C. § 72(p) apply. Avoiding a prohibited transaction under 26 U.S.C. § 4975(d)(1) takes more than a plan provision. The loan must also meet the other conditions in that section, and the plan document and its administrator decide whether a given loan does.

An IRA-based plan, including a SEP, SIMPLE or traditional IRA, cannot lend to its owner under 26 U.S.C. § 4975(f)(6). For the full list of conditions, see the section "Can a Solo 401(k) Lend to Its Owner?" above.
