Yes, you can write your own will without a lawyer in Louisiana. Under Louisiana Civil Code article 1575, an olographic will requires no notary or witnesses if you write, date, and sign the entire document in your own handwriting. If you type the will, Louisiana Civil Code article 1577 requires you to sign at the end and on each separate page before a notary and two competent witnesses. The notary and witnesses must also sign a specific attestation clause. Because most online will services only prompt for witness signatures, a will produced through them is invalid in Louisiana until you separately take it to a notary and two witnesses for execution.
