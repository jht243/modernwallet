UTMA and UGMA accounts are taxed the same way, and the income belongs to the child. In 2025 the first $1,350 of a child's unearned income is tax-free, the next $1,350 is taxed at the child's rate, and anything above $2,700 is taxed at the parents' marginal rate under the kiddie tax. Parents can instead report a child's interest and dividend income on their own return with [Form 8814](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/i8814.pdf) if the child meets the conditions, though with qualified dividends or capital gain distributions the election can cost up to $135 more than a separate return. See [custodial account taxes and the kiddie tax](/guides/custodial-account-taxes-kiddie-tax/) for the full rules.
