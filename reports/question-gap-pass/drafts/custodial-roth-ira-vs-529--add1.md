Yes, a custodial Roth IRA can help pay for college, because Roth contributions can be withdrawn at any time tax- and penalty-free. Earnings withdrawn early for qualified higher education costs (tuition, fees, books, supplies, and equipment) skip the 10% additional tax, though income tax may still be owed on the taxable part, according to [Internal Revenue Service (IRS) Publication 970](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p970).
