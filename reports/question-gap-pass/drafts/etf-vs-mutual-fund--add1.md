Some funds charge sales loads and 12b-1 fees. A front-end load is paid when shares are bought and a back-end load when they are sold, while [12b-1 fees](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/glossary/12b-1-fees) come out of fund assets and are capped by law at 1% of average net assets per year. A fund's front-end and back-end loads are listed in its [prospectus fee table](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins/mutual-fund-and-etf-fees-and-expenses-investor-bulletin), which does not show brokerage commissions or intermediary fees.
