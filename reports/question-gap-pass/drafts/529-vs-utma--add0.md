Yes, UTMA money can pay for college, since the custodian (then the child) can spend it on any purpose, including tuition. Selling holdings to cover a tuition bill can trigger capital gains under the kiddie tax. On the FAFSA, the account counts as a student asset assessed at 20%, compared with up to 5.64% for a parent-owned 529. Once the child reaches majority, control passes to the child, who then decides how the money is spent; the full rules are in [UTMA custodial account explained](/guides/utma-custodial-account-explained/).
