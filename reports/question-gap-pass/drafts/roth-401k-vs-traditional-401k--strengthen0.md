Roth 401(k) balances have no required minimum distributions (RMDs) while the owner is alive. The [IRS RMD FAQ](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/retirement-plans-faqs-regarding-required-minimum-distributions) states that RMD rules do not apply to Roth IRAs or designated Roth accounts, such as a Roth 401(k), during the owner's lifetime.

Traditional 401(k) balances follow different rules. RMDs on those generally begin at age 73. In a plan that holds both kinds of money, RMDs apply to the traditional balance and not to the Roth balance.

Beneficiaries are a separate case. Anyone who inherits a Roth 401(k) or a Roth IRA is still subject to RMD rules.

Rolling a Roth 401(k) into a Roth IRA is optional and is not required to avoid lifetime RMDs. For the full side-by-side, see [Roth IRA vs Roth 401(k)](/compare/roth-ira-vs-roth-401k/).
