No, a UTMA cannot be converted or rolled into a custodial Roth IRA. A Roth accepts only money, not property such as UTMA shares, and its 2025 limit is the smaller of $7,000 or the child's earned income; UTMA interest and dividends do not count as compensation ([IRS Publication 590-A](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p590a)). Selling UTMA investments to raise that cash can create taxable gains under the [kiddie tax](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc553), and contributions stay capped at the child's earned income. See [custodial Roth IRA vs brokerage account](/compare/custodial-roth-ira-vs-brokerage-account/) for the full trade-off, and project the growth with the [Roth IRA calculator](/investing/roth-ira-calculator/).
