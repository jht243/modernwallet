The 4% rule for VA loans caps seller concessions at 4% of the home's reasonable value, which is the figure shown on the VA Notice of Value. The seller can pay closing costs, because VA does not limit closing-cost credits, and those credits do not count toward the 4% cap. Concessions that do count toward the cap include the seller paying the funding fee, paying off the buyer's debts, or prepaying hazard insurance. On a VA purchase loan, only the funding fee can be financed, so any other fee the seller does not cover is paid at closing, as the [VA funding fee and closing costs page](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/funding-fee-and-closing-costs/) explains.
