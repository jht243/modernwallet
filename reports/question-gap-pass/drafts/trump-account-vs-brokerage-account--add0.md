No, a Trump Account is not a brokerage account, and the [IRS describes it](https://www.irs.gov/trumpaccounts) as a new type of individual retirement account (IRA) for a child. Under its rules, the money is locked until January 1 of the year the child turns 18, and it can only hold an S&P 500 or U.S.-equity index fund. After 18 it works like a traditional IRA, while a brokerage account is fully liquid and can hold any investment.
