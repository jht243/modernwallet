The VA funding fee on a purchase loan is 1.25% to 3.3% of the loan amount. Veterans who receive VA compensation for a service-connected disability are exempt, which on a $350,000 loan means not paying $4,375 to $11,550. Purple Heart recipients on active duty and surviving spouses receiving DIC are also exempt. On a purchase loan, the [VA funding fee schedule](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/funding-fee-and-closing-costs/) sets the rate by down payment: 2.15% on first use (3.3% after first use) with less than 5% down, 1.5% with 5% to under 10% down, and 1.25% with 10% or more down. Each rate is a percent of the loan amount, so it is charged on what you borrow after the down payment rather than on the home's purchase price.
