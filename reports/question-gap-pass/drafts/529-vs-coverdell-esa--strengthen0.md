The Coverdell Education Savings Account (ESA) contribution limit is $2,000 per beneficiary for 2025, and that cap covers all contributions from all sources across every Coverdell ESA held for that child. The $2,000 is gradually reduced when the contributor's modified adjusted gross income (MAGI) falls between $95,000 and $110,000 for single filers, or between $190,000 and $220,000 for joint filers. A contributor with MAGI of $110,000 (single) or $220,000 (joint) or more can't contribute at all. Contributions can only go to a beneficiary under age 18 or a special needs beneficiary, according to [IRS Publication 970](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p970).
