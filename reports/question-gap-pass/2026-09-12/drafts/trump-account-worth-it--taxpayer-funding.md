Yes, the federal seed contribution is funded through general federal revenue and appropriations, like any other government spending program or tax credit. Created under the 2025 tax law, the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, the deposit is structured as a refundable federal tax credit paid directly by the U.S. Treasury. It is not a new or separate tax charged to individuals for this program. Official policy and budget details are documented in the Congressional Research Service overview and U.S. Treasury releases cited in the sources section.
