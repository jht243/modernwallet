Yes, you can lose money in both accounts if the market drops. Custodians typically invest these balances in securities such as stocks, mutual funds, and exchange-traded funds instead of holding plain cash. Neither account carries Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC) or National Credit Union Administration (NCUA) deposit insurance, which covers only cash held at a bank or credit union. Securities Investor Protection Corporation (SIPC) coverage protects against a brokerage firm failing, but it does not protect against ordinary market losses.
