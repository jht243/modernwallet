CLOSED FACT LIST:
- New Mexico allows a simplified small-estate affidavit process (skipping full probate court
  proceedings) when the estate that would be subject to probate is valued under $50,000 and at
  least 30 days have passed since the death — this is under NMSA 1978, Section 45-3-1201.
- Certain assets skip probate entirely regardless of the estate's total size and don't count
  toward that $50,000 figure: life insurance with a named beneficiary, retirement accounts with
  a named beneficiary, and assets already held in a living trust.
- For estates above that threshold, New Mexico (as a Uniform Probate Code state) offers an
  informal/unsupervised probate option that moves faster than formal probate, available when
  there is a valid, uncontested will (or no dispute over its absence) and no contested property
  claims.
