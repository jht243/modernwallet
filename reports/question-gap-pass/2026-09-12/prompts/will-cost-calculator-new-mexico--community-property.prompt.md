CLOSED FACT LIST:
- New Mexico is one of nine U.S. community-property states (the others are Arizona, California,
  Idaho, Louisiana, Nevada, Texas, Washington, and Wisconsin) — this is an established fact
  already used elsewhere on this site.
- In a community-property state, a person's will can generally only give away their own
  separate property plus their half of the couple's community property — not their spouse's
  half of the community property.
- Couples with meaningful community property (and no separate marital agreement) should make
  sure the will's language actually matches how their accounts and property are titled, since a
  mismatch there is a common source of confusion or disputes after death.
