CLOSED FACT LIST:
- A 529 plan's age-based portfolio option automatically shifts money from stocks toward
  conservative holdings (bonds, cash-like investments) as the child gets closer to college age —
  this reduces, but doesn't eliminate, market risk right before the money is needed.
- A UTMA account has no automatic "glide path" like that — it stays invested however the
  custodian originally chose, which can mean it's still heavily in stocks right up until the
  child takes control of the account.
- Neither account type is FDIC or NCUA insured, and both can lose value if the market drops —
  there's no guarantee on either side.
