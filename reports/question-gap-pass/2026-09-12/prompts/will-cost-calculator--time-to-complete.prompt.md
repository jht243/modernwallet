CLOSED FACT LIST:
- Online will-making services (questionnaire-based tools like the ones already named elsewhere
  on this page) typically produce a completed document in well under an hour.
- Attorney-drafted wills typically take a few business days for the attorney to prepare a first
  draft after the initial consultation, and about 2 to 4 weeks total from first consultation
  through revisions and final signing for a moderate-complexity will; more complex situations
  (blended families, business interests, trusts) can take longer.
- Either way, a will is not legally effective until it is properly executed — signed with the
  required witnesses present (execution requirements vary by state and are covered elsewhere
  on this page/site).
