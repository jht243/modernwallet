CLOSED FACT LIST:
- A custodial Roth IRA and a custodial (UTMA/UGMA) brokerage account are both typically invested
  in securities (stocks, mutual funds, ETFs) chosen by the custodian — not held as plain cash.
- Neither carries FDIC or NCUA deposit insurance (that only covers bank/credit-union cash
  deposits, not invested securities).
- So yes — the balance in either account can go down if the investments lose value, exactly as
  it can in any ordinary brokerage account. SIPC coverage, where it applies, protects against
  the brokerage firm itself failing, not against normal market losses.
