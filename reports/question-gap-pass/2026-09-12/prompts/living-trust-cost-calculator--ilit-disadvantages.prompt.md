CLOSED FACT LIST:
- Putting life insurance into an ILIT (Irrevocable Life Insurance Trust) means giving up
  ownership and control of the policy — an ILIT is irrevocable, unlike a revocable living trust.
- Attorney-drafted ILIT setup costs $2,500 to $4,000 (already stated elsewhere on this page).
- If you transfer an EXISTING life insurance policy into an ILIT and die within 3 years of the
  transfer, the proceeds are still pulled back into your taxable estate under IRC section
  2035(a) — the 3-year rule. A new policy issued directly in the ILIT's name from the start
  avoids this.
- Ongoing administration requires the trustee to send "Crummey notices" to the trust's
  beneficiaries each time a premium payment is made, so the payment qualifies as a tax-free
  gift under the annual gift-tax exclusion — a compliance step people who DIY this often miss.
