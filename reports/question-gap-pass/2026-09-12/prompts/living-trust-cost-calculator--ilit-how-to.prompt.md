CLOSED FACT LIST:
- The cleanest way is to have a NEW life insurance policy issued directly in the name of an
  ILIT (Irrevocable Life Insurance Trust) from the start, rather than transferring an existing
  policy — a transferred existing policy is subject to the IRC section 2035(a) 3-year lookback
  rule (die within 3 years of the transfer and the proceeds are still counted in your taxable
  estate).
- The trustee, not you, must pay the ongoing premiums and send "Crummey notices" to
  beneficiaries for each payment so it qualifies as a tax-free gift.
- Attorney-drafted ILIT setup runs $2,500 to $4,000 (already stated elsewhere on this page).
- DIY/online estate-planning software is not appropriate for setting up an ILIT — this requires
  an attorney (already stated elsewhere on this page).
