CLOSED FACT LIST:
- Both a custodial Roth IRA and a UTMA account are typically invested in securities (stocks,
  mutual funds, ETFs) chosen by the custodian — they are not held as plain cash by default.
- Neither account type carries FDIC or NCUA deposit insurance (that insurance only covers cash
  deposits at a bank or credit union, not invested securities).
- So yes, the account balance can go down if the underlying investments lose value, the same as
  in any other investment/brokerage account. Where SIPC coverage applies, it protects against
  the brokerage firm failing — it does not protect against ordinary market losses.
