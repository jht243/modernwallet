CLOSED FACT LIST (verified 2026-09-12 against current Iowa law — use ONLY these figures, do not
use any other Iowa small-estate dollar figure you may have seen elsewhere):
- Iowa allows a small-estate affidavit process (skipping full probate) when the decedent's
  personal property subject to probate is valued at $100,000 or less — this threshold was
  raised from $50,000 effective July 1, 2026 under Iowa Code section 633.356 (amended by House
  File 2660, signed by Governor Kim Reynolds). At least 40 days must have passed since the
  death, and no probate proceeding can already be pending.
- This small-estate procedure covers personal property ONLY — it does not apply if the
  decedent had an interest in real estate that would need to go through probate.
- For estates that don't qualify (real estate involved, or personal property over $100,000),
  Iowa is one of only 9 states that sets a statutory attorney/executor fee schedule by law
  (Iowa Code section 633.197/198) rather than using a "reasonable fee" model — this is already
  covered on the site's probate fee calculator.
