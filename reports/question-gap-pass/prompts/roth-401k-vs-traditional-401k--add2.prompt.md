- A qualified distribution is generally one made after a 5-taxable-year period of participation and either on/after age 59½, after death, or due to disability; the 5-year period ends when five consecutive taxable years have passed, starting the first day of the taxable year of the first designated Roth contribution (source: https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/retirement-plans-faqs-on-designated-roth-accounts)
