FAQ answer to rewrite (current):
No. Both are taxed the same way under the kiddie tax. In 2025 the first $1,350 of a child's unearned income is tax-free, the next $1,350 is taxed at the child's rate, and unearned income above $2,700 is taxed at the parents' marginal rate.

- Parents can elect to report a child's income on their own return on Form 8814 if the child was under 19 (under 24 if full-time student), had only interest and dividends (incl. capital gain distributions), had gross income under $13,500 (2025), and is required to file; the child then does not file a return (source: https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/i8814.pdf (2025 Instructions for Form 8814, Purpose of Form))

- A separate Form 8814 is needed per child; election made by attaching to the parent's timely filed return (source: https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/i8814.pdf (How to make the election))
