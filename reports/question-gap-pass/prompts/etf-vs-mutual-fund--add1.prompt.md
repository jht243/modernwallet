CLOSED FACT LIST (anything not on this list, you do not know. Never invent a price, limit, benchmark, or URL; if unsure leave it out):
- A front-end load is paid at purchase and a back-end load at sale; both are shareholder fees disclosed in the prospectus fee table. (source: https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins/mutual-fund-and-etf-fees-and-expenses-investor-bulletin)
- 12b-1 fees pay for distribution and sometimes shareholder services, are paid from fund assets, and by law cannot exceed 1 percent of average net assets per year. (source: https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/glossary/12b-1-fees)
- The prospectus fee table does not show brokerage commissions or intermediary fees. (source: investor.gov bulletin above)
