CLOSED FACT LIST (anything not on this list, you do not know. Never invent a price, limit, benchmark, or URL; if unsure leave it out):
- VA does not limit credits for closing costs but limits seller concessions to no more than 4% of the home's reasonable value (shown on the VA Notice of Value). (source: https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/funding-fee-and-closing-costs/)
- Concessions include credits for the funding fee, debt payoff, or prepaying hazard insurance. (source: same)
- On a purchase loan only the funding fee can be financed; all other fees must be paid at closing. (source: same)
