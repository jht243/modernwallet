The W-9 looks the same for a sole proprietor and a disregarded single-member LLC: use the owner's name and check the "Individual/sole proprietor" box. Put your legal name on line 1; if you have an LLC, put its name on line 2. For the taxpayer identification number, use your Social Security number or an EIN issued to you as the owner, rather than an EIN issued to the LLC. You do not need an EIN just to complete the W-9, but using your own EIN lets you give clients that number instead of your Social Security number.
