CLOSED FACT LIST (anything not here, you do not know):
- The Robinhood 24 Hour Market runs continuously from Sunday at 8:00 p.m. ET until Friday at 8:00 p.m. ET; there is no overnight session on the weekend.
- 24 hour trading is available on a curated list of stocks and ETFs, not every security.
- Overnight orders must be whole-share limit orders; fractional shares do not trade overnight.
- Options cannot be traded in the 24 Hour Market.
- Overnight orders are routed to 24H market makers and alternative trading systems.
- Robinhood can suspend overnight market access without prior notice.
- Overnight liquidity is lower and bid-ask spreads wider, so a limit order away from the market price may not fill.
