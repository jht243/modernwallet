An order is usually blocked because it breaks one of the 24 Hour Market's specific session rules. Check the clock first: overnight trading runs continuously only from Sunday at 8:00 p.m. ET to Friday at 8:00 p.m. ET, with no weekend session. Next, verify the asset and order type against [Robinhood's 24 Hour Market guidelines](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/24hour-market/). The overnight window supports whole-share limit orders on a curated list of stocks and ETFs, so options, fractional shares, and unlisted tickers will not route. Finally, Robinhood can suspend overnight market access without notice, and lower liquidity paired with wider spreads means limit orders priced away from the prevailing market often sit unfilled.
