Park the money for 30 to 90 days in a high-yield savings account first, then clear high-interest debt, fund an emergency reserve, and invest what remains. In a hypothetical example with $12,000 of credit card debt and $2,500 in monthly expenses, paying off the cards leaves $88,000. Sizing a three-to-six-month reserve at that spending level requires $7,500 to $15,000, as explained in our [emergency fund guide](/guides/how-much-emergency-fund/). That leaves between $73,000 and $80,500, which you can split between living expenses to free up workplace retirement contributions and taxable index funds following our walkthrough on [investing $100,000](/guides/how-to-invest-100k-to-1-million/).
