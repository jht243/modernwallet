There is no single best way, because monthly income depends on the annual yield you target divided by 12. At 3%, $100,000 generates $250 a month; at 4%, it pays about $333; and at 5%, it reaches about $417. Prioritizing current payouts through [monthly dividend ETFs](/roundup/best-monthly-dividend-etfs/) or fixed income trades away long-term growth, and pulling that cash out halts the compounding required to reach larger wealth targets. Principal in stock and bond funds also fluctuates with the market, and yields change over time, so verify current rates or test potential cash flows with our [dividend calculator](/investing/dividend-calculator/) before committing the funds.
