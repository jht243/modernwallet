## FAQ question (use verbatim)
"What was the standard mileage rate for 2025?"

## CLOSED FACT LIST — state ONLY these facts. Anything else, you do not know.
1. The 2025 IRS business standard mileage rate was 70 cents per mile for the entire year (a single rate, unlike 2026's mid-year split). Source: IRS — Standard mileage rates (https://www.irs.gov/tax-professionals/standard-mileage-rates), which also already appears in this page's own Sources list — do not add a duplicate citation link if the page already links it; if you do cite it, use exactly this URL.
2. Contrast with 2026 explicitly: 2026 has two rates (72.5 cents January 1 - June 30, 76 cents July 1 - December 31), while 2025 was a flat 70 cents the whole year — so a filer doing a late or amended 2025 return uses the single 70-cent figure, not either of the 2026 figures.
3. Do not state the 2025 medical/moving or charitable mileage rates — not verified for this run; do not guess them.
