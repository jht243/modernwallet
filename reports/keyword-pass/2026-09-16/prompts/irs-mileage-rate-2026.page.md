# IRS Mileage Rate 2026: Two Rates, One Year

The 2026 business standard mileage rate is 72.5 cents per mile for January 1 through June 30, and 76 cents per mile for July 1 through December 31. The IRS raised it mid-year, so 2026 has two business rates rather than one.

## How it works

The standard mileage rate is the IRS's per-mile stand-in for what driving actually costs, covering fuel, insurance, maintenance, tyres, registration, and depreciation in one figure. For 2026 there are three rates, each with a first-half and second-half figure except the charitable rate: business driving is 72.5 cents a mile from January 1 through June 30, and 76 cents from July 1 through December 31. Medical and qualified military moving mileage is 20.5 cents in the first half and 23.5 cents in the second. The charitable rate stays at 14 cents all year.
