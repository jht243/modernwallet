CLOSED FACT LIST (use only these facts; anything not here, you do not know):
- There is no minimum dollar amount required to open a Trump Account or to make a contribution.
  The law and IRS guidance set only a maximum, not a minimum.
- The maximum is $5,000 per year, combined, from family and friends (that cap is indexed to
  inflation starting after 2027). An employer may add up to $2,500 a year, but that counts
  inside the $5,000 cap, not on top of it. The one-time $1,000 federal seed (for eligible
  U.S.-citizen children born 2025-2028) does not count against the $5,000 cap.
- No contributions were allowed before July 4, 2026.
- Whatever amount is contributed must be invested in the required low-cost S&P 500 / U.S.-equity
  index fund.
- The account is opened through an approved trustee: the Bank of New York Mellon (BNY) is the
  Treasury's financial agent, Robinhood is the initial trustee, and Fidelity, Schwab, Vanguard,
  and Bank of America are among the other approved trustees. A trustee may apply its own
  account-level minimum or fee schedule, so the reader should check that trustee's own terms
  before funding.
- This page's own "Step 4: Fund the Trump Account" section already tells readers: "You do not
  have to max it out to start. Even small, steady contributions add up over years of market
  growth."
