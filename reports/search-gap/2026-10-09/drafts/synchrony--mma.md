## Does Synchrony Bank Offer a Money Market Account?

Yes, [Synchrony Bank](https://www.synchrony.com/banking/products/money-market-account) offers a Money Market Account with no minimum deposit, no minimum balance requirement, and no monthly fees. You can write checks, withdraw money online, by phone, or via ATM, and request an optional ATM card. Synchrony does not charge an ATM fee itself and refunds domestic ATM fees charged by other institutions up to $5, with unlimited ATM fee refunds per statement cycle for depositors who hold Diamond status in its Perks Rewards Program. Synchrony also charges no fee for standard transfers to and from the account, though outgoing wire transfers cost $25. Deposits carry FDIC coverage up to $250,000 per depositor, per insured bank, for each ownership category.

Synchrony is not one of the seven picks ranked on this page, and its yield is variable and can change at any time without notice. Because that yield can move, check the [Synchrony Bank money market page](https://www.synchrony.com/banking/products/money-market-account) to compare its current annual percentage yield (APY) against the rates listed above before applying.
