# Check vendor coverage before repeating vendor research

Owner update, 2026-11-08. Follow `_vendor-research.md` before researching vendors for Mindmap Pass or its callers. Check the existing target-page coverage FIRST. This supersedes older blanket instructions to reopen every vendor homepage/pricing page before deciding whether it is already covered. Keep the original unfiltered intake, all candidate IDs, canonical jobs, findings and counters.

Vendor BUILD/chart/content-review roles include `.claude/commands/_vendor-research.md` as an additional instruction in their EXISTING phase preflight. Read it through the normal complete bounded delivery/application process, with permitted same-context reuse on later checks. MAIN remains an operational dispatcher; it passes the queue and complete intake to those roles rather than doing their content research itself.

For a vendor-addition task, use current full canonical own-page captures plus exact vendor identity. A vendor already present in the actual first-column row of the ORIGINAL target can go straight to the existing independent coverage review, without another company/pricing crawl. Saved aliases must come from real independently reviewed positive identity evidence; no fuzzy name guesses, footer mentions, discarded cuts or invented redirects. Stale identity gets an identity-only refresh. New products, versions, announcements, explicit refreshes, conflicting information or new reader intent require the relevant new research; a company mention never covers a new claim.

Layer3's native Vertical prepare now supplies a source-bound `vendorResearchPlan` before BUILD. Pass it with the COMPLETE original intake. BUILD uses its research queue, retains each covered vendor for the normal independent chart review, and does not repeat deep research for `none-covered` unless a genuine new issue is discovered. Record that issue and its evidence under the original candidate ID. The plan is not a cut, no-op, editorial PASS or publication authority. All-covered still needs the normal independent chart/accounting process. Unknown or unproven coverage falls back to normal research automatically.

Other fleet callers use the same coverage-first principle and generic helper with their actual canonical site, original target/intent and existing complete rendered evidence; no Layer3 local activation is copied. Preserve full SEO/deduplication, source/testing honesty, exclusions, factual profiles, professional review, 1/2/3 review budgets, exact rendered/live checks, native release exceptions and existing authorizations. Never present cached prices/capabilities/medical claims as newly verified. No extra human gate or shared opportunity queue is created.
